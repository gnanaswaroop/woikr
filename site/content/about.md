<p style="text-align: justify;">We are a technology blog providing news and reviews from an Indian perspective. We love talking about Apple, Android and Internet in general.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">We love exploring and reviewing new and interesting iPhone/iPad/Mac apps. If you have something interesting drop us an email at reviews [at] woikr.com. We also review gadgets in Delhi, Bangalore or Hyderabad. Contact us for further information.</p>
<h4 style="text-align: justify;">Inspiration</h4>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Excerpt from <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_The_Big_Bang_Theory_Episodes#The_Cooper-Hofstadter_Polarization" target="_blank"><strong>The Cooper-Hofstadter Polarization</strong> </a> (<a href="http://www.cbs.com/primetime/big_bang_theory/video/video.php?cid=501654023&amp;pid=ro_Rzkyu7uJCbt06oOzIxeMeS2OntlsD&amp;play=true&amp;cc=2" target="_blank">Season 1 Episode 9</a>,&nbsp;<a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Big_Bang_Theory" target="_blank">The Big Bang Theory</a>):</p>
<blockquote><p><strong>Leonard (to Penny):</strong> Hang on, hang on. Do you not realize what we just did?<br>
<strong>Penny:</strong> Yeah, you turned your stereo down with your laptop.<br>
<strong>Leonard:</strong> No, we turned our stereo down, by sending a signal around the world via the Internet.<br>
<strong>Penny:</strong> Oh. You know you can get one of those universal remotes from Radioshack? They’re really cheap.<br>
<strong>Leonard:</strong> No, you don’t get it. Howard, Enable public access.<br>
<strong>Howard:</strong> Public access enabled.<br>
<strong>Penny (after a few seconds of nothing happens):</strong> Boy, that’s terrific. I’ll see ya.<br>
<strong>Leonard:</strong> No hang on, hang on! (The lights in the apartment start blinking) See?<br>
<strong>Penny:</strong> No.<br>
<strong>Leonard:</strong> Someone in Sichuan province, China, is using his computer to turn our lights on and off.<br>
<strong>Penny (kinda frustrated):</strong> Oh, that’s handy. Here’s a question, Why?<br>
<strong>Leonard, Sheldon, Howard and Koothrappali (all together):</strong> <em>Because we can!</em></p></blockquote>
<p style="text-align: justify;">With a plethora of technology blogs around, does anybody actually need another one? Maybe not.&nbsp;So why woikr? Well, because we can 😉</p>
