<p>As part of advertising for their upcoming animation movie "Rio", 20th Century Fox have partnered with Rovio Mobile and are going to launch Angry Birds Rio in March this year. The trailer ahead is pretty interesting but I'm sure the game will be pretty much the same with a Rio De Janeiro theme.</p>
