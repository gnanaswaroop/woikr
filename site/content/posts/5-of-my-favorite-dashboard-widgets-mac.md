<p>Now that your <a href="/mac/kickstart-your-mac-dashboard">Dashboard kicks ass</a>, you need to fill it up with widgets. Widgets that will change your life forever (or at least keep you happy for an hour).</p>
<p>Here are 5 of my favorite, most used widgets:</p>

<ul>
	<li><strong>Stickies<br>
</strong><img src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/09/screen-capture.jpg" alt="Stickies Dashboard Widget" width="225" height="206" /><br>
Instead of the Stickies app, I prefer using the widget. It's easily  accessible and never intrudes your workspace.You can post as many as you  want and not even worry about saving them. The widget comes  pre-installed with your Mac.
</li>
<br />
	<li><strong><a href="http://www.palple.net/widgets/currencyconverter/index.html" target="_blank">Currency Converter</a></strong>
<img src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/09/currency-converter.jpg" alt="Currency Converter widget" width="220" height="150" /><br>
The best currency converter I've used on any platform. Comes with lots of options and 6 themes to choose from.</li>
<br />
	<li><strong><a href="http://www.cyanworks.net/dboardTunesTEXT.html" target="_blank">TunesTEXT</a></strong>
<img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-4508" title="TunesText" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/09/TunesText.jpg" alt="TunesTEXT Dashboard Widget" width="408" height="280" /><br>
There are a lot of lyrics displaying apps and widgets but I like this  the best because it updates the lyrics inside to the song playing on  iTunes so it can be seen offline and also on your iPod and iPhone. The  app also has a lyrics editor and a custom search app in case it fails to  find proper lyrics automatically.</li>
<br />
	<li><a href="http://www.stephenthorson.com/imageshackle/" target="_blank"><strong>Image Shackle</strong></a>
<img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-4506" title="image_shackle" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/09/image_shackle.jpg" alt="Image Shackle Dashboard Widget" width="255" height="142" /><br>
The fastest way to resize an Image. Just select an image drag it over  the widget, enter the target width/height and press Shackle. It'll  calculate the other dimension as per the proportion, resize the image,  convert the image to JPG if you want and store it right next to the  original image. It's an excellent tool if you are a blogger and  otherwise too.</li>
<br />
	<li><strong><a href="http://www.tarcolesfilmarts.com/software/" target="_blank">Screenshot Plus</a></strong>
<img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-4507" title="screenshot_plus" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/09/screenshot_plus.jpg" alt="Screenshot Plus Dashboard Widget" width="311" height="69" /><br>
Every mac comes with a really good screenshot utility inbuilt. But it  can be used only through a complex set of shortcuts. Unless you use them  regularly they are hard remember. Screenshot Plus aims to provide a  solution. It's a simple dashboard app with 5 options namely, Grab  screen, Grab screen with timer, Grab a window, Grab a widget and Grab a  custom defined screen area. After taking the screenshot it shows a  zoomable preview and you can choose to save or reject it. All the  screenshots for this article were captured using the tool.</li>
</ul>
<p>For more widgets you can browse the Top 50 list of <a href="http://www.apple.com/downloads/dashboard/" target="_blank">Apple's Dashboard Widgets page</a>. Don't forget to post your suggestions in comments :)</p>
