<p>Ahead are some pictures from AT&amp;T Bell Labs in the 1960s. In the very next decade from when the pictures were taken, some of these dorky looking guys created the C Programming Language so that they could write the UNIX Operating System. Later they created C++ to enhance C by adding Object Oriented features.</p>
<p>If you never studied Computer Science in College and don't get who they were or simply don't care, they were the inventors of photo-voltaic cell, the <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transistor" target="_blank">transistor</a> (no not radio), <a title="Electron diffraction" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electron_diffraction">electron diffraction</a> that helped lay the foundation for <a title="Solid-state electronics" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solid-state_electronics">solid-state electronics</a>, <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_cryptography" target="_blank">modern cryptography</a>, <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_cell" target="_blank">solar cell</a>, <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MOSFET" target="_blank">MOSFET</a>s and LSI chips, most of the communication standards from <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_division_multiple_access" target="_blank">GSM</a> to <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CDMA" target="_blank">CDMA</a> to <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CDMA" target="_blank">WLAN</a>. Yeah, they did invent pretty much everything.</p>

<p>The pictures somehow remind me strongly of <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dharma_Initiative" target="_blank">The Dharma Initiative</a>. Which makes me wonder whether these guys also discovered Time Travel and The Island.</p>
