<p>There are very few companies other than Apple which make beautiful products which are just as functional. <a href="http://www.in2technologies.com/engageunity/unity/">Unity by Engage</a> is one such product. It combines a powerful Home Theater and a beautiful media center (TV stand really) in a stylish package.</p>
<p>Unity is a Home Theater built in the shape of a TV stand. The package features:</p>
<ul>
<li>6 speakers</li>
<li>Blu Ray Player</li>
<li>iPhone &amp; iPod Dock</li>
<li>HDMI connectivity</li>
<li>Capability to support any HDTV unto 60 inches in size</li>
</ul><p>Here is how the components are integrated:</p>
<p><img title="Unity_Spec.png" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/Unity_Spec.png" alt="Unity by engage" width="540" height="357" /></p>
<p>Looks like an awesome combination of form and function. I just hope they have taken into consideration the vibrations which will be produced from those two 10'' woofers. Although I suppose they will be suppressed by the weight of the TV. The speaker placement might not be optimum, but it will be a lot better than a sound bar.</p>
<p>They go on sale this year at a price tag of $999 in the US. Style is never cheap, huh.</p>
