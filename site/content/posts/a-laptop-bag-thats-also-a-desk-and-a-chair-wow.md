<p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/03.jpg"></a>This is something I couldn't have thought of in my craziest dreams. Openaire designed by <a href="http://nicktrincia.com/artwork/755560_Openaire_by_NICK_BEAU.html" target="_blank">Nick and Beau Trincia</a> is the coolest and the most utilitarian laptop case I've ever seen. In their words:</p>

<blockquote>Openaire is a hybrid of a laptop carrying case, work surface, and chair. The system consists of a semi-rigid protective sleeve designed as an ergonomic laptop work surface and a protective shell that also functions as a comfortable seat. Together, they offer the portability and comfort to support work anywhere.</blockquote>
