<p>All these touchscreen devices have one thing in common - the lack of <a href="http://www.google.com/search?q=define%3Atactile">tactile</a> gaming. And the iPad is no exception. So a company called <a href="http://tenonedesign.com/">Ten One Design</a> which makes some cool accessories for (mostly) apple products have come up with this uber cool looking joystick for iPad - <a href="http://tenonedesign.com/fling.php">Fling</a>.</p>
<p>The joystick sticks to your iPad on the bezel using the suction cups. This way, it can be placed over the onscreen joystick in your favorite game. When you move the joystick, its conductive material touches the corresponding onscreen control to control the character taking you into total retro gaming nirvana.</p><p><img title="NewImage.jpg" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/NewImage36.jpg" alt="NewImage.jpg" width="190" height="85" /></p>
<p>The joystick is available for <a href="http://tenonedesign.com/checkout.php?product=Fling">pre order for $24.95 here</a> and will ship around January 28. It is available in 3 colors - Ice (transparent), ultraviolet &amp; Ninja (black).</p>
<p><img title="NewImage.jpg" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/NewImage30.jpg" alt="NewImage.jpg" width="320" height="160" /></p>
<p>Fling will (ideally) work with all games that have an onscreen joystick. However, the three games that have been certified by the Ten One Design are <a href="http://itunes.apple.com/us/app/meteor-blitz/id328194605?mt=8">Meteor Blitz</a>, <a href="http://itunes.apple.com/us/app/across-age/id351652012?mt=8">Across Age</a>, <a href="http://itunes.apple.com/us/app/super-mega-worm/id388541990?mt=8">Super Megaworm</a>. You can also use two flings on both sides of your iPad. They are available in packs of pair at a discounted price.</p>
<p>Also, to top things off, it comes with a micro fiber carrying case which can double up as a cleaning cloth for your iPad's screen. Neat.</p>
