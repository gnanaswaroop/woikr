<p>It’s time for iPhone fans to rejoice once again with the iPhone4S finally coming to India and I just can't wait to get my hands on one. Aircel has <a href="https://www.facebook.com/aircel/posts/199391396802594" target="_blank">announced</a> that it'll launch the phone soon. We are yet to see a similar announcement from Airtel.</p>
<p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/11/Aircel_iPhone4S_India_Coming_Soon.jpg"><img title="Aircel_iPhone4S_India_Coming_Soon" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/11/Aircel_iPhone4S_India_Coming_Soon_thumb.jpg" alt="Aircel_iPhone4S_India_Coming_Soon" width="318" height="323" /></a></p>
<p>The exact release date and prices are not officially out yet. <a href="http://www.bgr.in/2011/10/25/nov-24-iphone-4s-india-launch-likely-will-be-expensive/" target="_blank">BGR.in</a> earlier reported that the release date will be November 24/25th 2011 and the prices would be</p>
<p>16GB – 40,000 INR<br>
32GB – 45,000 INR (Not clear, but seems logical).<br>
64GB – 50,000 INR</p>
<p>Let's all hope that this time, they get rid of the crappy “<a href="/iphone/airtel-announces-its-iphone-4-plans/" target="_blank">Reverse Subsidy</a>” plans and really offer something for a discount. Psst… I don’t mind a free bluetooth headset.</p>
<p>In other news, in case you're not really happy with these prices. The much awaited Unlocked iPhone4S is now available for <a href="http://store.apple.com/us/browse/home/shop_iphone/family/iphone/iphone4s" target="_blank">pre-order</a> in the US. Call your friends who're returning from the US and can smuggle one for you.</p>
<p>16GB – 649$ (around 32,500 INR + taxes)<br>
32GB – 749$<br>
64GB – 849$</p>
<p>So are you going get an iPhone4S or will you wait for the "real iPhone5"? BTW, did you know that Siri works great with <a href="http://gadgets.ndtv.com/video.aspx?id=213873" target="_blank">Indian accents</a> too.</p>
<p><strong>Update 13th November 2011</strong><br>
Aircel has <a href="https://www.facebook.com/aircel/posts/291275660903607" target="_blank">confirmed</a> 25th November 2011 as the release date. The <a href="http://www.aircel.com/AircelWar/appmanager/aircel/chennai?_nfpb=true&amp;_pageLabel=P32800172221321001090208" target="_blank">pre-orders</a> are starting on November 18th. Don't be late. Also Airtel has <a href="http://www.airtel.in/iphone-files/learn-more.html" target="_blank">confirmed</a> to be launching the iPhone4S too in an email communication.</p>
