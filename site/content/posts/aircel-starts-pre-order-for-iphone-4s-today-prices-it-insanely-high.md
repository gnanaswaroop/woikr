<p>As promised, <a href="http://www.aircel.com/iPhone4S" target="_blank">Aircel</a> and <a href="http://www.airtel.in/iphone-files/learn-more.html" target="_blank">Airtel</a> have started pre-order for the iPhone 4S today – 18th November 2011. The prices come as a surprise/shock to all Apple Fans who were <a href="/apple/aircel-bringing-iphone4s-to-india-unlocked-iphones-available-for-pre-order-in-the-us/" target="_blank">expecting it</a> to be priced &lt;40,000 INR.</p>
<p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/11/image.png"><img title="image" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/11/image_thumb.png" alt="image" width="571" height="126" /></a></p>

<p></p>
<p>The iPhone 4S is available in both Black and White. The phone will be factory unlocked and doesn't require any commitment to Aircel. The prices are</p>

<blockquote>
<p>16GB – 44,500 INR<br>
32GB – 50,900 INR<br>
64GB – 57,500 INR</p>
</blockquote>
<p>The pre-orders start today, so that means you need to pay the whole amount at an Aircel/Airtel store (can use cash/card) and get a pre-order confirmation receipt. As per the Aircel store, the iPhone will be handed over to you on 24th November 2011 mid-night, the official iPhone 4S launch date.  Airtel has a <a href="https://shop.actify.in/s4a/page.do?pageID=welcomePage" target="_blank">online pre-order</a> system or you can go visit their store and book your iPhone 4S too .</p>
<p>They’re also re-introducing an <strong>iPhone 4 – 8GB version at a new price of 37,900 INR</strong>. Which is actually lame as iPhone4 16GB version has been available in the market officially for 34,500 INR.</p>
<p><strong>Should you buy it in India?</strong></p>
<p>An unlocked iPhone4S is priced at 649$ in the US. With taxes and a <a href="http://www.google.co.in/search?q=1%24+in+INR" target="_blank">soaring dollar rate</a> (1 USD = 51.37 INR at the moment) the phone will cost you around 35-36,000 INR. It’s your judgment call if you can spend the extra 7-8,000 INR and purchase it here to avoid all the hassle.</p>
