<p>Following <a href="/iphone/iphone-4-price-announced-in-india/">Aircel's iPhone 4 plans announcement yesterday</a>, Airtel has today <a href="http://216.205.49.67/iphone4-website/iphone-4/choose-your-plan.html">revealed its iPhone 4 plans</a> for Indian consumers. All their plans as also based on the reverse subsidy model in which you pay the full price of the iPhone 4 to the carrier and then they pay you back a certain amount per month in form of rental discount for the next 2 years.</p>
<p>Airtel's iPhone 4 post paid plans start from INR 600 and go till INR 2000. Airtel is offering flat 50% discount on the monthly rental of these plans. So your monthly rental will range between INR 300 and INR 1000. Plans include free local airtel to airtel minutes, local SMSs &amp; 3G data (wherever available). Note that no free STD and ISD calls are bundled with the plans. Needless to say, the discount is only on the monthly rental, so you will have to pay in full for any calls above the free usage that you make.</p>
<p>Airtel has put up a <a href="http://www.airtel.in/iphone4/index.html">nice microsite</a> for consumers to easily select the plan of their choice. The site also displays your total savings over the reverse subsidy period of 2 years. Pre paid plans are also available.</p>
