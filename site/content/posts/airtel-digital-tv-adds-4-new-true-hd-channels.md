<p>In an attempt to further fuel the HD war in India, Airtel Digital TV has added 4 new true HD channels to its bouquet. These channels are:</p>
<ol>
<li>UTV Stars HD</li>
<li>History TV 18 HD</li>
<li>Colors HD</li>
<li>CNBC TV 18 Prime HD</li>
</ol>
<p>This move takes Airtel Digital TV's HD offering to 11 true HD channels. Surprisingly, they still do not offer Star Cricket HD &amp; ESPN HD. Nevertheless, this is good news for all their subscribers. They have also introduced a couple of <a href="http://www.airtel.in/hdchannels/">HD plans</a> for their subscribers which offer different  combinations of these channels.</p>
<p><a href="/home-entertainment/tatasky-is-maxed-out-on-its-capacity-to-beam-hd-channels/">Despite being maxed out on their transponder space</a>, <a href="/home-entertainment/tatasky-hd-will-add-espn-hd-in-time-for-indian-formula-1-grand-prix/">TataSky squeezed in ESPN HD (by dropping a few SD channels) last month</a> bringing their HD channel offering to 8 true HD channels. This clearly shows that DTH operators are thriving to offer as much HD channels as they can to rope in more customer. And with <a href="/home-entertainment/indian-cable-operators-planning-to-launch-hd-channels/">Cable operators gearing up to offer HD services next year</a>, they really need to buckle up.</p>
