<p>The three major 3G operators in India - Airtel, Idea &amp; Vodafone have join hands to offer pan India 3G roaming to their subscribers in the country. This move was inevitable as TRAI only allowed five 3G operators per circle. Those who could not get the 3G license in all the circles will have to tie up with other operators in order to provide 3G data roaming to its subscribers. And without this move, subscribing to a 3G data plan was totally useless if you are a frequent traveller.</p>
<p>In any case, this is good news for the subscribers. No word on whether the operators plan to charge anything extra or not. We hope they don't.</p>
