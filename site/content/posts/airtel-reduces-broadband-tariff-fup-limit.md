<p><img src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/12/logo_airtel.gif" /> </p>  <p>Airtel has reduced tariffs for most of its broadband plans in India. Here is a quick glance at the new tariff (Click to expand):</p>  <p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/02/image.png"><img title="image" height="313" alt="image" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/02/image_thumb.png" width="554" /></a> </p>   <p>The caveat here is the reduced FUP (Fair usage policy) quota. FUP quota is an absurd limit applied by ISPs. Once you use up your quota in a billing cycle (uploads + downloads both are counted), you will get half speeds for the rest of the billing period.</p>  <p>Our recommendation – If you are on one of the old plans, and are a heavy user, stay on that as the FUP quota was much more in the old plans. For example, the 1699/- per month 1 mbps unlimited plan , had a FUP quota of 100GB.</p>  <p>However, if you are a casual user, and don’t care about the FUP, go ahead and jump the boat.</p>
