<p>Airtel has finally launched their <a href="http://airtel.in/3g">3G services</a> in the cyber city of Hyderabad in India. This launch was much anticipated as Hyderabad is one of the biggest IT hubs in the country and has the potential of being one of the largest 3G subscriber base in India.</p>
<p>So far, we are getting <a href="/telecom/airtel-3g-offering-amazing-speed-in-delhincr">mixed reactions</a> for Airtel's 3G performance in other parts of India. It will be interesting to see the performance in Hyderabad.</p>
