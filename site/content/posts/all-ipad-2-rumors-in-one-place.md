<p>If you've been thinking of buying an iPad since a few months, your biggest problem has to be the iPad 2. Everyone knows that sometimes Apple offers insane upgrades between two versions and this time it's more than expected. iPhone 4 is already out with a much higher resolution screen and so many new features and obviously these features are supposed to trickle down to the next release of iPad as well.</p>
<p>So what exactly should you expect from iPad 2? We prepared a list so you can decide if it's worth the wait.</p>

<ul>
	<li><strong>Video Calling<br>
</strong>This one is coming for sure. iPhones, iPods, Macs all have FaceTime. Why should iPad stay behind?</li>
	<li><strong>Lighter, Sleeker Body<br>
</strong>Given Apple's record with iPhones, iPods and Macbooks, we think that this will happen too.</li>
	<li><strong>Better Display<br>
</strong>Definitely yes. But will it have the Retina Display like the iPhone? The resolution will be too hot for the iPad Processor to handle. We expect it will be a higher resolution display but maybe not the Retina Display. Unless Apple redefines the term for the device of that size. According to the <a href="http://thenextweb.com/apple/2010/12/28/ipad-2-to-have-a-smudge-free-reflection-proof-screen/" target="_blank">latest</a>, it's actually supposed to have a smudge-proof and reflection-free display.</li>
	<li><strong>Better Processor</strong><br>
Maybe a dual core processor? I don't think so. Definitely an upgrade but not that big an upgrade possibly.</li>
	<li><strong>3-axis gyroscopes<br>
</strong>Certainly. Why not?</li>
	<li><strong>USB Ports</strong><br>
Yes there are <a href="http://thenextweb.com/apple/2010/11/26/digitimes-ipad-2-will-ship-with-5-new-features/" target="_blank">such rumors</a>. Though it'll be a great addition to transfer photos from your camera and data from other devices but we find it hard to believe.</li>
</ul>
<p>Do you think these are enough reasons for you to wait?</p>
