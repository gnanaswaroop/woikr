<p>Amazon has officially launched their kindle e-reader in india. The base model is priced at INR 6,999 and is available exclusively at Croma retail stores throughout India and also online at <a href="http://www.cromaretail.com/Kindle-e-reader-Wi-Fi-152cm-pc-15040-160.aspx">cromaretail.com</a>. In order to facilitate Indian readers buy kindle ebooks, Amazon has also launched the <a href="http://www.amazon.com/gp/browse/?ie=UTF8&amp;camp=1789&amp;cor=IN&amp;creative=390957&amp;linkCode=ur2&amp;node=133141011&amp;tag=quickonlineti-20">India kindle store</a> which offers over a million ebooks at cheap prices.</p>
<p>Here is a quick list of the base model specs:</p>
<ul>
<li><span>6 inches E Ink Display</span></li>
<li><span>USB v2.0 micro-B Connector</span></li>
<li><span>2 GB Internal Memory, Up to 1,400 Books</span></li>
<li><span>Supports Public and Private Wi-Fi Networks</span></li>
<li><span>In the Box: Kindle, USB 2.0 cable, Quick Start Guide</span></li>
</ul>
