<p><a href="http://amazon.co.uk">Amazon.co.uk</a> is offering free Super Saver Delivery to India, Australia, New Zealand and South Africa till 15th of May, 2011. According to them all the items that are eligible to be shipped to these destinations are entitled for free shipping. In other words, this offer applies to Books, Music, DVD and Video.</p>
<p>The only other major <a href="http://www.amazon.co.uk/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=200627060" target="_blank">condition</a> is that you need to order items worth at least £25 (~ 1800 INR). Most of the other conditions are trivial, like items should be shipped at the same address, they should be available while ordering etc.</p>
<p>All in all, it's an excellent offer if you find something worth getting from them. One thing that will be cheaper than India is console games and they should be covered under this offer. Give it a try and tell us!</p>
