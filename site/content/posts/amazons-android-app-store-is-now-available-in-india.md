<p>Its time for Android fanboys to rejoice! <a href="http://market.android.com/">Amazon's Android App Store</a> is now available in India. The store was previously only available to people residing in the US and carried out awesome <a href="http://www.amazon.com/appstoredeals">free app a day promotions</a> and gave away pretty nice paid apps to early adopters.</p>
<p>So point your android phones to <a href="http://market.android.com">market.android.com</a> to start using the store!</p>
<p><strong>UPDATE</strong>: It looks like amazon pulled the international stores a day after they were launched. Sorry for the confusion. We will update you as soon as the app store is again available in India.</p>
