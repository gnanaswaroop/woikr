<p>Yeah, that's right. According to a strange list of best companies picked by <a href="http://bwnt.businessweek.com/interactive_reports/global_champs_2009/index.asp?chan=top+news_special+report+--+health+care+reform_special+report+-+world%27s+best+companies+2009+#methodology" target="_blank">A. <p>Apparently, the basis of the list is sales figures in 2008 and international sales percentage. To qualify a company needs to have sales &gt; $10 billion with atleast 25% from foreign regions.</p>
<p>Nintendo is followed by Google and Apple on the list. Is the world getting better or is Nintendo turning into a big evil conglomerate?</p>
