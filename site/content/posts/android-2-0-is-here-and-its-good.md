Features in brief:
<ul>
	<li>Accessible anywhere Contacts and with something they call Quick Contact.</li>
	<li>Support for multiple screen sizes in a write-once run-anywhere sort of way.</li>
	<li>Full Bluetooth 2.1 support for those awesome games.</li>
	<li>Exchange support</li>
	<li>Full multitouch and many other tweaks and improvements.</li>
</ul>
