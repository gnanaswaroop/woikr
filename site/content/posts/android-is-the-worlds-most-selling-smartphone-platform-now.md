<p>With the pace it has been growing, everyone expected it to happen one day. According to a report by <a href="http://www.canalys.com/" target="_blank">Canalys</a>, Android OS now has 33.3% share in the smartphone market. They were 2.3% ahead of Nokia in Q4 2010. Apple comes third 16.2% and RIM follows with 14.6%.</p>
