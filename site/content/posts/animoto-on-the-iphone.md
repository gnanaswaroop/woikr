<p><a href="http://www.animoto.com" target="_blank">Animoto</a> is an online video creation service where you can upload your images and create cool videos out of them. They have developed an <a href="http://iphone.animoto.com/" target="_blank">app for the iPhone</a>. The app lets you choose photos from your photo library and/or camera roll, select a track from a limited set of music tracks available with them and then creates a super cool video out of it using various image transitions.</p>

<img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1877" title="animoto_screen1" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/12/animoto_screen1.png" alt="animoto_screen1" width="256" height="380" />

The steps include:
<ol>
	<li>Choose between 8 to 16 photos from your iPhone
<img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1878" title="animoto_screen2" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/12/animoto_screen2.png" alt="animoto_screen2" width="250" height="375" /></li>
	<li>Listen and select a soundtrack
<img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1879" title="animoto_screen3" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/12/animoto_screen3.png" alt="animoto_screen3" width="253" height="376" />

<img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1880" title="animoto_screen4" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/12/animoto_screen4.png" alt="animoto_screen4" width="253" height="379" /></li>
	<li>Rename your video (if you want to)
<img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1881" title="animoto_screen5" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/12/animoto_screen5.png" alt="animoto_screen5" width="247" height="375" /></li>
	<li>Submit the video for rendering
<img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1882" title="animoto_screen6" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/12/animoto_screen6.png" alt="animoto_screen6" width="252" height="374" /></li>
</ol>
<p>The last steps might take a few seconds to a few minutes depending upon the size and number of images selected. Once the video is created, it is available under My Video to play, share and edit.</p>

<img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1883" title="animoto_screen7" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/12/animoto_screen7.png" alt="animoto_screen7" width="250" height="372" />
<p>You can share the video with your friends and family right from the iPhone. The video is stored on Animoto's servers and hence can be played back from anywhere in the world.</p>
