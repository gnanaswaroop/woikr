I liked the AOL ad on the first glance but I frankly didn't get what are they trying to convey here.
<p>I guess it means that you hate your boss and hence you should move to AOL so you get rid of him. But even if he moves to AOL, it'll be equally good for you won't it be? You wanted to get rid of him and it happened. And what's the guarantee that if you move to AOL, your boss won't follow you there. After all it's an interesting place to work and that's why you moved there. I don't know, maybe I'm thinking too much.</p>
Anyone has a logical explanation for this?
