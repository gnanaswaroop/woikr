<p>Now that the <a href="/iphone/apple-announces-iphone-4s/">iPhone 4S has landed</a>, it is time for price reductions on the previous iPhone models. Apple is still continuing with the iPhone 3GS (proving again that it is still a very powerful device capable of running iOS 5). The iPhone 4's capacity has been reduced to 8GB. The previous models are available at dirt cheap prices in the US with 2 year contracts. But who gives a shit about contract prices? We want to buy unlocked iPhone from US and bring them to India and use them here. So here are the reduced prices of these two babies:</p>
<ul>
<li><a href="http://store.apple.com/us/product/MC637?mco=MjU5MjAxMjg">iPhone 3GS 8GB: <strong>$375</strong></a></li>
<li><a href="http://store.apple.com/us/browse/home/shop_iphone/family/iphone/iphone4?mco=MjU1NTYyNDI">iPhone 4 8GB: <strong>$549</strong></a></li>
</ul>
<p>They are available for purchase now. No official word yet whether Apple will be selling the unlocked iPhone 4S from day one, but there is <a href="http://www.tuaw.com/2011/10/04/apples-site-suggests-unlocked-iphone-4s-will-be-available/">considerable evidence</a> that suggest that they plan to launch it. And if they do, there is a high chance that they will price it same as the previous unlocked iPhone 4 prices.</p>
