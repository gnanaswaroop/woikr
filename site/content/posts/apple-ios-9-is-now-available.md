<p>Apple’s latest mobile operating system is now available for download. iOS 9 focuses majorly on performance improvements and is compatible with iPhones up to 4S and iPads up to original mini. Here are some key changes in the new version:</p>
<ul>
<li>Battery life improvements.</li>
<li>A new system wide font. This is the same font used in Apple Watch.</li>
<li>A new News app to subscribe and catch up to &nbsp;news.</li>
<li>Improved notes app supporting photos, sketches &amp; lists.</li>
<li>Transit directions in Maps.</li>
<li>Store cards in Wallet (previously passbook) in addition to Financial institution cards.</li>
<li>Far better multitasking features for the iPad.</li>
<li>Smarter Siri.</li>
<li>6 digit passcodes.</li>
</ul>
<p>The battery life improvements alone make this upgrade worthy. Head to Settings – General – Software Update on your device to update to the latest version.</p>
