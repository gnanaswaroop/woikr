<p><img title="ipad-air.png" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2013/10/ipad-air.png" alt="ipad air" width="233" height="300" /></p>
<p>Apple launched the <a href="http://www.apple.com/ipad-air/">iPad Air</a> and <a href="http://www.apple.com/ipad-mini/">iPad Mini with Retina display</a> at their press event on October 22, 2013. The iPad Air is the new flagship model in the iPad line which boasts some very impressive specs:</p>
<ul>
<li>A7 chip with 64-bit architecture and M7 motion coprocessor</li>
<li>FaceTime HD camera</li>
<li>5 MP iSight camera</li>
<li>1080p video recording</li>
<li>10 hours battery life</li>
<li>Weight: 1 pound</li>
</ul>
<p>What is impressive is that Apple has managed to juice up the power, reduce the size &amp; weight and still maintain a 10 hours battery life. The new iPad follows the form factor of the iPad Mini with thinner bezel on the sides. The name air comes from the incredible light weight of the device. The iPad Air is priced at the same price points as the previous generation iPad.</p>
<p><img title="ipad-mini-retina.png" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2013/10/ipad-mini-retina.png" alt="ipad mini with retina display" width="124" height="108" /></p>
<p>Apple also announced the iPad Mini with retina display at the event. According to Tim Cook, retina display was the most demanded feature in the iPad Mini from their customers. The new iPad Mini packs exactly the same features as the iPad Air and differs just in size and weight. The iPad Mini weighs at an impressive 0.73 pounds making it extremely light. It is kept at a starting price of $399 for the 16GB WiFi only model.</p>
<p>What was surprising was the fact that the mini is now as powerful as the air. In other words, the iPad Mini upgrade has skipped a generation. It was previously powered by the A5 processor and has now jumped to A7, skilling the A6 family. It also boasts of all the features of iPad Air. So it is basically just about your personal preference now. I personally prefer the iPad Mini just because of it small, yet extremely appropriate size. With the same battery life and the amazing retina display, it will be very difficult to choose one out of the two for many people.</p>
<p>Apple is also continuing the iPad Mini without the retina display at a starting price of $299. Apparently, <a href="/apple/why-is-apple-still-selling-the-ipad-2/">iPad 2 also lives on</a> with a starting price of $399.</p>
<p>No word yet on India launch and prices. We will keep you posted. Stay tuned.</p>
