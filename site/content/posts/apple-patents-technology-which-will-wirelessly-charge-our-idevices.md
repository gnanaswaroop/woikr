<p>It looks like Apple has filed a patent for a system which can one day let us charge our devices wirelessly. The system utilizes the <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wireless_energy_transfer">Near Field Magnetic Resonance</a> wireless energy transfer technology to transmit power from one device to another. So, in future, we will be able to charge our iDevices (or anything else, like Apple's wireless keyboard and trackpad) just by keeping them close to our macs. Isn't that awesome?</p>
