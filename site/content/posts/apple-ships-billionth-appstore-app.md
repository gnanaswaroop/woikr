Apple has shipped its billionth AppStore app. The one billionth app, <a onclick="s_objectID=&quot;http://itunes.apple.com/WebObjects/MZStore.woa/wa/viewSoftware?id=305479724&amp;mt=8_1&quot;;return this.s_oc?this.s_oc(e):true" href="http://itunes.apple.com/WebObjects/MZStore.woa/wa/viewSoftware?id=305479724&amp;mt=8">Bump</a> created by Bump Technologies, was downloaded by Connor Mulcahey, age 13, of Weston, CT.
<p>Apple ran a contest for this milestone in which the lucky chap to download the billionth app (Connor Mulcahey) will get the following goodies:</p>

<ul>
	<li>a $10,000 iTunes gift card</li>
	<li>an  iPod touch</li>
	<li>a Time Capsule</li>
	<li>a Macbook Pro</li>
</ul>
<p>Whoa. Thats a lot of stuff. Congratulations Connor!</p>
