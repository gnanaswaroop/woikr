<p>A woman in Norway wandering in the icy cold -14°C weather took out her iPhone and saw that the cold had taken a heavy toll on it. The phone was shattered beyond repair. She took it to the Apple Store but the Apple Geniuses refused to repair it saying that iPhone isn't designed for extreme temperatures. And that means below freezing and above 35°C. 35 Degree Celsius! Isn't that a bit too low for extreme heat! When I had the iPhone, it worked perfectly up to 45 °C and never caused any trouble due to heating up.</p>
<p>Anyway as a warning if you are travelling to places with extreme weather anytime soon, it will be safer to leave your iPhone home. Unless you want to see it break down.</p>
