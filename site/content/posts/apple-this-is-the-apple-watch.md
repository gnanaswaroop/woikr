<p style="text-align: justify;">In addition to launching the <a href="/apple/apple-launches-the-iphone-6/">iPhone 6</a>, Apple also introduced its brand new product line – the Apple Watch.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><img title="apple-watch.png" src="/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/apple-watch.png" alt="apple watch" width="414" height="395" border="0"></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The Apple watch is a wearable device which comes in 2 sizes – 38mm and 42mm. The crown, or as apple calls it – digital crown, is the new way of interacting with the watch without blocking the screen. The watch also offers a touch screen – so don’t worry, you are covered there. The touch screen is an advanced capacitive &amp; resistive screen which can differentiate between a tap and a press. So you can use different modes for different actions.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><img title="apple-watch-straps.png" src="/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/apple-watch-straps.png" alt="apple watch straps" width="600" height="311" border="0"></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">In addition to offering various watch faces, the watch will be capable of making and receiving phone calls, Sending and receiving text messages &amp; providing some Siri functions. Apple has also built in an intelligent algorithm that can figure out the message context and give you pre-populated replies to tap onto instead of having to type them in – which is really cool.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The watch also features something called as Digital Touch which allows you to communicate with your selective friends in weird new ways. You can read more about them <a href="http://www.apple.com/watch/features/">here</a>.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><img title="apple watch sensors.png" src="/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/apple-watch-sensors.png" alt="apple watch sensors" width="281" height="332" border="0"></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The watch is also an always on fitness monitor with various sensors on the back that monitor your heartbeat. Coupled with the inbuilt accelerometer and GPS and WiFi from an iPhone, it can record various types of physical activity data that you can use to track your fitness activities. There are various fitness apps that will be available on the watch for all kinds of users.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The watch will also be able to give you a tactile feedback similar to a tap on your wrist. And there can be different types of taps for different types of notifications. This is going to be great as we definitely need some filtering when notifications are coming to our wrists.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">In addition to the above features, the watch will also support the Apple Pay features and you can do merchant transactions by brining your watch closer to the mobile enabled merchant terminals.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The Apple Watch will be available in early 2015 at a starting price of $349. At this entry price, it is certainly going to be a premium product, however, it does look like the first smart watch which seems to have understood the concept of how smart watches should work. In Tim Cook’s words – we have not shrunk an iPhone into a watch. This is really important as no one wants to interact with their watches (or fitness trackers) the way they interact with their phones. A new, innovative way to interact with such devices was needed and the digital crown coupled with the advanced touchscreen seems to be the right way to do it.&nbsp;</p>
