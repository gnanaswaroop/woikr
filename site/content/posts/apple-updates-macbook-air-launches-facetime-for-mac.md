Check out some pics to drool over at the end of this post.</p>  <p><img title="image" alt="image" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/10/image3.png" width="221" height="47" />&#160;</p>  <p>Apple has also launched FaceTime for all its Mac users. The software is currently in beta and is available as a free download at <a title="http://www.apple.com/mac/facetime/" href="http://www.apple.com/mac/facetime/">http://www.apple.com/mac/facetime/</a>.</p>  <p><img title="image" alt="image" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/10/image4.png" width="234" height="240" /> </p>  <p>This will enable millions of existing mac users to make FaceTime calls to iPhone 4, iPod Touch and other mac users. The setup is hassle free with literally few clicks required to initiate a FaceTime call.
