<p>Apple just sent out emails to let everyone know that their annual world wide developer conference (WWDC) 2012 is scheduled for June 11 - 15 in San Francisco. The conference is primarily aimed at developers who want to interact with Apple engineers, however, many iconic Apple products have been launched at the conference in the past. As is the case with Apple, we will not know until the conference if the next iPhone will be released at the time or not.</p>
