<p>HDFC bank has become the first bank in India to launch a mobile banking app in Hindi. The app is available on the <a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.snapwork.hdfc.hindi&amp;hl=en">Google Play store</a> and can be used by HDFC bank customers to bank in Hindi and perform various other banking operations like payment of utility bills, credit card payments, funds transfer and more.</p>

<p><img title="hdfc-hindi-mobile-banking.png" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2012/12/hdfc-hindi-mobile-banking.png" alt="hdfc hindi mobile banking" width="153" height="230" /> <img title="hdfc-mobile-banking-hindi.png" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2012/12/hdfc-mobile-banking-hindi.png" alt="hdfc hindi mobile banking" width="153" height="230" /></p>
<p>The app is a good move to tap a lot more customers and get them used to mobile banking practices. Majority of population in India is Hindi speaking and this move should make it easy for them to get on board.</p>
