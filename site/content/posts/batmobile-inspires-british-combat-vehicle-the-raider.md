<p>Remember the awesome Batmobile from Batman Begins and The Dark Knight? It was called "The Tumbler" and they <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batmobile#cite_note-29" target="_blank">actually made 6 production pieces</a> for the movies. We didn't know people other than fans were actually studying it's design.</p>
<p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/12/Tumbler-Batmobile.jpg"><img title="Tumbler-Batmobile" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/12/Tumbler-Batmobile-300x165.jpg" alt="" width="300" height="165" /></a></p>
<p><a href="http://www.baesystems.com/" target="_blank">BAE Systems</a>, the world's second largest defense company, recently unveiled a concept combat vehicle whose design was apparently inspired by the Batmobile.“The Raider” has been designed as a “a small, highly agile autonomous reconnaissance and attack vehicle which can carry a variety of payloads.”</p>
<p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/12/the_raider_batmobile.jpg"><img title="the_raider_batmobile" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/12/the_raider_batmobile-300x191.jpg" alt="" width="300" height="191" /></a></p>
<p>On being asked will "The Raider" be considered serious by British Army, because of it's Batmobile inspiration, Hisham Awad replied:</p>

<blockquote>“Well, why not? In all seriousness, we decided that we didn’t have a monopoly on inspiration, and if we saw something in a film that we thought might be a good idea, why not take a look at it and see if there’s something practical we can develop. You see, it turns like a motorbike and it has the same wheel configuration.”</blockquote>
<p>While we'd love to see a Bat-Pod being launched in the market, we hope someone hasn't designed the "3D Sonar System" yet.</p>
