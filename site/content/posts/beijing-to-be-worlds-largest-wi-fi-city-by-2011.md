<p>Apparently, the folks in Beijing are too happy with the free wifi test runs during the mammoth Olympics of 2008. They are now gonna roll out the free wi-fi service all over Beijing and will cover it completely by 2011.</p>
<p>According to the  <em>Beijing Business Today</em>, the plans for free Wi-Fi are being extended:</p>

<blockquote>
<p>by 2011 Beijing will become the “largest wireless city in China”...</p>
<p>...At present there are pilot schemes for in the districts of Xuanwu, Chongwen, Chaoyang, Shijingshan, Daxing and Pinggu, in places associated with agriculture, local government offices, and scientific technology.</p>
<p>Furthermore, nearly half of Beijing’s top universities have wireless Internet: Tsinghua university  alone has 500 hotspots.</p>
</blockquote>
