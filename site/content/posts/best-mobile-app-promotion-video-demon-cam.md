<p>It's brilliant. I doubt that anyone else will ever top this effort, especially to promote a 99 cent mobile app.</p>
<p>I strongly suggest you to watch it in full screen. Switch the resolution to 720p. Buffer it if you have to, but this deserves to be seen in High Definition.</p>
