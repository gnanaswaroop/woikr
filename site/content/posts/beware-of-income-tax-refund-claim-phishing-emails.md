<p>I've just got a phishing email claiming that I’ve got a lot of money in tax refund and I should click on the link for them to process it.</p>
<p>Damn, Google – Why couldn’t you filter out this spam? It landed right in the inbox. Here’s the email content.</p>

<blockquote>Dear <strong>Valued Taxpayer</strong>,<br>
We have reviewed your tax fiscal payment for previous months and have resolved that you are qualified to obtain a refund of the sum of xx,xxx.00 INR which is your accumulated tax excesses. Please submit a tax refund request and allow us to process it within 7 days.<br>
To submit a request, <strong>CLICK HERE</strong><br>
Thanks for taking the time to learn about our tax refund.It's one more way Income tax department can make your tax payment experience better.<br>
Endeavor to fill in your Information correctly,to enable us make refund to your account without any delay.<br>
Refund can be delayed for some reasons:
<ul>
	<li>Applying after deadline of notification</li>
	<li>Submitting incorrect account information</li>
</ul>
<strong>Tax Refund Department<br>
Department of revenue,<br>
Ministry of finance, Government of India.</strong></blockquote>
<p>The Link has been suppressed for obvious reasons. Although this email comes from probably a trusted sender <a href="mailto:“[email removed]">“[email removed]</a>”, watch out for the website which actually goes to a domain called as zinuru.org.</p>
<p></p>
<p>The page is a phished website with some of the links pointing to NSDL and webpage which the users can enter the bank details.<br>
<a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/12/image6.png"><img title="image" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/12/image_thumb1.png" alt="image" width="698" height="572" /></a></p>
<p>Beware, although banks have provided additional security measures through SMS Validations, Grid number validation, your common sense about phishing would help you a long way.</p>
