<p>Bill Gates is back. Apparently, he has created a new company called the <a href="http://www.bgc3.com/" target="_blank">BG c3</a>. The BG in the name probably stands for Bill Gates. What's mysterious is the c3 part. The logo is pretty creative though:</p>
<p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/10/c3-logo.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-medium wp-image-1565" title="c3-logo" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/10/c3-logo-300x242.jpg" alt="" width="300" height="242" /></a></p>
<p>If you look closely, the logo spells c3.</p>
<p>The new company has a federal trademark as a think-tank, covering:</p>

<ul>
	<li>Scientific and technological services.</li>
	<li>Industrial analysis and research.</li>
	<li>Design and development of computer hardware and software.</li>
</ul>
<p>Gates has opened a small office near his home for the company. Whatever be the motive, let's hope that this new venture does not push us to install crappy things (read OS) on our computers.</p>
