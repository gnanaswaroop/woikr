<p>In this new video released by BlackBerry, they compare the PlayBook browser to the iPad and well, the PlayBook performs comprehensively better!</p>

<p>Here are the key things to note:</p>

<ul>
	<li>The PlayBook browser loads and renders the websites faster than the iPad every single time.</li>
	<li>PlayBook loads flash convincingly. It's not the choppy, sub par rendering you see on most mobile devices. I wonder why they didn't show Flash video playback though.</li>
	<li>PlayBook scores 100/100 and renders perfectly on the <a href="http://acid3.acidtests.org/" target="_blank">Acid3 test</a>. iPad scores 100 too with a tiny imperfect pixel. On my Mac, Chrome scores 97 with really screwed up rendering, Firefox 4 Beta scores 97 with decent rendering and Safari scores 100 with perfect rendering.</li>
	<li>PlayBook renders the Pocket Full of Canvas demo <a href="http://www.nihilogic.dk/labs/pocket_full_of_canvas/#presets/embossblobs.js" target="_blank">Emboss Blobs</a> pretty well and smooth. iPad is pretty choppy. On my Mac, all the browsers perform smooth to choppy depending on the load. Good Job <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/QNX" target="_blank">QNX</a>!</li>
	<li>Despite how good the PlayBook performs, you can't help but notice how small it looks. The 7" size is not the best way to browse. Especially with all the large-ish smartphones available nowadays.</li>
</ul>
Anyway, we are happy that BlackBerry is letting the product do the talking. After all, that it is the best approach.
