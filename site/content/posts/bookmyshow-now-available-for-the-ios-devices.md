<p>We've long cribbed that movie booking was not easy from mobile devices.    <br /><a href="http://in.bookmyshow.com/" target="_blank">BookMyShow</a> answers our cries by releasing an iOS version of the application.</p>  <p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/12/BookMyShowLogo.jpg"><img title="BookMyShow-Logo" alt="BookMyShow-Logo" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/12/BookMyShowLogo_thumb.jpg" width="172" height="59" /></a> </p>  <p>Here's a quick look at what all the application provides. </p>  <p>The iOS application allows you to select from a list of movies only at the moment, but booking for plays, sport and other events is going to come soon – Well, this is a start.</p>  <p></p>  <div></div>  <p>It's got a couple of interesting features. One can</p>  <ul>   <li>     <div>Select the movie to watch and can book across all cinemas in all the Indian cities. </div>   </li>    <li>     <div>Seat selection – pretty useful if you’d not want to sitting in the front rows. I wonder when the Indian railways will ever implement this for their online reservation. </div>   </li>    <li>     <div>Payment options are flexible Credit/Debit cards &amp; Internet banking. </div>   </li> </ul>  <p>Here're some screenshots. </p>  <p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/12/BookMyShowHomeScreen.jpg"><img title="BookMyShow-HomeScreen" alt="BookMyShow-HomeScreen" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/12/BookMyShowHomeScreen_thumb.jpg" width="169" height="253" /></a>&#160;
