<p>Great news for all you PC gamers. Braid, earlier an Xbox Live exclusive, is now available on PC (Windows only).</p>
<p>If you are a gamer and have been living in a cave, Braid is last year's super hit  indie game that won everyone's  heart with it's ingenious puzzles, art style and music. As described by the creator, Jonathan Blow, "Braid is a platform game in a paintery style where you manipulate the flow of time  to solve puzzles".</p>
<p>Take our word for it, the game is an amazing, mind-bending experience. Gamer or not, you must own and play it. The game is a $15 download away from <a href="http://store.steampowered.com/app/26800/" target="_blank">Steam</a> or various other websites mentioned in the <a href="http://braid-game.com/news/?p=556" target="_blank">official announcement.</a></p>
<p>If you're still in doubt, download the demo from <a href="http://www.impulsedriven.com/braid" target="_blank">Impulse</a> and prepare to be amazed.</p>
