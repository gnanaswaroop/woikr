<p>Following the launch of  <a href="/3g/mtnl-launches-3g-telecom-service-in-india/" target="_blank">"Jadoo" by MTNL</a>, state owned Bharat Sanchar Nigam Ltd. (<a href="http://www.bsnl.co.in" target="_blank">BSNL</a>) has launched 3G cellular services in India.</p>
<p>The service offers 3G voice and data plans. Head over to <a href="http://bsnl.co.in/newsdetailed.php?news_id=448" target="_blank">http://bsnl.co.in/newsdetailed.php?news_id=448</a> for tariffs and chages.</p>
<p>BSNL has put up a pretty good FAQ sheet to clarify your queries here: <a href="http://www.bsnl.in/faq/faqans.php?paramCategory=3G%20Mobile%20Services" target="_blank">http://www.bsnl.in/faq/faqans.php?paramCategory=3G%20Mobile%20Services</a></p>
<p>In case you wish to register online for 3G service, you can do so here: <a href="http://portal.bsnl.in/bsnlccp/New3GuserReg.aspx" target="_blank">http://portal.bsnl.in/bsnlccp/New3GuserReg.aspx</a></p>
