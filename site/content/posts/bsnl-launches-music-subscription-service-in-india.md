BSNL has launched an amazing service for all you fans of Indian music. It's  a part of BSNL's new <a href="http://bsnl.hungama.com/index.php" target="_blank">Hungama portal</a> and it allows you to (finally!) download legal music in India at a decent price.

It's not that this is the first legal music download service in India. Other broadband providers (like <a href="http://myhome.tataindicombroadband.in/Cultures/en-US/Entertainment/Music/Music+Downloads/" target="_blank">Tata Indicom</a>) have had such services since long but they have all been expensive. And, who uses Tata Indicom anyway?

<strong>What we like</strong>
<ul>
	<li>Rich library comprising of 30,000+ tracks. You can find a lot of new and old albums. They also have some collections from old singers like <em>Kishore Kumar</em>.</li>
	<li>Only INR 149/month.</li>
	<li>Includes regional, devotional albums as well (If you're that type).</li>
	<li>More than 1000 music videos.</li>
	<li>Ability to preview songs (A bit useless since you have already paid for it)</li>
</ul>
<strong>What we don't like</strong>
<ul>
	<li>The library still isn't rich enough for our taste. For instance, we couldn't find classics like <em>Taal </em>and <em>Dil Se</em>. You'll have to stick to ripping your Audio CDs.</li>
	<li>No Western music.</li>
	<li>Tied to BSNL Broadband.</li>
	<li>Search sucks. Try searching for <em>"Kishore Kumar"</em> without quotes. The first result  has neither <em>Kishore</em> nor <em>Kumar</em>. Now search <em>"Kishore Kumar" </em>with quotes. Better results but try pressing next.</li>
</ul>

	<li>DRM issues aren't clearly mentioned. Will the music be tied to a single PC/MP3 player or to a person or to the family?</li>
</ul>
But all thing considered it's a good service and more importantly a step in the right direction. For a country like ours, a subscription based service is the best way to counter music piracy.

I hope they will cleanup the bugs in time and add more music to the collection. And, if possible open it to non-BSNL users.
