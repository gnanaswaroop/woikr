<p>Times Group has launched an online shop offering Ayurvedic and organic products for sale in India. Aptly names <a href="http://satvikshop.com">Satvikshop</a>, The site stocks products from Dabur, Organic India among other brands and aims to offer over 2000 products by the end of this year. The current objective of the site is to offer the convenience of buying Ayurvedic and organic products from one place buy offering pricing at par with the offline stores. The site also offers free consultation with their in house panel of 20 experts. Users can post their questions online and get responses via chat, email or calls from the call center.</p>
<p>Overall a good initiative in the sector. Note that Himalaya already has its <a href="http://store.himalayahealthcare.com/default.asp">online store</a>.</p>
