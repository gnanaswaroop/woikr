<p>The iPhone camera is great. But it can be much better if there was a flash. The camera takes great pictures in ample light conditions. However, it fails when it comes to low light scenes.</p>
<p>The folks at <a href="http://www.snapturelabs.com" target="_blank">Snapture</a> (awesome camera replacement app for iPhone) have developed a prototype <a href="http://www.snapturelabs.com/snaptureflash.html" target="_blank">SnaptureFlash</a> add on for jailbroken iPhones. This add on is basically a xenon flash fitted inside a slip on cover for the iPhone. The flash will work with the Snapture app on jailbroken iPhones only.</p>
