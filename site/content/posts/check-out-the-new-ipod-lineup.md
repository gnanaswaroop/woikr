<p>In addition to launching the <a href="/apple/the-iphone-5-is-here/">iPhone 5</a>, Apple also announced its new lineup of iPods. They have done major hardware upgrades to iPod touch and iPod nano. Lets look at both of them.</p>
<p><img title="iPod-touch-name-transparent.png" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2012/09/iPod-touch-name-transparent.png" alt="iPod touch" width="173" height="32" /></p>
<p>The iPod touch has got a major design overhaul with the 4 inches retina screen (same as the iPhone 5), 5 megapixels iSight camera, FaceTime HD camera, Panorama, Dual core A5 chip, something called the loop &amp; new vibrant colors!</p>
<p>Lets have a look at the other new specs:</p>
<ul>
<li>Siri</li>
<li>802.11a/b/g/n Wi-Fi (802.11n 2.4GHz and 5GHz)</li>
<li>Bluetooth 4.0</li>
<li>Nike+ support built in</li>
<li>1136-by-640-pixel resolution at 326 pixels per inch</li>
<li>Lightning port</li>
<li>iPod touch loop - basically a wristband to secure your iPod</li>
</ul>
<p>The new iPod Touch looks gorgeous and is the thinnest iPod yet. It comes bundled with Apple's new EarPods and color matched loop. Here are some high resolution images:</p>

<p>The iPod touch is priced at <strong>$299 for 32Gb model and $399 for the 64GB</strong> one.</p>
<p><img title="ipod-nano-name-transparent.png" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2012/09/ipod-nano-name-transparent.png" alt="ipod nano" width="165" height="33" /></p>
<p>The nano has been completely redesigned from the ground up. It features a 2.5 inches multi touch screen and an iPhone like home button. Other key upgrades include:</p>
<ul>
<li>Hardware buttons for volume control and basic playback operations</li>
<li>Lightning port</li>
<li>Video playback</li>
<li>Built in pedometer </li>
<li>Nike+ built in</li>
<li>Bluetooth 4.0</li>
<li>Enhanced battery life giving up to 30 hours of music playback</li>
</ul>
