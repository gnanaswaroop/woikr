<p>Google is doing some charity work and they have invited you to be a part for free! Everytime you open a new tab on Google Chrome, they do something for a good cause from your side. They contribute to the following 5 good causes and you can choose to contribute to different ones on different days.</p>

<ul>
	<li><strong><a href="http://www.nature.org/" target="_blank">The Nature Conservancy</a></strong> is a conservation organization working around the world to protect ecologically important lands and waters for nature and people.</li>
	<li><a href="http://charitywater.org/" target="_blank">charity: water</a> is a non-profit organization bringing clean and safe drinking water to people in developing nations.</li>
	<li><strong><a href="http://www.doctorswithoutborders.org/" target="_blank">Doctors Without Borders</a> </strong>is an independent, international medical humanitarian organization that delivers emergency aid in nearly 70 countries to those most in need.<strong> </strong></li>
	<li><strong><a href="http://www.untecho.org/" target="_blank">Un Techo para mi País</a></strong> is working in 19 countries across the Americas to improve the quality of life of impoverished families through the construction of transitional housing</li>
	<li><strong><a href="http://www.roomtoread.org/" target="_blank">Room to Read</a> </strong>is a non-profit organization seeking to transform the lives of children in developing countries by focusing on literacy and gender equality in education.</li>
</ul>
 <img title="chromeforcause" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/12/chromeforcause.jpg" alt="" width="400" height="295" />

Here is what your new tabs will do:
<ul>
	<li>10 tabs = 1 tree planted</li>
	<li>10 tabs = 1 book published and donated</li>
	<li>25 tabs = 1 vaccination treatment provided</li>
	<li>100 tabs = 1 square foot of shelter built</li>
	<li>200 tabs = 1 person's clean water for a year</li>
</ul>
You need to <a href="http://google.com/chrome" target="_blank">download Google Chrome</a> (you must anyway!) and the <a href="http://www.google.com/chrome/intl/en/p/cause/" target="_blank">Chrome for Cause extension</a> and browse as usual. The, only, catch is that you only get to do this from Dec 15-19. So, Hurry up and download the extension now!

Being the cynical people we are, we suspect this is just a way to mix charity with the mega Chrome advertising campaign Google is running these days. Anyway it's not a problem, Chrome is already our preferred browser.
