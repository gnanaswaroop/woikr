<p>A new startup in the already crowded Income Tax e-filing game in India is here to stay. <a href="http://cleartax.in/">clearTax</a> claims that it is the fastest way to e-file your taxes in India. So does everyone else. The key differentiator here though is that it is free. Whereas other players charge around INR 150 per form 16 to e-file your returns, clearTax is offering the service for free. The only thing you have to pay for is any consulting services you might need for help with tax savings.</p>
<p>Convinced? Hop on to <a href="http://cleartax.in/">clearTax.in</a> to start. Though we still believe that its super easy to do this on your own using the excel based software from <a href="https://incometaxindiaefiling.gov.in/portal/index.do">incometaxindiaefiling.gov.in</a>.</p>
