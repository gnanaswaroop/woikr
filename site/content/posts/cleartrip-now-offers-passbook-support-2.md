<p><a href="http://www.cleartrip.com/">Cleartrip</a> is famous for its neat and simple, yet amazingly powerful UI. Oh, and seamless ticket bookings as well :). When it comes to user experience, cleartrip clearly is one of the best case studies. They are always on their toes to keep innovating and setting new standards.</p>
<p>Keeping with the tradition, cleartrip is now supporting Apple's passbook format for all your bookings. Users can now send their bookings to their iPhones &amp; iPod touches (with iOS 6 or above) and keep all the tickets, and passes handy. <a href="http://www.apple.com/ios/whats-new/#passbook">Apple's passbook</a> is a nifty app that serves as a digital folder for all such things. Think of it as the digital equivalent of your document kit. Learn more about it <a href="http://www.apple.com/ios/whats-new/#passbook">here</a>.</p>
<p><img title="cleartrip-passbook.png" alt="cleartrip passbook" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2013/01/cleartrip-passbook.png" width="550" height="232" /></p>
<p>Unfortunately, airports in India currently do not support paperless boarding passes. So you will need to collect one from a counter. However, the pass should work as a paperless ticket, which, apparently, the airports are beginning to accept now. Pretty good move by Cleartrip.</p>
