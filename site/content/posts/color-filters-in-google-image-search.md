<p>Google has added color filters to their image search results. This means you can now select a color to further narrow your image search.</p>
<p>Let's say you want to search for a yellow <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dahlia" target="_blank">Dahlia</a>. A normal search for Dahlia in the image search box will give you the following results page (click to enlarge):</p>

<img class="alignnone size-medium wp-image-2446" title="normal_search_dahlia" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/04/normal_search_dahlia-300x272.png" alt="normal_search_dahlia" width="300" height="272" />

Now click on the colors drop down and select yellow. Voila! this is what you get (click to enlarge):

<img class="alignnone size-medium wp-image-2447" title="search_dahlia_yellow_filter" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/04/search_dahlia_yellow_filter-300x260.png" alt="search_dahlia_yellow_filter" width="300" height="260" />

Cool eh? Head over to <a href="http://images.google.com/" target="_blank">http://images.google.com/</a> to try it out.
