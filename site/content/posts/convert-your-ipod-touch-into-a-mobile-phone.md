<a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/07/image28.png"><img title="image" height="164" alt="image" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/07/image_thumb24.png" width="244" /></a>  <p>Currently a working prototype, the Apple Peel 520 is a case which contains all the electronics necessary for communicating with a GSM network.</p>  <p>The electronics include a SIM card slot, Infineon baseband chip and a battery, all of which will connect to the iPod with the dock port. The whole setup will involve jailbroken apps to be installed to make and receive phone calls and messages.</p>  <p>This thing is far from commercial production as of now, however, if it were ever to emerge in the market, it will probably cost around $44 - $74.</p>  <p>[<a href="http://www.engadget.com/2010/07/28/ipod-touch-turned-into-a-phone-using-yosions-apple-peel-520/">Engadget</a> via <a href="http://9to5mac.com/call_me_happy">9to5mac</a>]</p>
