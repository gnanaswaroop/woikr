<p>Most of the phones and some computers releasing now contain <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solid-state_drive" target="_blank">flash memory/SSD</a>. Corsair, as <a href="http://www.corsair.com/pressrelease/corsair-to-preview-force-series-gt-ssds-at-cebit" target="_blank">earlier announced</a> has previewed a new line&#160;&#160; of SSDs, the Corsair Force GT which is an ultra fast (world’s fastest as of now) 240GB SSD which can do read/writes at up to 500MB per second (has native SATA3 support too).</p>  <p><strong><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/03/corsairforcegtssd240GB500mbps.jpg"><img title="corsair-force-gt-ssd-240GB-500mbps" alt="corsair-force-gt-ssd-240GB-500mbps" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/03/corsairforcegtssd240GB500mbps_thumb.jpg" width="194" height="242" /></a> </strong></p><p><strong></strong></p>  <p>This is faster than their <a href="http://www.corsair.com/solid-state-drives/performance-3-series.html" target="_blank">Performance 3 Series</a> and twice as fast as their previous <a href="http://www.corsair.com/solid-state-drives/force-series.html" target="_blank">Force SSD series</a> which read/write do up to 280Mbps. These will be available by Q2 2011.</p>  <p><strong>What does fast SSD mean?</strong>    <br />In most applications, the I/O operations (input/output) hit the performance and slows the application down (ever wondered why those applications using a database or slow?). With faster I/O operations – your computer's operating system and other applications which use the disk to persist data will become faster/snappier.</p>  <p>Let the SSD race begin, my next computer (in 2012) hopefully won’t have any hard drive.</p>  <p>[Via <a href="http://www.engadget.com/2011/03/04/behold-corsairs-force-gt-ssd-flaming-red-shell-and-500-mb-s/" target="_blank">Engadget</a>]</p>
