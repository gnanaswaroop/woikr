<p>Looks like the ecommerce arena in India is getting more and more players everyday. <a href="/internet/amazon-entering-india-soon/">After amazon</a>, croma is planning to open an online store in India. Croma is Tata's mega electronics retail chain which operates retail stores in major cities across India. Croma has been setting <a href="/wtf/reliance-digital-vs-croma/">high standards</a> in electronics retail in India and we hope that they continue the same in their online business.</p>
<p>India desperately needs to have more major players in online retail. Currently there are not many major players in this business. More online stores will mean more competition which will lead to cheaper electronics for consumers.</p>
