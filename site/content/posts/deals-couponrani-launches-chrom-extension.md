<p>Indian coupon codes site <a href="http://www.couponrani.com">couponrani.com</a> has launched a <a href="https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/india-discount-coupons-by/imcheapjllkgajfmpdmnggnfmngoaepj">Google Chrome extension</a> which suggests the best coupons to apply to your cart when you are on the cart page of any supported eCommerce site. Couponrani supports most majors online retailers in India and has a huge collection os coupons. Before this, users had to visit the site, search for coupons and try every coupon to figure out which one is the best for them.</p>
<p><img title="couponrani-chrome-extension.png" src="/wp-content/uploads/2013/01/couponrani-chrome-extension.png" alt="couponrani chrome extension" width="600" height="375"></p>
<p>The extension can be installed from <a href="https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/india-discount-coupons-by/imcheapjllkgajfmpdmnggnfmngoaepj">here</a>&nbsp;on Google Chrome.</p>
<p>Another interesting plugin for Chrome on similar lines is <a href="https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/makkhichoose/gllmlkidgbagkcikijiljllpdloelocn">Makkhichoose</a>, which shows you the price of the product you are currently browsing on other online retailers in India. No need to open multiple tabs at once.</p>
