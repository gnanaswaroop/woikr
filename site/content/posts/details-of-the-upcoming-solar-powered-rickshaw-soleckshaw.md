<p>The Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (<a href="http://www.csir.res.in/" target="_blank">CSIR</a>), New Delhi (don’t bother clicking through, their site sucks) have come up with an eco friendly solar powered rickshaw for India.</p>
<p></p>
<p>There are 8 million rickshaw pullers in India and the Soleckshaw is aimed at reducing their plight.  The rickshaw will be driven partly by pedal and partly by electric power supplied by a battery that is charged from solar energy. These solar powered rickshaws are expected to serve as a carbon free alternative to manual rickshaws. Currently, the only upgrade from manual rickshaws is the auto rickshaw which runs on fossil fuels and is not eco friendly.</p>
