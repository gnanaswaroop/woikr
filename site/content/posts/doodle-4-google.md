<p>Everyone knows about Google's doodles that appear on the Search Homepage based on the day it is. Google is running a contest that allows school kids from all over India to submit their own doodles on the theme "My India". Voting is open till October 31st. The winner's doodle will be posted on Google India homepage on 14th November, 2009.</p>

Go ahead and <a href="http://www.google.com/intl/en_in/doodle4google/vote.html" target="_blank">vote</a>. You'll be surprised at the amazing display of creativity. I am.
