<p>Doodle Jump set record sales when it was launched on the app store for the iPhone. When the iPad was launched, even though the game ran pretty well in emulation mode, people demanded the developers to launch a native iPad version which utilizes the large screen of the iPad. After more than a year of waiting, its finally here.</p>
<p><img title="doodle-jump-ipad.png" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/09/doodle-jump-ipad.png" alt="doodle jump ipad" width="360" height="480" /></p>
<p>The insanely addictive game is available for <a href="http://itunes.apple.com/us/app/doodle-jump-for-ipad/id457687954?mt=8">$2.99 on the App Store</a>.</p>
