<p>Have you ever wondered how people become CTOs and Chief Architects at 25? Did you know there are multiple companies developing exciting Android/iOS games in India? Would you like to work in modern technologies and programming languages like Node.js, Ruby on Rails, Python/Django, Scala, Clojure and MongoDB? Do you wish to be part of a  company that grows from $10 to $1 Billion right in from of your eyes?</p>
<p>If this excites you, <a href="http://dreamstarts.in" target="_blank">DreamStarts.in</a> can help you in shaping up your career.</p>
<p>DreamStarts brings various jobs from a wide range of emerging Indian Startups. DreamStarts.in is a channel between Startups or Talents. It’s a Job Portal/Job Board/Opportunities book where Startups post job vacancies and potential employees (Talents) apply to the jobs, which is absolutely free. Startups can explore their ideas, thoughts, services, achievements and vision to the emerging market.</p>
<p><strong>What makes DreamStarts different than other job sites in India?</strong></p>

<ul>
	<li>Talents can find jobs offered by emerging and exciting Startups.</li>
	<li>You don't need to waste time in creating a profile.</li>
	<li>Talents can apply online and post resume to the employers directly.</li>
	<li>We do not collect resumes. We provide a platform where talents can directly contact to Startups.</li>
	<li>This service is absolutely free for both Startups and Talents.</li>
	<li>We do not display company’s contact information without prior permission.</li>
	<li>Startups can find talents without any involvement of third-party recruiting agency.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>What’s in it for the Talent?</strong></p>
<p>The jobs on <a href="http://www.dreamstarts.in/">DreamStarts</a> offered by startups are not just your everyday office job. These jobs will test your skills and creativity, lead to all sorts of opportunities and often come with a decent sized pay.</p>
<p>Candidates can apply to these jobs without creating login/password and this service is absolutely free. Candidate can also search city wise jobs.</p>

<p><strong>What’s in it for the Startups?</strong></p>
<p>Startups are not like other companies, so why hire people the way other companies do? Why do startups need to pay big bucks to recruiting firms? With <strong>DreamStarts.in</strong>, you can post your jobs for free and talents can reach to you directly. Startups can post any number of job advertisements and receive applications from skilled, experienced and best suitable talents.</p>
<p>Startups can also mail their job requirements to DreamStarts @ <a href="mailto:info@dreamstarts.in">info@dreamstarts.in</a> which will be published on board.</p>
<p>DreamStarts also features Startups in <strong><a href="http://www.dreamstarts.in/Indian-Startups/">Indian-Startups section</a></strong> and provides them unique URL according to their name. e.g. <strong>http://dreamstarts.in/company-name</strong> in simple English it’s “The Dream Starts in ‘company-name’”.</p>
