<p>ebay launched its iPhone app long back. However, users in India could not use it as it did not support the Indian store (ebay.in). Not anymore! ebay India's Country Manager announced in a press release that the app now supports purchasing stuff from the India store in addition to several other country specific stores. I checked and could indeed see India as the option under settings. Awesome.</p>
<p>So all of you ebay fans, buyers or sellers, get your iPhones and make a sale/purchase on the move! The app is available <a href="http://itunes.apple.com/us/app/ebay-mobile/id282614216?mt=8">for free on the App Store</a>.</p>
