<p>Ever been drunk and posted something embarrassing on Facebook or twitter that you regretted later and wished you could undo it? Well, there is an app for that!</p>
<p><a href="http://lastnightapp.com/">Last night never happened</a> is an app for such occasions and lets you delete any Facebook or twitter messages that you might have sent while you were high.</p><p>The app currently only works with Facebook and Twitter and presents you with an intuitive UI to let you delete all those embarrassing messages that you might have sent under the influence of alcohol.</p>
