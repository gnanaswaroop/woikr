<p>They designed these Spy Cams to withstand freezing cold weather (as low as -40° C) in the Arctic Islands of Svalbard. They made smart mechanisms like "Blizzard Cam", "Snow Cap Cam" and "Ice Bear Cam" to keep them disguised while they try to get as close as possible to the Polar Bears for BBC's docmentary "Polar Bear: Spy on the Ice". The Result? The curious Polar Bears probed and poked and scrutinized the Spy Cams till they found a way to break them all. Anyhow, BBC managed to get some interesting footage. A small 2 minute preview ahead.</p>
