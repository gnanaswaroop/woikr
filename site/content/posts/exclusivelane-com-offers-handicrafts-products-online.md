<p><a href="http://www.exclusivelane.com">Exclusivelane.com</a> is a Noida based startup which offers authentic handicrafts for sale online. The site is one of a kind in India and aims to provide quality products with fantastic customer service.</p>
<p>Exclusivelane is also planning to start 24 hour delivery service within Delhi and will give you the products for free if they are not able to meet this deadline. This will also be first of a kind service by an ecommerce company in India.</p>

<p>The online store offers a good collection of handicrafts in various categories in all price ranges. One can argue that the same item can probably be bought at a cheaper price in the market, however, exclusivelane.com will offer convenience to those who cannot (or do not want to) go to such markets. Plus, you always have an option to return any defective product without any hassle.</p>
<p>The site is offering free shipping across India and flat 15% off if you <a href="http://www.exclusivelane.com/index.php/customer/account/create/">sign up now</a>.</p>
