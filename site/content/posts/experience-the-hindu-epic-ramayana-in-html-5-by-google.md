<p>Google Indonesia team, as part of <a href="http://www.chromeexperiments.com/">Chrome experiments</a> project, has launched the <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramayana">Hindu Epic Ramayana</a> in HTML5. The story is same as the Ramayana, except that the characters use modern tools like Google maps, Google Talk, Docs, Gmail etc. to plan their strategy. Pretty cool eh? Jatayu also writes a blog!</p>
<p>Its a brilliant creative work in my opinion. The site currently only works properly in the latest version of Chrome. It is completely in Bahasa language, however, it is easily understandable.</p>
<p>It is totally worth checking it out. The site is live at <a href="http://www.ramaya.na/">ramaya.na</a></p>
