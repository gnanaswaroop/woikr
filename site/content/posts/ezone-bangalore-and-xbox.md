Since last week I've had this undying urge to buy a game console. I've been reading reviews watching game trailers and raiding <a href="http://www.consoul.in/forum/index.php" target="_blank">forums</a> to find out the perfect fit for my requirement. Based on games/price/graphics I chose XBox 360. Good job Microsoft. (You can't imagine how surprised I am to say that!)

Anyway, since a week or so, Microsoft and some big retail chains (Landmark, Croma, e-Zone etc) have been running a Diwali offer on the XBox 360 core. You get a free 20 GB HDD (Microsoft charges 5.5k for this!) and a cool wireless controller free with the normal package. I checked with e-Zone for this on Thursday and they said that the offer will run for a week more and I can come and buy on weekend.

Come Saturday and I visited the e-Zone Koramangala all excited to get my new XBox.
<blockquote><strong>Me</strong>: (Playing around with the XBox demo) "Do you have the Diwali offer right now?"<br>
<strong>Sales Guy 1</strong>: "Yes Sir, We have the Diwali offer with us, it's a great offer, you should buy."<br>
<strong>Me</strong>: "Ok, then get me one."<br>
<strong>Sales Guy 1</strong>: (looking surprised) "What, Sir? "<br>
<strong>Me</strong>: "I want to buy an XBox Core model. Please get me a box."<br>
<strong>Sales Guy 1</strong>: "Oh ok sir, I'll see." (Looks here and there finds a few empty boxes saying XBox Diwali offer and runs to another guy)<br>
<strong>Sales Guy 2</strong>: (Comes and looks at the same boxes and starts whispering to the first guy)<br>
<strong>Me</strong>: (Is this the first time anyone has asked for an XBox!)<br>
<strong>Sales Guy 1 &amp; 2</strong>: (Call another guy who comes and starts playing on the XBox demo and whispers something to the other 2)<br>
<strong>Sales Guy 2</strong>: "Sir, wait for a few minutes, we'll get the XBox from inside."<br>
<strong>Me</strong>: (Finally!)<br>
(After some 5 minutes)<br>
<strong>Sales Guy 2</strong>: "Sorry Sir, we are out of stock."<br>
<strong>Me</strong>: (WTF!) "Not even 1 box, you said that you have the Diwali offer?"<br>
<strong>Random Sales Guy 4</strong>: "Sorry Sir, we'll get stocks next week."<br>
<strong>Me</strong>: "Will they have this offer?"<br>
<strong>Sales Guy 2</strong>: "Yes Sir."<br>
<strong>Random Sales Guy 4</strong>: "No Sir."<br>
<strong>Me</strong>: (Crying over my pathetic situation) "Yeah right, I get it."</blockquote>
And, I left the store without an XBox cursing my lucky stars. Now it turns out the offer is officially over in all other places (Landmark, Croma etc) but e-Zone guys have been extra lazy not to remove the offer banners or even check their inventory!

<strong>UPDATE:</strong>

I finally got the XBox 360 Diwali offer from HomeTown in Marathahalli. Wait for new XBox related woiks coming soon!
