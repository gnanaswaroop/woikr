<p>As much hyped, Facebook announced and showed how they believe next generation would want their social communication. So here's our take on what it is and if we’d really use it.</p>
<p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/11/Facebookmessages.png"><img title="Facebook-messages" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/11/Facebookmessages_thumb.png" alt="Facebook-messages" width="483" height="89" /></a></p>
<p>The features were inline with what we've <a href="/apple/announcements-galore-itunes-facebook-mail-what-to-expect" target="_blank">predicted before the event</a>.<br>
Here are some of the cool things it brings to Facebook 'revamped' messaging’</p>

<ul>
	<li>
<div>A new email address. You'll get a @FaceBook.com email address. And you can’t customize your handle, it'll be your facebook username by default. Thanks facebook – I'll now get a ton of spam (in case you don't manage it well) as Facebook profile handle is public.</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Unified Inbox – I know for a while, Google's been saying the same and has quite <a href="/google/voice-and-video-chat-from-within-gmail" target="_blank">achieved</a> it partially (atleast <a href="/google/call-phones-from-gmail" target="_blank">the way</a> we love it). We've even posted a wish list for <a href="/misc/wish-list-for-the-google-new-social-network" target="_blank">Google's new social network</a>. So here Facebook shows an unified inbox with chat/messages and Facebook mails all unified together to be a single timeline.</div></li>
	<li>
<div>A person-centric conversation history – This means if you open up your Girlfriend's profile – you can see all the love and hate messages you sent to her together (Why did you store them in the first place!!). Keep them private :)</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Priority Inbox – With facebook not allowing customization of email handles. There comes a problem of SPAM. So this solves the same purpose as of Google’s priority inbox – You don't want spam or never-emailed-me-before kind of a friend's message to be the first unread. For most of us who check our mail frequently – It's just the SPAM we're worried about.</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Others – An awesome simple UI to do this. Although Joel Seligstein, The Engineering Manager for the Facebook’s new messages product claims that <a href="http://techcrunch.com/2010/11/15/inside-the-war-room-answering-the-questions-behind-facebook-messages/" target="_blank">it'll take a while for every one to get used to</a> its awesome'ness (Do u think we're dumb dude?).</div></li>
</ul>
<p>But we do agree with him that it'll be the college folks that'll adopt it first who wish to have immediate communication or a painless way to communicate. But we don't think email is going away till this 'next-gen email-ution' continues. We of course <a href="/google/good-bye-google-wave" target="_blank">loved</a> Google Wave and still believe it's how the over-lords would communicate.</p>
