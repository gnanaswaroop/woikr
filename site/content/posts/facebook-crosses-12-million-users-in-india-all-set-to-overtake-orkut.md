<p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/07/image16.png"><img title="image" height="195" alt="image" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/07/image_thumb12.png" width="194" /></a> </p>  <p>Everyone knows that Facebook recently crossed <a href="http://blog.facebook.com/blog.php?post=409753352130">500 million users worldwide</a>.&#160; What many might not know that Facebook also crossed 12 million users in India. At this rate, it will be able to overtake orkut very soon.</p>  <p>Facebook announced this milestone on the <a href="http://www.facebook.com/FacebookIndia">official Facebook India page</a>. As part of the celebration. Facebook has launched the <a href="http://stories.facebook.com/?ref=mf">Facebook-Stories</a> initiative where users can share their own stories about impact of Facebook on their lives.</p>  <p>Certainly a big number for India. however, when we look at it from a population penetration perspective, it is just close to 1%.</p>  <p>[via <a href="http://www.facebook.com/FacebookIndia">Facebook India</a> &amp; <a href="http://www.pluggd.in/facebook-userbase-in-india-297/?utm_source=feedburner&amp;utm_medium=feed&amp;utm_campaign=Feed%3A+pluggd+%28pluGGd.in%29&amp;utm_content=Google+Reader">PI</a>]</p>
