<p>Facebook has filed for an IPO of US$ 5 billion. The whole process is expected to make many Facebook employees millionaires.</p>
<p>In his letter to the SEC, Mark Zuckerberg states that Facebook was not created to be a company, but to serve the "social mission" of making the world more open and connected and that they hope to strengthen how people relate to each other.</p>
<p>Facebook has more than 800 million active users and caters to 2.7 billion likes &amp; comments every day. It is also the most popular photo sharing site on the web with 250 million photos uploaded everyday.</p>
