This is probably the coolest thing I might have seen in recent times. Rubber stamps (you know, the kind of stamps used by government officials) are now available in the form of Facebook Like &amp; Unlike buttons. Imagine that you submit an application to a government agency and when its accepted, you get a copy with the “Like” button stamped. Cool eh?
