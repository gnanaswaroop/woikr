<p>For those already not using a <a href="/misc/use-facebook-places-in-india" target="_blank">hack to get Facebook Places</a> to work in India or other un-supporting countries, Facebook today silently rolled-out &quot;Places&quot; check-ins in India.</p>  <p><img src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/10/FacebookPlaces.png" /> </p>  <p>This feature was actually available to me since like a week and more users are discovering this now which could mean a phased rollout by Facebook. </p>   <p>At the moment, it appears to work only for a few Android users while others are still seeing the error that the feature isn’t available in the region.
