<p>While it's already been the #2 website on Alexa for long, Facebook has finally overtaken Yahoo to become the third most popular destination on the web. As per comScore's data, in the last month (November 2010) Facebook had an estimated 648 million unique visitors from around the world compared to Yahoo's 630 million. Google and Microsoft stood at 970 million and 869 million respectively. In the US, Yahoo is still #1 whereas Facebook stands at #4.</p>
