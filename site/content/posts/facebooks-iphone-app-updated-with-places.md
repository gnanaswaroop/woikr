<p>Facebook totally wants to get in the location game with Places. Facebook Places is a Foursquare like feature in which you can check in a place on Facebook if you are physically present at that place. For example, if you are in your neighborhood comic book store, you can check in to it on Facebook so that your friends can know where you are. You can also tag your friends if they are present there.</p>

<div></div>
<p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/08/image48.png"><img title="image" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/08/image_thumb24.png" alt="image" width="125" height="240" /></a></p>
<p>Previously, the feature was only available on <a href="http://touch.facebook.com" target="_blank">touch.facebook.com</a> for the iPhone and iPad. Facebook has updated its <a href="http://itunes.apple.com/us/app/facebook/id284882215?mt=8" target="_blank">iPhone app</a> to version 3.2 (there is still no official iPad app yet) to include the Places feature. This means you can use the updated Facebook app on your iPhone or iPad to use the feature.</p>
