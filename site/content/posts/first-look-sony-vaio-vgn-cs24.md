<p>The laptop is targeted at students and home users. Clocking at 2 GHz with an ample amount of RAM, this laptop should be able to handle our daily surfing and multimedia tasks with ease. Students should be able to work seamlessly on MS Office and their school projects. The 250 GB HDD should be sufficient for those photos and media. Need more? get a portable USB hard drive.</p>

<a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/05/vaio-cs-24-black.jpg"><img title="vaio_cs_24_black" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/05/vaio-cs-24-black-thumb.jpg" alt="vaio_cs_24_black" width="494" height="358" /></a>

This is not a high end machine, however, the good amount of RAM and a sound processor should be enough to run heavy applications like Adobe Photoshop, Flash etc. it will also be able to do your seldom video compression tasks. I wouldn’t count on this as a serious <a href="/tag/gaming/">gaming</a> machine, owing to the standard graphics, however, you should be able to play most games at medium or low settings. Although, Counter Strike, AOE etc. should run pretty smoothly on this.
<p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/05/vaio-showcase.png"><img title="vaio_showcase" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/05/vaio-showcase-thumb.png" alt="vaio_showcase" width="534" height="136" /></a></p>
<p>A screen resolution of1280 x 800 should be enough for document editing, playing games and watching movies. The isolated (Macbook type) keyboard makes typing easier and faster. The wireless card supports “802.11 Draft n” networks which means that it will be compatible with the upcoming high speed wireless networks (802.11n).</p>
<p>The only downside is the bundled Windows Vista Home Basic OS. We would recommend installing Windows XP to leverage the full potential of the hardware.</p>

<a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/05/vaio-cs-colors.png"><img title="vaio_cs_colors" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/05/vaio-cs-colors-thumb.png" alt="vaio_cs_colors" width="331" height="120" /></a>
<p>With a decent battery life of 2.5 hours, this is a good choice for students and home users with casual multimedia needs. The laptop is available in 3 colors - Jet Black, Blazing Red &amp; Noble Brown. The MRP is INR 49,990, but you can get this for approx INR 46k at most retail outlets and probably for even lesser at Nehru Place.</p>
