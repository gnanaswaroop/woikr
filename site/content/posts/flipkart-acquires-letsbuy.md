<p><a href="http://www.pluggd.in/its-official-flipkart-acquires-letsbuy-pr-297/?utm_source=feedburner&amp;utm_medium=feed&amp;utm_campaign=Feed%3A+pluggd+%28pluGGd.in%29">PluggdIn is reporting</a> that India's leading ecommerce site flipkart.com has acquired letsbuy.com. Letsbuy was Flipkart's biggest competitor in the consumer electronics space. Flipkart has seen tremendous growth in online sales of books &amp; media sales. However, letsbuy has enjoyed a much better place in online sales of consumer electronics with a much larger catalog and all India shipping - things where flipkart falls back. With this acquisition, flipkart aims to become the leading online retailer of consumer electronics in India.</p>
<p>Flipkart + Letsbuy can be a winning combination if the resulting company has a catalogue as wide as letsbuy in electronics &amp; follows the processes of flipkart. Letsbuy has a sloppy delivery and customer service, whereas flipkart has an awesome self delivery and a customer service which actually listens to you and understands your issues. Together they have the potential to become a solid ecommerce player in India and give a healthy competition to any new player (domestic or foreign).</p>
<p>We will keep our fingers crossed.</p>
