<p>Mega online retailer Flipkart has launched an Android tablet in India. Launched under the Flipkart owned Digiflip Pro brand, the <a href="http://www.flipkart.com/digiflip-pro?otracker=hp_imgModule_digiflippro">XT712 tablet</a>, priced at INR 9,999 comes in two colors - black &amp; white and offers comparable specs to tablets fro HP, Lenovo &amp; Samsung. Here is a quick list of the tablet's configuration:</p>
<ul>
<li>1.3 Ghz QuadCore Processor</li>
<li>3G Dual Sim with voice calling support</li>
<li>1280 X 800 7 inch IPS screen</li>
<li>5MP rear camera with LED flash</li>
<li>2MP front camera</li>
<li>16GB internal storage, microSD upto 32 GB</li>
<li>Dimensions: 113.7 x 185.3 x 9.2 mm</li>
<li>Weight: 285g</li>
</ul>
<p><img title="flipkart tablet XT 712.png" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/flipkart-tablet-XT-712.png" alt="flipkart tablet XT 712" width="314" height="314" /></p>
<p>Flipkart is offering the following offers in order to promote the tablet’s sale in India:</p>
<ul>
<li>Discounts worth INR 5000 when shopping on flipkart using the app on the tablet (<a href="https://twitter.com/chupchap/status/482046400549498882">Conditions apply</a>)</li>
<li>Free eBooks worth INR 2300 (The eBooks are pre-selected by flipkart)</li>
<li>20% off on tablet’s book case</li>
</ul>
<p>The tablet seems like a good alternative to people looking for an affordable stock Android tablet. However, be aware that this tablet comes with Android Jellybean 4.2.2 which is super outdated (introduced in Feb 2013). Furthermore, the discount offers seem to have a big catch and might not be useful for most customers.</p>
