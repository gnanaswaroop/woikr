<p>Flipkart.com, one of India's biggest online retailer has launched an <a href="http://www.flipkart.com/ebooks">eBook store</a> under its flyte digital store strategy. Though a bit late than planned, the eBook store from flipkart offers around 64,000 eBooks at the time of launch.</p>
<p>The eBook store app is currently only available for Android based devices, however, flipkart is working on apps for other platforms. The <a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.flipkart.fkreader&amp;hl=en">Android app</a> enables users to purchase (using flipkart wallet), download and read eBooks in the app. Furthermore, users can download each eBook on up to 6 devices at once.</p>
<p>The catalog offers a lot of free eBooks which can be downloaded for free. For paid eBooks, users can download a free preview in order to decide whether to buy the book or not. The pricing of an eBook is marginally cheaper than its physical version.</p>
<p>Though the eBooks from flipkart are delivered in a proprietary format and cannot be read on eBook readers other than flipkart's own, it is a great move by flipkart and should be welcomed by bookworms in India. With the availability of apps across various other platforms, their eBook store should gain further popularity in India.</p>
