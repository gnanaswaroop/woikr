<p>Think before you buy that HP TouchPad for $99. <a href="http://lacs.co.in">Lakshmi Access Communications Systems Pvt. Ltd.</a>, a Delhi based firm has launched <a href="http://lacs.co.in/products/pepper-m74v.php">Magnum Pepper</a> - a INR 4500 (~$99) Android tablet in india. This might probably be the cheapest Android tablet in the world! The specs include:</p>
<ul>
<li>7 inch LCD sensitive touch display (800*480)</li>
<li>WIFI enabled</li>
<li>Supports 10/100 BASE-T Ethernet LAN (RJ45)</li>
<li>Support USB 2.0</li>
<li>Support Micro SD card (maximum 32 GB)</li>
<li>3G compatible dongle</li>
<li>3.5 mm stereo headset jack</li>
<li>G- sensors 360 degree</li>
<li>Front camera</li>
<li>Built in 2GB memory</li>
<li>Android 2.2 version</li>
<li>Supported audio formats eg: MP3,AAC,WMA,AAC+</li>
<li>Supported Video formats eg: 3GP, AVI, MP4, H.264,720P</li>
</ul>
<p>Impressive specs coupled with a very affordable price might make this the tablet to beat in India. With the launch of android based tablets from Bharti &amp; Reliance, looks like this segment has lot of potential for a price war in India.</p>
