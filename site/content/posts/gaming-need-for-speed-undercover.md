</strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">NFS is being developed by EA, which was among the first series of 3D racing games out there for  <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_video_game_consoles_(fifth_generation)" target="_blank">5th generation consoles</a> (or the better known 32bit gaming).</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>Why do we Need for Speed.. ?</strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">NFS gives us the power, the control for an enthusiastic gamer to excel and push his talents to a new high. One needs a good graphics card like the Ge Force 9x series to take these babies out for a spin.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><a href="/wp-content/uploads/2008/08/need_for_speed_undercover_021.jpg" rel="lightbox[204]"><img loading="lazy" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-231" title="NFS Undercover" src="/wp-content/uploads/2008/08/need_for_speed_undercover_021.jpg" alt="" width="436" height="245" srcset="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/08/need_for_speed_undercover_021.jpg 640w, /wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/08/need_for_speed_undercover_021-300x168.jpg 300w" sizes="(max-width: 436px) 100vw, 436px"></a></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><em><strong>What’s special about this one ?</strong></em></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">“Need for Speed  Undercover features a deep and engaging story of spectacular Hollywood-style live-action that will transport players into the fictional world of the Tri-City Bay Area,” said Bill Harrison, Need for Speed Undercover Executive Producer.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">As always, NFS brings out even better graphics with every release and this one which packages hot cop chases and the best cars out there to drive and trash is to hit the American stores on Nov 18, 2008. Keep your calendars marked all gamers.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">This is gonna be the hottest NFS game with action cut-scenes involving <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maggie_Q" target="_blank">Maggie Q</a>, who has starred in huge Hollywood movies like MI 3 and the recent Balls of Fury. 🙂</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><img loading="lazy" class="size-medium wp-image-219 alignnone" title="Maggie Q" src="/wp-content/uploads/2008/08/maggie_q_01-225x300.jpg" alt="" width="158" height="210" srcset="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/08/maggie_q_01-225x300.jpg 225w, /wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/08/maggie_q_01.jpg 400w" sizes="(max-width: 158px) 100vw, 158px"></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Now who wouldn’t want that ? Eh ?</p>
