<g:plusone size="medium"></g:plusone></li><li></li></ul><p style="text-align: justify;">The next version of Nintendo DS, the 3DS which offers glasses free 3D gaming has started <a href="http://www.nintendo.com/3ds/buy" target="_blank">pre-order</a> and will set you back by USD 249.99. Will be available in the US on March 27th 2011 and in Europe on March 25th 2011.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><a href="/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/Nintendo3DS.png" rel="lightbox[6479]"><img style="display: inline; border-width: 0px;" title="Nintendo 3DS" src="/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/Nintendo3DS_thumb.png" border="0" alt="Nintendo 3DS" width="297" height="272"></a></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><span id="more-6479"></span>Some of it’s unique features are</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>Dual Screens<br>
</strong>The widescreen display on the the top display resolution – 800×240&nbsp; and the touch screen input on the bottom operates on a 320×240 resolution. This’ll definitely shut the folks who say that they need custom UI/input buttons for playing games, other than the usual joystick keys.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><a href="/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/Nintendo3DS2.png" rel="lightbox[6479]"><img style="display: inline; border-width: 0px;" title="Nintendo 3DS-2" src="/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/Nintendo3DS2_thumb.png" border="0" alt="Nintendo 3DS-2" width="187" height="191"></a></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>3D Camera</strong>:<br>
This comes with 2 screens and 2 cameras to shoot 3D video from your handheld. Imagine playing a computer game where the input is taken from the video that’s getting recorded in front of you.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><a href="/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/Nintendo3DS3DCamera.jpg" rel="lightbox[6479]"><img style="display: inline; border-width: 0px;" title="Nintendo-3DS-3DCamera" src="/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/Nintendo3DS3DCamera_thumb.jpg" border="0" alt="Nintendo-3DS-3DCamera" width="129" height="132"></a></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The console comes in 2 colors only at the moment (Aqua Blue and Cosmo Black)</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><a href="/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/image10.png" rel="lightbox[6479]"><img style="display: inline; border: 0px;" title="image" src="/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/image_thumb3.png" border="0" alt="image" width="243" height="96"></a><br>
You can still play the existing Nintendo DS and DSi games on this gaming handheld in 2D.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Just like all the new phones, this also comes with a Motion Sensing Accelerometer and a Gyroscope for a 6 Axis movement input.</p>
