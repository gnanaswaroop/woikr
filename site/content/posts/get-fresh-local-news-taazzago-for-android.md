<p>There are so many ways to get the News fix on your Android phone. You can download the <a href="http://www.appbrain.com/app/ndtv-news/com.rp" target="_blank">NDTV app</a>, <a href="http://www.appbrain.com/app/ibnlive-for-android/com.divum.ibn" target="_blank">IBN Live app</a>, one of many unofficial newspaper apps; you can subscribe to the popular News feeds; there is Google News for India and you can always visit the <a href="http://m.timesofindia.com/" target="_blank">various</a> <a href="http://www.dnaindia.com/mobile/index.php" target="_blank">newspaper</a> <a href="http://m.rediff.com/news/headlines" target="_blank">websites</a>. But <a href="http://taazza.com/" target="_blank">Taazza</a> wants to provide you with a better and more localized experience of News for many locations in India. Ahead is a small description of what they do in their words.</p>

<blockquote>Taazza is about getting you closer to the news story that matters to you. We bring this fresh perspective by connecting you  to three important aspects of news:
<ul>
	<li>Who wrote the story? (Author)</li>
	<li>Where is the story relevant? (Location)</li>
	<li>What is it talking about? (Topic)</li>
</ul>
How we are different?
<ul>
	<li>Cutting edge NLP &amp; classification algorithms</li>
	<li>Popular newspapers, citizen journals &amp; niche neighborhood papers</li>
	<li>Easy content API for developers to build on</li>
</ul>
</blockquote>
<p>We downloaded the app for a quick review. See the gallery below for all the images.</p>
<p>The app offers to calculate or select a location according to which the searching will be done. After the boot screen we see the following tabs:</p>

<ul>
	<li><strong>City News</strong><br>
Gathers news and deals from the city and displays the headlines collected from various newspapers/websites. As you click on the news Taazza opens up a page with a smartly selected category, location of the news on the map and Share (Email/Facebook/Twitter) and Save (Instapaper) options. The "Read More" option opens up the source website within the app. <strong></strong></li>
	<li><strong>Nearby</strong><br>
Displays the news articles nearest to the selected location along with the approximate distance. The details are similar to City News. <strong></strong></li>
	<li><strong>Deals</strong><br>
Gathers various Deals near you. The detail page contains the Overview, Details, Outlet location, Reviews and the deal details. Pressing the BUY button allows you to call a phone number or visit a link to buy the deal.</li>
	<li><strong>Cities<br>
</strong>Shows the complete list of cities and locations the app supports. You can browse to view the news/deals in any location.</li>
</ul>
<p>The other sections worth noticing are:</p>

<ul>
	<li><strong>Settings</strong><br>
The page that allows you to connect to Facebook, Twitter and enter Instapaper credentials.</li>
	<li><strong>Map</strong><br>
Shows all the news on a neat map interface.<strong> </strong></li>
	<li><strong>Feedback</strong><br>
Allows you to repost and issue and more importantly suggest your favorite source of news.<strong> </strong></li>
</ul>
<p><strong>What we liked</strong></p>

<ul>
	<li>Once you select the location, the gathered news articles are pretty good.</li>
	<li>Categorization of news is good but not used optimally. Users should be allowed to browse the news by category.</li>
	<li>Except the buying process, the Deals interface is excellent.</li>
	<li>The Map interface is slick and innovative.</li>
	<li>Weather icon shown on the Top-Right corner is interesting but not detailed enough. Current temperature and a link to the detailed weather will be great.</li>
	<li>Integration with Social Networks is good.</li>
	<li>Right after we posted the review the developers informed us that the new release is getting ready with the fixes to the issues we pointed out. Good job guys!</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>What needs improvements</strong></p>

<ul>
	<li>The intermediate pages feel unnecessary.</li>
	<li>No option to read news in browser. It's helpful as you can open multiple tabs and someone may want to render the article in a different browser like Opera Mini.</li>
	<li>The app is somewhat slow and not really polished. While loading different tabs, the UI seems to hang.</li>
</ul>
The app is definitely worth a download but we'd wait for the next version to decide how long it stays on the phone.
