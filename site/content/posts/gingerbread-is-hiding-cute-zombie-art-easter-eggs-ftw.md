<p>Are you one of those few who have an Android phone running Android 2.3 Gingerbread? Here is a cute easter egg that XDA member teknoraver <a href="http://forum.xda-developers.com/showthread.php?t=975490" target="_blank">found</a>.</p>
<p>Goto Settings-&gt;About Phone and scroll down to the bottom of the list. Now tap "Android version" column repeatedly. It may take some time (took me some 50-60 taps) but keep tapping swiftly and it will eventually work and you'll see a cute Zombie painting by <a href="http://www.facebook.com/zombieart" target="_blank">Jack Larson</a> as shown in the screen below.</p>
