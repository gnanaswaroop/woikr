<p>Gmail now lets you paste images from your clipboard directly in the compose window in the browser. They previously enabled <a href="http://gmailblog.blogspot.com/2010/05/drag-images-into-messages.html">dragging and dropping of images</a> in the compose window, but with this new feature, you can just take a snapshot of your screen and then paste it in the email message without the need of saving it in a file. Pretty neat.</p>
<p>The feature currently works only in Chrome, but they are probably working on to enable it in other browsers too.</p>
