<p><img title="image" alt="image" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/12/image.png" width="240" height="189" /> </p>  <p>Google and Samsung are all set to launch the successor of Nexus one, the <a href="http://www.google.com/nexus" target="_blank">Nexus S</a> in the US. Nexus S is the next Google branded smartphone which is manufactured by Samsung and will be available both unlocked and with a contract with T mobile in the US starting Dec 16, 2010. <strike>No word on pricing yet.</strike> <strong>UPDATE</strong>: The Nexus S will go for $199 with a contract and $529 for the unlocked version at Best Buy.</p>   <p>The nexus S is very similar to the bestselling <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samsung_Galaxy_S" target="_blank">Samsung Galaxy S</a> and offers butt loads of improvements over the nexus one. In addition to being much more sleek and thinner, it boasts of the following features:</p>  <ul>   <li>     <div><a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gingerbread" target="_blank">Gingerbread</a> – the latest iteration of the Android OS pre installed without any crappy UI layer</div>   </li>    <li>     <div>1 GHz Hummingbird processor</div>   </li>    <li>     <div>16GB internal memory</div>   </li>    <li>     <div>5 megapixel rear and VGA front facing camera</div>   </li>    <li>     <div>4’’ contour display with 480x800 resolution</div>   </li>    <li>     <div>Wi-Fi 802.11 n/b/g</div>   </li>    <li>     <div>Native VOIP/SIP support</div>   </li>    <li>     <div>Up to 6.7 hours of talk time over 3G</div>   </li>    <li>     <div>Portable WiFi hotspot for up to 6 devices</div>   </li> </ul>  <p>Surprisingly, there is no SD card slot in the phone which means you cannot expand the memory beyond 16GB.</p>  <p>Google and Samsung handed out a few review pieces to some popular tech sites and their initial reviews are out.
