<p style="text-align: justify;">We all know that mapping the world is not easy. Google has done a great job in developed countries but collaborating with the authorities. However, for developing countries like India, where establishments and points of interests change as frequently as every 6 months, it is difficult to just rely on the government authorities to provide accurate up to date data.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><img title="google-mapmaker.png" alt="google mapmaker" src="/wp-content/uploads/2013/02/google-mapmaker.png" width="480" height="245" border="0"></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">In an effort to enhance the Google Maps data for India, Google India is running a Mapathon where anyone with a Google account can improve the Google maps data for their neighborhood or places they are familiar with. Google India is also offering attractive prizes for people like&nbsp;10 Samsung Galaxy Note 800 Android Tablets,&nbsp;40 Samsung Galaxy S II GT I9100 Android Phones,&nbsp;50 Flipkart gift coupons worth Rs. 5000 each&nbsp;and 1000 Google Mapathon 2013 t-shirts and certificates (includes the top 100 above and another 900 people).</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">In order to participate in the effort, log on to <a href="https://www.google.com/mapmaker/mapathon/india2013/">Google India Mapathon 2013 microsite</a> and enroll yourself. Thereafter, any improvements you make in <a href="http://www.google.com/mapmaker">Google Mapmaker</a> will count towards the effort.</p>
