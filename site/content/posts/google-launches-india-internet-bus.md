<p>To spread awareness among the common Indian population about the benefits of Internet and Google products, Google India launched its India Internet Bus project in Tamil Nadu.</p>
<p>The Bus will follow a fixed route across Tamil Nadu and will demostrate the benefits of Internet (specifically Google tools) in simplifying day to day tasks using Google Search, Gmail, Google Maps and more. According to Google:</p>

<blockquote>The Internet Bus Project is an attempt to educate people about what the Internet is, and how it may be beneficial to their lives, by taking the Internet experience to them through a customised Internet-enabled bus, which will travel to several towns and cities across India.</blockquote>
