<p>Well, I am not really breaking any news here. Just for those folks who might've missed this. </p>  <p>Google Maps now shows Live traffic information. This is a sign of relief for Indian users who were only relying on <a href="http://www.waze.com/" target="_blank">Waze</a> at the moment. The traffic data isn't too accurate at the moment. But as we all know the more the users the more the accuracy (it's crowd sourced).</p>  <p>The UI is very intuitive and this traffic information is also available on the Google Maps applications on Android and iOS (probably on other OS with traffic layer support too). </p>  <p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2012/09/Photo-11-09-12-11-42-38-AM.png"><img title="Photo 11-09-12 11 42 38 AM" alt="Photo 11-09-12 11 42 38 AM" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2012/09/Photo-11-09-12-11-42-38-AM_thumb.png" width="300" height="450" /></a>&#160;</p><p>The same information is also available on the Google Maps on your regular browser too, ensure to select the &quot;Traffic&quot; layer on the top right. This should be incredibly useful not just to the users but also to the local Traffic Police too. </p>  <p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2012/09/image5.png"><img title="image" alt="image" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2012/09/image_thumb5.png" width="574" height="366" /></a>&#160;</p>  <p>If your phone doesn't have a Google Maps installable app (or has a really old one), the Google Maps Mobile website provides this information too. So launch up your mobile browsers for this. </p>  <p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2012/09/Photo-11-09-12-11-59-43-AM.png"><img title="Photo 11-09-12 11 59 43 AM" alt="Photo 11-09-12 11 59 43 AM" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2012/09/Photo-11-09-12-11-59-43-AM_thumb.png" width="300" height="450" /></a></p>  <p>I am still gonna be using both Waze and the <a href="http://www.google.com/mobile/maps/" target="_blank">Google Maps mobile application</a> to contribute to the <a href="http://googlesystem.blogspot.com/2009/08/google-maps-mobile-users-send-traffic.html" target="_blank">crowd-sourced traffic</a> information.&#160; I still hope to see <a href="/google/turn-by-turn-voice-navigation-now-available-in-google-maps-in-india/" target="_blank">voice directions for India</a> on iOS for this to be completely useful. </p>  <p>Who wouldn't want their phone to shout out &quot;<em>Heavy traffic ahead, take evasive actions immediately</em>&quot;?</p>  <p>The best mapping service (accuracy &amp; usability) in India has to be Google Maps and this&#160; puts Google much ahead of Apple Maps (Starting iOS6) in India. Hope to see a stand-alone unified Google Maps Application on iOS soon. </p>
