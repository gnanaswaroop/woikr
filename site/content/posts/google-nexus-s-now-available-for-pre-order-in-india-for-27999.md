<p>Good news for all of you waiting desperately for Nexus S to be released in India. After <a title="How credible are the Nexus S India Launch Price Rumors?" href="/mobile-phones/how-credible-are-the-nexus-s-india-launch-price-rumors">all the rumors</a> that Infibeam started were rendered baseless, the phone is now <a href="http://www.flipkart.com/samsung-google-nexus-s-mobile-mobcu43efesazke2" target="_blank">available on Flipkart.com</a> for pre-order. According to them, the phone will be available sometime in the first week of April. At INR 27,999 the price is pretty much similar to the International price.</p>
<p>Should you buy it? Well, if you are interested in a device that will be <em>more </em>frequently updated to the latest releases, you should. I did. And I must say it's one of the better Android phones around.</p>
