<p>You probably know by now that <a href="http://www.google.com/nexus">Nexus S</a> is currently the best Android phone you can buy. It was launched on <a href="/google/google-and-samsung-debut-the-nexus-s">16th December 2010</a> in the US through Best Buy. The phone is currently available in the US <strong><span>unlocked</span></strong> or locked with T mobile. The unlocked one retails for $530 plus taxes which comes close to 26k in INR. Not bad. But does it work properly in India? Will you be able to utilize all its features here in India if you happen to get yourself an unlocked one from the US?</p>
<p>We have an unlocked nexus S bought from the US and we can confirm that it works well over MTNL's 3G network in India. Here are some pics to confirm:</p>

<p><img title="NewImage.jpg" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/NewImage64.jpg" alt="NewImage.jpg" width="448" height="600" /></p>
<p><img title="NewImage.jpg" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/NewImage65.jpg" alt="NewImage.jpg" width="448" height="600" /></p>
<p>The phone is packed with lots of super awesome features and is a great buy if you are traveling to the US or have some friend coming from there.</p>
<p>Here are the APN settings you will need:</p>
<p><span><strong>MTNL 3G</strong></span> (prepaid):</p>

<ul>
	<li>APN: pps3g</li>
	<li>username: mtnl</li>
	<li>password: mtnl123</li>
</ul>
<p><span><strong>MTNL 3G</strong></span> (postpaid):</p>

<ul>
	<li>APN: mtnl3g</li>
	<li>username: mtnl</li>
	<li>password: mtnl123</li>
</ul>
<p>So all of you who were planning to buy this phone can confidently go ahead and buy one to use in India.</p>
