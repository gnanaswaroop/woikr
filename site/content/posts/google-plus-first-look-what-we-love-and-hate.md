Google plus is the new and much-awaited social network from the search giant. This was also termed as a Facebook-Killer etc. Let’s look inside and see what Google Plus offers.

<strong><a href="http://www.google.com/support/profiles/bin/static.py?hl=en&amp;page=guide.cs&amp;guide=1257347&amp;rd=1" target="_blank">Circles</a> – </strong>You can add a group of friends into a circle like a Family, Work etc. Having circles would let you select and share selectively. If you're a school teacher, you should create a different group for your students for your party friends. This way you can share your "Drunken Night" photos with the appropriate audience.

<a href="http://www.google.com/support/profiles/bin/static.py?hl=en&amp;page=guide.cs&amp;guide=1257360&amp;rd=1" target="_blank"><strong>Stream</strong></a> – Like your regular Facebook or Twitter Timeline, where you can see what your friends shared with you and comment/re-share them too. You can also post pictures/videos/links and also your location.

<a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/06/GoogleStream.png"><img title="GoogleStream" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/06/GoogleStream_thumb.png" alt="GoogleStream" width="128" height="128" /></a>
<div></div>
<a href="http://www.google.com/support/profiles/bin/static.py?hl=en&amp;page=guide.cs&amp;guide=1289752&amp;rd=1" target="_blank"><strong>Sparks</strong></a><strong> – </strong>All the news about the topics you're interested in one place. If you're below 20 or a geek – you probably don't care much about this anyway.

<a href="http://www.google.com/support/profiles/bin/static.py?hl=en&amp;page=guide.cs&amp;guide=1257349&amp;rd=1" target="_blank"><strong>Hangouts</strong></a> – Video Chat Baby!! You can now group video chat with your friends. Of course if you don’t have a webcam, you could use it to voice chat with your friends. I know that group video chatting has been there for ages - So were touch screens phones before the iPhone set new standard.

<a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/06/GoogleHangoutVideoChat.jpg"><img title="Google-Hangout-VideoChat" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/06/GoogleHangoutVideoChat_thumb.jpg" alt="Google-Hangout-VideoChat" width="110" height="107" /></a>

<strong>What we Love</strong>
<ul>
	<li>
<div>It is Fast – opens up in no time.</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Group Video chat – You can create "Circles" and video chat with multiple friends.</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Better Notifications/Integration – You can see "Plus" notifications on all the Google apps you use - GMail, Google Reader etc.<br>
You can also check them and reply back without leaving the page.<br>
<a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/06/GooglePlusNotifications.jpg"><img title="Google-Plus-Notifications" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/06/GooglePlusNotifications_thumb.jpg" alt="Google-Plus-Notifications" width="469" height="171" /></a></div></li>
	<li>
<div>Great new "Picture Preview" – you can view the picture and comment right on the picture. The pictures and comments are of course Picasa Web Albums driven.<br>
<a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/06/GooglePlusImageViewerCommentsNew.jpg"><img title="GooglePlus-Image-Viewer-Comments-New" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/06/GooglePlusImageViewerCommentsNew_thumb.jpg" alt="GooglePlus-Image-Viewer-Comments-New" width="520" height="278" /></a></div></li>
</ul>
<strong>What we Hate</strong>
<ul>
	<li>
<div>Invite only (hope it’ll change very soon) – Google has probably the best hardware infrastructure in the world, I’d expect them to launch a social network where I can really socialize. You should start with a feature to support importing “Social Graph” from Facebook.</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Messaging isn’t very obvious, you can send them a Google Talk IM or create a status update by just sharing it with your "friend". It's not as easy as going to someone's profile and writing on their Facebook wall.</div></li>
	<li>
<div>No Profile URL – Yuck who wants a random generated integer in their Profile URL? The least they could do is integrate with the Google Profile URL handle.</div></li>
	<li>
<div>No Reply to comments by email – We love replying back to Facebook comments from our email/phone. We miss that.</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Doesn’t the Google+ page look empty?<br>
We'll probably see more info flowing when Google exposes out APIs and 3rd party services use that to start cluttering your stream.</div></li>
</ul>
We believe this definitely is a strong competitor to Facebook, but it'll take time for people to move away from Facebook (like they eventually did with Orkut). Let there be less spam.
