<p>I was browsing for restaurants earlier this week and saw that Google has started adding &quot;See Inside&quot; in India too. Despite setbacks to Google from providing a real Street View for Indian cities, Google has managed to <a href="/apple/google-maps-now-available-on-the-iphone/" target="_blank">innovate</a>.     <br />“See Inside” is a Street View for inside restaurants, shops and other establishments. There are already tons of places in Hyderabad for which you can check out the ambience in person.&#160; </p>  <p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2013/01/image.png"><img title="image" alt="image" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2013/01/image_thumb.png" width="500" height="333" /></a></p><p></p>  <p>To use this, drag and hold the “Yellow Guy” on Google Maps and all those places with a yellow dot appearing against them have an inside view. Drop it on one of the points to enter the place, Or visit each of this places in Google maps “More info” and click on “Look Inside”. </p>  <p>This is also supported on the Google Maps Mobile application too.    <br />    <br />Here, have a look at how cool these are – <a href="https://maps.google.com/maps?q&amp;layer=c&amp;z=17&amp;sll=17.429624,78.412789&amp;cid=2342875694317877059&amp;panoid=nmQF7cJbsxf9Rki2qj5UMA&amp;cbp=13,284.63526007058874,,0,0&amp;ved=0CBMQ2wU&amp;sa=X&amp;ei=MYD-UNyQDtHSiALF9YHIDg&amp;gl=US&amp;hl=en" target="_blank">Beyond Coffee</a>, <a href="https://maps.google.com/maps?q&amp;layer=c&amp;z=17&amp;sll=17.435207,78.401597&amp;cid=12057681443752691948&amp;panoid=AhnG6Pux5nwhD0epPljmuA&amp;cbp=13,283.48872838424342,,0,0&amp;ved=0CA8Q2wU&amp;sa=X&amp;ei=KID-UI_BLoiXiALOpIGYCA&amp;gl=US&amp;hl=en" target="_blank">N-Grill</a> or a <a href="https://maps.google.com/maps?q&amp;layer=c&amp;z=17&amp;sll=17.434200,78.403463&amp;cid=15880623709796772205&amp;panoid=_gU6jjmf4KGm1rRvUO5ihA&amp;cbp=13,217.2782250015637,,0,0&amp;ved=0CA8Q2wU&amp;sa=X&amp;ei=BoH-UPHeEoqdiQK7y4BA&amp;gl=US&amp;hl=en" target="_blank">Levis Store</a>. Go around the place using the arrow keys and explore the different views.     <br />    <br /><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2013/01/image1.png"><img title="image" alt="image" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2013/01/image_thumb1.png" width="500" height="381" /></a>    <br />    <br /><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2013/01/IMG_4659.png"><img title="IMG_4659" alt="IMG_4659" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2013/01/IMG_4659_thumb.png" width="375" height="561" /></a></p>  <p></p>  <p>Now when your friends tell you about a great restaurant , check it out with just your smart phone.
