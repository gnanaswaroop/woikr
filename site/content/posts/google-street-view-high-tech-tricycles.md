<p><a href="/tag/google">Google</a> is apparently trying to add high tech tricycles to its fleet of street view vehicles. The bike was spotted in a local San Diego bike shop:</p>  <p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/05/google-street-view-tricycle-1.jpg"><img title="google_street_view_tricycle_1" height="379" alt="google_street_view_tricycle_1" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/05/google-street-view-tricycle-1-thumb.jpg" width="504" /></a></p>  <p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/05/google-street-view-tricycle-2.jpg"><img title="google_street_view_tricycle_2" height="379" alt="google_street_view_tricycle_2" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/05/google-street-view-tricycle-2-thumb.jpg" width="504" /></a> </p>  <p></p>  <p>The bike is carrying a generator and will arguably be pretty heavy. It might be difficult for people in the US to pedal this thing, however, it will not be a problem here in India. Thanks to already prevalent <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rickshaw" target="_blank">rickshaws</a> on the Indian roads, there are a lot of men in India who can pedal this thing.</p>  <p>This makes me wonder when Google might be planning to launch the street view project in India. This thing might actually be pretty useful here.</p>  <p>[via <a href="http://gizmodo.com/5255361/google-street-view-employs-high+tech-tricycles-for-hard+to+reach-places" target="_blank">Gizmodo</a>]</p>
