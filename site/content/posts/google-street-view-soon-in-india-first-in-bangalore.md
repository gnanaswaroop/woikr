<p>So the cars <a href="/google/google-street-view-could-soon-be-real-in-india" target="_blank">we spotted earlier</a> across India may not really be the &quot;Street View&quot; cars, they could have been just collecting WiFi triangulation data only. </p>  <p>As reported on the <a href="http://googleindia.blogspot.com/2011/05/google-street-view-start-of-driving-in.html" target="_blank">Google India blog</a> today, starting <strike>today</strike> tomorrow (27th May 2011), the Google Street View cars and a few Google Trikes (modified indian rickshaw with a StreetView Camera on the top) would be collecting pictures of select locations of roads in Bangalore. This will probably be done for other major cities too soon.</p>  <p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/05/Google_Street_View_Cars_Tricycle_Captiva.jpg"><img title="Google_Street_View_Cars_Tricycle_Captiva" alt="Google_Street_View_Cars_Tricycle_Captiva" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/05/Google_Street_View_Cars_Tricycle_Captiva_thumb.jpg" width="428" height="262" /></a> </p>  <p>In case you pass by these cars on the roads of Bangalore, do let us know in the comments. </p>  <p>[Via <a href="http://storify.com/gaurish/finallygoogle-streetview-its-coming-to-bengaluru-i/" target="_blank">Gaurish</a>]</p>
