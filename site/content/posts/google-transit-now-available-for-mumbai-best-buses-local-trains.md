<p>Google Maps already provides <a href="/google/delhi-metro-routes-now-available-on-google-maps">Metro routes in Delhi</a> &amp; transit information for their users in Bangalore &amp; Pune. The same transit information is now available to Mumbai users with Google maps offering information about <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BEST_Bus">BEST</a> buses &amp; the infamous Mumbai <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mumbai_local_train">local train system</a>.</p>
<p>The system lets anyone to look for routes from one place to another in Mumbai and find transit information in the route. This is a great feature for people in Mumbai as well as tourists visiting Mumbai.</p><p><img title="NewImage.png" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/03/NewImage37.png" alt="NewImage" width="400" height="289" /></p>
<p><strong>How to use Google Transit ?</strong></p>
<p>Search for information to reach from Place A to B on Google Maps</p>
<ul>
<li>The result shows a map with the possible route and driving directions for the commuter</li>
<li>Choose the transit option by clicking the train icon (next to the car icon)</li>
<li>Commuters would get first hand information on available routes and the step by step directions including frequency, and travel duration for each leg of the trip in text and on the map.</li>
</ul>
