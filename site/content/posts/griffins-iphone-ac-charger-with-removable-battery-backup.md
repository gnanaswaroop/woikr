<p>There are designs. And there are intelligent designs. The griffin’s latest iPhone/iPod charger is the latter:</p>

<a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/05/powerblockreserve.jpg"><img title="powerblock-reserve" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/05/powerblockreserve-thumb.jpg" alt="powerblock-reserve" width="504" height="215" /></a>
<p>Its basically Griffin’s own <a href="http://gizmodo.com/tag/powerblock-reserve/" target="_blank">PowerBlock Reserve</a> and <a href="http://www.griffintechnology.com/products/powerjolt-reserve" target="_blank">PowerJolt Reserve</a> combined into a single product. The two separately cost $39.99 each, the combined one costs $59.99.</p>

<h3>Key Features:</h3>
<ul>
	<li>
<div>Charges iPhone, iPod, most MP3 players and other USB-chargeable devices</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Removable Reserve Battery Pack provides additional hours of use time for <a href="http://www.com/tag/ipod">iPod</a> and <a href="/tag/iphone">iPhone</a></div></li>
	<li>
<div>Removable Griffin Reserve Battery Pack is interchangeable between the PowerJolt Reserve and PowerBlock Reserve</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Additional Reserve Battery Packs, available late Spring, 2009, provide added freedom and flexibility</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Lighted meter on Reserve Battery Pack indicates remaining power</div></li>
</ul>
