I have to buy a new postpaid connection for my mom. Now I want to go with either <a href="http://www.airtel.in" target="_blank">Airtel</a> or <a href="http://www.vodafone.in" target="_blank">Vodafone</a>. The decision will depend on whosoever provides cheaper calling cards for ISD.
<p>I browsed Airtel's website and found out the different calling cards option. To be sure, I called up their customer care and within 2 minutes, I was able to reach a customer care executive and he tells me the various calling cards available for postpaid. Period.</p>
<p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/05/vodafone_logo.gif"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-2805" title="vodafone_logo" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/05/vodafone_logo.gif" alt="vodafone_logo" width="116" height="99" /></a></p>
<p>Next up was Vodafone. Since I could not find any info on calling cards on their website (they forgot?), I called up their customer care and chose the option - I am not a Vodafone Customer and am interested in a new postpaid connection.</p>
<p>After a few button clicks, I was able to reach the customer care guy (who greeted me in Hindi even though I had selected English). The conversation went like this:</p>

<blockquote><strong>Me</strong>: Can you tell me about the calling cards available for a Vodafone postpaid customer for making cheaper ISD calls?
<strong>CC: </strong>Surely sir, could you please provide me your Vodafone number?
<strong>Me</strong>: I am not a Vodafone customer yet. I might become one if I like your calling cards
<strong>CC</strong>: OK. Could you please provide me your Vodafone number?
<strong>Me</strong>: As I told you, I do not have a Vodafone number. I am currently looking to buy a new postpaid connection from either Airtel or Vodafone and that decision will solely depend on who is offering cheaper Calling cards.
<strong>CC</strong>: But sir, I need your Vodafone number to give you that information.
<strong>Me </strong>(frustrated): Can't you simply tell me about the various calling cards available to postpaid customers?
<strong>CC</strong>: No Sir, you need to have a Vodafone number first. Moreover, how will you use that calling card if you do not have a Vodafone number?
<strong>Me </strong>(confused): Who said anything about buying the card right away, I have called up to get some info. Now I cannot first buy the connections and then evaluate the calling cards. I need to check them out before hand.
<strong>CC</strong>: I can give you info on a new Vodafone connection sir. (<em>Then starts blabbing about it).
</em><strong>Me</strong>: I know everything about a new connection. I just needed info around calling cards.
<strong>CC</strong>: I am sorry sir, I cannot give you that information until I have your Vodafone number.
<strong>Me</strong>: Fine. Thanks.
<strong>CC</strong>: Happy to Help (read hell) sir !</blockquote>
<p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/05/zoozoo.png"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-2806" title="zoozoo" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/05/zoozoo.png" alt="zoozoo" width="310" height="199" /></a></p>
<p>Apparently, Vodafone has no idea how frustrating its customer care is. I mean I understand they should discourage trolling, but they were hiding the calling cards info as if I was asking for a top secret mission info which is perhaps only available to their existing customers. They might be Happy to help, but the help isn't making the (perspective) customers happy.</p>
<p>That's 1 vote down for Vodafone.</p>
