<p>Apple has <a href="http://store.apple.com/us/browse/home/shop_iphone/family/iphone/iphone4s?mco=MjU5MTk4NzE">announced the pricing and availability</a> details for the unlocked <a href="/iphone/apple-announces-iphone-4s/">iPhone 4S</a>. It is priced as follows:</p>
<ul>
<li>16GB: $<strong>649</strong></li>
<li>32GB: $<strong>749</strong></li>
<li>64GB: $<strong>849</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>The unlocked models are listed as "Shipping in November" on Apple's online store. Which means we will only be able to order them after a few weeks. Note that the unlocked iPhones only work with supported GSM carriers around the world (In India, Airtel &amp; Vodafone are supported, however, iPhone works with all GSM carriers in India). CDMA only works when iPhone is purchased with a CDMA carrier contract. This means that you won't be able to buy an unlocked iPhone 4S and use it on Reliance or Tata Indicom's network in India.</p>
<p>Unlocked iPhone 4 and iPhone 3GS are already available at a <a href="/iphone/apple-drops-prices-for-unlocked-iphone-4-and-iphone-3gs/">reduced price</a>.</p>
<p><strong>UPDATE</strong>: Unlocked iPhone 4S is available in many other countries where government regulations mandate sale of unlocked model in addition to subsidized ones. These are countries like <a href="http://store.apple.com/ca">Canada</a>, <a href="http://store.apple.com/au/">Australia</a> and <a href="http://store.apple.com/uk/browse/home/shop_iphone/family/iphone/iphone4s?mco=MjU1NTYyNzM">UK</a>. Grab them from there if you have any sources there! (Thanks <a href="https://www.facebook.com/northzone13">Shubham</a>!)</p>
