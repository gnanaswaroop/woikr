<p>Star India is launching a new channel called <a href="http://premiere.starworld.in">Star World Premiere in HD</a> for the tech savvy generation in India which will air popular TV shows from USA at the same time they are aired there. This is a first of a kind initiative taken in India and will let users watch the latest shows at the same time with the rest of the world.</p>
<p>The channel will air <a href="http://premiere.starworld.in/schedule">26 of America’s most popular TV shows</a> in their current season.</p>
<p>Star World Premiere HD has confirmed in a tweet that the channel will be available on TataSky HD platform from 24th September 2013 onwards:</p>
<blockquote class="twitter-tweet">
<p>24th September onwards, Star World Premiere will be available on Tata Sky HD.</p>
<p>— Star World Premiere (@SWPremiere) <a href="https://twitter.com/SWPremiere/statuses/381084139350290432">September 20, 2013</a></p></blockquote>
<p></p>
<p>This is a really good news for TataSky HD subscribers. Given the platform’s <a href="/home-entertainment/tatasky-is-maxed-out-on-its-capacity-to-beam-hd-channels/">shortcomings in technical capability</a> to beam a decent amount of HD channels in the extremely competitive HD DTH arena, this move is going to pacify a lot of frustrated subscribers.</p>
<p>TataSky is also making an investment of about INR 900 crores in order to <a href="/home-entertainment/tatasky-replacing-all-mpeg2-set-top-boxes-with-mpeg4-set-top-boxes-to-free-up-bandwidth/">replace all the old MPEG-2 set top boxes with MPEG-4 set top boxes</a> free of cost for the customers. The move which will be completed by mid 2014 will enable TataSky to beam more than 300 channels up the current 230.</p>
