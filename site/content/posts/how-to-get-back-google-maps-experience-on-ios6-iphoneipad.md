 <li>Click &quot;Add to Home Screen&quot;. </li>    <li>This creates an application like home screen shortcut along with the other apps on your iPhone/iPad.      <br /><a to launch the Mobile Safari in full screen (without the address bar displayed). </li>    <li>The downside of this is that you need to toggle ON your &quot;traffic layer&quot; each time as it won't This is available for both iPhone and iPad. </li>    <li>Just install the application and it does exactly what you wish for. </li>    <li>Displays Google Maps with &quot;Traffic data&quot; on. </li>    <li>I can't seem to get the directions working in this application. The app is of course very buggy.
