<strong>What is Push Notification?</strong>

Basically it allows any app to notify you of any event using an SMS like pop-up, a numbered badge over the app icon or a custom sound. Apple explains it better <a href="http://developer.apple.com/iphone/program/sdk/apns.html" target="_blank">here</a>.

<strong>Why do you need it?</strong>

It's an awesome feature that, for one, allows you to have a proper IM client on your iPhone.

<strong>How can you make it work on your iPhone 2G?</strong>

There you go:
<ol>
	<li><a href="/apple/iphone/how-to-upgrade-and-unlock-your-iphone-2g-using-redsnow/" target="_blank">Upgrade to iPhone OS 3.0 and Jailbreak your iPhone</a> if you haven't already.</li>
	<li>Remove any Push-enabled apps that you might have installed. Some of the popular ones are AIM, BeeJive and reQall. In OS 3.0, they show up as a list in Settings-&gt;Notifications.</li>
	<li>Open Cydia/Icy, add a new repository with URL <strong><em>http://cydia.iphoneil.net </em></strong>and refresh<strong><em>.</em></strong></li>
	<li>Search for "Push Fix" and install the package.</li>
	<li>Re-install all your favorite Push-enabled apps.</li>
</ol>
That's it!

<strong>What all apps can you use?</strong>

<a href="http://mashable.com/2009/07/06/iphone-apps-push-notification/" target="_blank">Mashable</a> has a a pretty good list. Or you can lookup Push Notification on the App Store.

<strong>What about the battery life?</strong>

I'll test it for a few days and update this area with my findings.

<strong>UPDATE</strong>: Turning Push notifications on sucks battery life like anything. In 13 hours of normal usage the battery went down from 100% to 20%. Even worse, the battery went down from 80% to 60% in 3 hours while the phone was in sleep mode.

I've disabled Push Notifications. So much for the effort. @Apple, this needs to be taken care of.
