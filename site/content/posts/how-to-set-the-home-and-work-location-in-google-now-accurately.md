Google Maps allows us to select a Home and Work location for quick use to determine Directions and for use in Google Now too .

Here’s how to set the location.
<ol>
	<li>
<div>Open Google Maps on your Android or iOS device</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Click on the “Person” icon on the top right<br>
<a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/image.png"><img title="image" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/image_thumb.png" alt="image" width="452" height="81" /></a></div></li>
	<li>
<div>You’ll be displayed a screen to enter/edit the Home and Work address<br>
<a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/image1.png"><img title="image" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/image_thumb1.png" alt="image" width="452" height="398" /></a></div></li>
	<li>
<div>Long press the Home/Work fields to open the edit page and type in the building name or the complete address.</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Select the suggestion provided by Google and Voila! you have selected a Home address..</div></li>
</ol>
Sometimes when you’re living in an apartment complex it wouldn’t let you select the individual block. Now here’s a quick hat tip to set this location accurately.
<ol>
	<li>
<div>Open the <a href="http://maps.google.com" target="_blank">Google Maps</a> website on your desktop browser</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Search for the apartment complex. For example – “Serene County, Hyderabad”.</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Long press on the building name and you should see that it displays more details about the building including the geo latitude and longitude too.<br>
<a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/image2.png"><img title="image" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/image_thumb2.png" alt="image" width="452" height="338" /></a></div></li>
	<li>
<div>Now go back to the Google Maps app on your Android/iOS device</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Follow Steps #3 and #4 to open the Home/Work location edit page.</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Enter the latitude, longitude information from Step#2 into the input box and press done.<br>
<a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/image3.png"><img title="image" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/image_thumb3.png" alt="image" width="452" height="94" /></a></div></li>
	<li>
<div>You’ll be re-directed to the previous page again, this time the data will be populated with the selected address<br>
<a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/image4.png"><img title="image" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/image_thumb4.png" alt="image" width="452" height="373" /></a></div></li>
</ol>
There you go, you’ve just learnt how to setup the Google Home or Work location accurately.
