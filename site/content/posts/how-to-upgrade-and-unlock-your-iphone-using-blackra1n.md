<p><strong>Step 0.</strong> Backup your iPhone (skip this if its a new iPhone/or you wish to setup a new iPhone i.e loose your previous settings/mail configurations ..yada...yada)</p>
<p><strong>Step 1</strong>. Restore your iPhone to the latest firmware 3.1.2 using iTunes. On the summary tab, hold shift (OPTION key for Mac) and click on Restore. Browse and select the iPhone firmware 3.0 file downloaded above. iTunes will restore the phone using this firmware. This step will take approx 10 – 15 minutes.</p>
<p><strong>Step 2.</strong> Execute the blackra1n software</p>
<p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/blackra1n.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-3559" title="blackra1n" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/11/blackra1n.jpg" alt="blackra1n" width="400" height="100" /></a></p>
<p><strong>Step 3.</strong> Click on 'make it ra1n' and that's it.</p>
<p>That's all you need to unlock the iPhone (for 2G version, for 3G and 3GS - read till last step). You don't have to worry about DFU mode/boot rooms etc.<br>
After the process ends the phone will reboot.</p>
<p><strong>Step 4.</strong> Restore from backup or Setup a new iPhone.</p>
<p>Connect to iTunes and you'll be presented with a restore from backup screen. Use it to restore from backup.</p>
<p><strong>Step 5</strong>. Installing Cydia or Rock from the blackra1n application.</p>
<p>For those who were wondering that Cydia was missing - Cydia has to be explicitly installed by launching the blackra1n application on the iPhone (Check for the application with a black rain drop icon). There's also a new installer named 'Rock' along with Cydia. Try it out and let us know.</p>
<p>Tip: You can use <a href="http://thebigboss.org/moreinfo/AptBackup.php" target="_blank">Apt-Backup</a> to backup the list of Jailbroken applications and reinstall them after the upgrade.</p>
<p><strong>Step 6.</strong> If you're using an iPhone 2G. You're already done. If you're using iPhone 3G or 3GS. Proceed with this step</p>
<p>Open the blackra1n application and install "Snow". That should unlock your iPhone 3G and 3Gs. More details <a href="http://www.iclarified.com/entry/index.php?enid=5886" target="_blank">here</a>.</p>
