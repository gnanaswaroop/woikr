<p><a href="http://ihomeaudiointl.com/">iHome</a> is coming up with an <a href="http://ihomeaudiointl.com/discover/airplay/">AirPlay compatible speaker system</a> with a rechargeable battery. You can beam audio from any iOS device or mac to this speaker system wirelessly using Apple's <a href="http://www.apple.com/itunes/airplay/">AirPlay technology</a> (previously called AirTunes).</p>
<p>The speaker system (called iW1) is expected to have the following features:</p>

<ul>
	<li>Multiple iW1 speaker support -- enjoy music streamed all around your house simultaneously with AirPlay and multiple iW1 speakers</li>
	<li>Built-in rechargeable li-ion bettery with included charging base</li>
	<li>Studio Series speakers -- high performance audio featuring  Bongiovi Acoustics DPS with specially tuned woofers and tweeters with a  clean, efficient Class-D amplifier</li>
	<li>iPhone / iPod / iPad docking, charging and local audio playback via USB cable</li>
	<li>A capacitive, backlit, touch-sensitive control panel</li>
</ul>
<p>The speaker system will be priced around US$465 and is expected to be available around Feb-Mar timeframe.</p>
