<p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/04/available_on_app_store.png"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-2419" title="available_on_app_store" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/04/available_on_app_store.png" alt="available_on_app_store" width="135" height="44" /></a></p>
<p>There are 2 new apps available on the iPhone which let you offer prayers to <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durga" target="_blank">Godess Durga</a> and <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vishnu" target="_blank">Lord Vishnu</a>.</p>
<p>The apps - Shri Vishnu Prarthana [$1.99, <a href="http://itunes.apple.com/WebObjects/MZStore.woa/wa/viewSoftware?id=306952186&amp;mt=8" target="_blank">App Store</a>] and Shri Durga Prarthana [$1.99, <a href="http://itunes.apple.com/WebObjects/MZStore.woa/wa/viewSoftware?id=307222405&amp;mt=8" target="_blank">App Store</a>] have been developed by <a href="http://www.robosoftin.com/vishnuprarthana" target="_blank">Robosoft</a>. <span>Both of these apps are divided into two parts.</span></p>

<a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/04/godess_durga_iphone.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-2490" title="godess_durga_iphone" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/04/godess_durga_iphone.jpg" alt="godess_durga_iphone" width="166" height="250" /></a>
<p>Durga and Vishnu Chalisa: In this part, you listen to the Durga and Vishnu Chalisa and chant along.</p>

<ul>
	<li>Both Chalisa text and Chalisa meaning can be viewed in Hindi and English</li>
	<li>Pictorial representation of Chalisa Text</li>
	<li>Rich-quality, soothing and devotional Chalisa audio</li>
	<li>Chalisa text, Pictorial representation and Chalisa meaning synchronized with Chalisa audio</li>
	<li>Easy swipe gestures for Fast Forward and Rewind</li>
</ul>
<a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/04/lord_vishnu_iphone.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-2491" title="lord_vishnu_iphone" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/04/lord_vishnu_iphone.jpg" alt="lord_vishnu_iphone" width="166" height="250" /></a>
<p>Durga and Vishnu Aarti: In this part, you pay tribute to and worship Goddess Durga and Lord Vishnu</p>

<ul>
	<li>High-quality, soothing and devotional Aarti audio</li>
	<li>Perform Puja starting with touching the bell to start Aarti</li>
	<li>Touch the lamps to light them</li>
	<li>Touch the incense sticks to light them</li>
	<li>Touch the flower bowl to shower flowers over the deity</li>
	<li>Apply tilak on the deity</li>
	<li>Offer fruits to the deity</li>
	<li>End the Puja by performing Aarti</li>
	<li>Creates an ambiance of devotion</li>
</ul>
