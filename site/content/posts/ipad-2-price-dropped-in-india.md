<p>PluggdIn is <a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/03/NewImage.png">reporting</a> that Apple has dropped the price os iPad 2 in India. The 16GB WiFi only model will now sell at INR 24,500 and the 16GB WiFi + 3G model is available at INR 32,900 (down from INR 36,900). No word on availability and price of the new iPad.</p>
