<p>Apple has sent out invites to the March 2nd 2011 where the iPad 2 will be announced. This is a respite to all those who've been holding out to purchase an iPad for a long time. Enough from the <a href="/search/ipad+rumors/" target="_blank">rumor mill</a>, we now have concrete date to wait for. </p>  <p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/02/iPad2March2ndAppleEventInvite.jpg"><img title="iPad-2-March-2nd-Apple-Event-Invite" alt="iPad-2-March-2nd-Apple-Event-Invite" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/02/iPad2March2ndAppleEventInvite_thumb.jpg" width="275" height="258" /></a> </p>  <p>Till now, Apple products were the first to set higher standards for every gadget, be it music players, mobile phones or the tablets. 2011 is going to be an exciting year. We earlier saw that Google's Android is <a href="/news/gartner-says-android-grew-888-in-2010-ate-mostly-from-symbians-pie" target="_blank">growing at a fast pace</a>, this is the time for Apple to shoot ahead.</p>  <div></div>  <p><strong><img src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/12/apple_ipad.jpg" width="144" height="211" /> </strong></p>  <p><strong>5 must have features on the iPad2</strong></p>  <ul>   <li>     <div><strong>Better screen</strong> (screen resolution) – Android tablet's like XOOM have 1280 resolution. </div>   </li>    <li>     <div><strong>Front camera</strong> – We’re pretty sure this'll be in there.
