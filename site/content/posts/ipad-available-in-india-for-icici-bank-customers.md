<p>&#160;<img title="image_thumb4[1]" alt="image_thumb4[1]" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/11/image_thumb41.png" width="240" height="237" /> </p>  <p><a href="http://www.icicibank.com/online-services/offer-zone/iPad.htm" target="_blank">ICICI Bank</a> and <a href="http://www.gadgetsguru.com/ipad/default.aspx" target="_blank">GadgetsGuru.com</a> are selling the <a href="/apple/its-a-laptop-its-a-netbook-its-a-phone-its-ipad" target="_blank">iPad</a> wifi models in India at absurd prices and calling it ‘offer’. Apparently, these morons do not know what an offer means.</p>  <p>Here are the details of the alleged ‘offer’:</p>  <ul>   <li>     <div>iPad 16GB wifi – INR 34,999</div>   </li>    <li>     <div>iPad 32GB wifi – INR 39,999</div>   </li>    <li>     <div>iPad 64GB wifi – INR 46,999</div>   </li> </ul>  <div></div>  <p>Above mentioned prices also include the original Apple iPad case, a screen protector and free shipping anywhere in India. The offer remains even with these goodies. No 3G model is available.</p>  <p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/11/image.png"><img title="image" alt="image" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/11/image_thumb.png" width="644" height="134" /></a> </p>  <p>The idiots have even gone to the limit of mentioning hypothetical MRPs as follows:</p>  <ul>   <li>     <div>16GB – INR 45,990</div>   </li>    <li>     <div>32GB – INR 55,990</div>   </li>    <li>     <div>64GB – INR 65,990</div>   </li> </ul>  <p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/11/image1.png"><img title="image" alt="image" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/11/image_thumb1.png" width="640" height="266" /></a></p>  <p>The MRP of the $40 US priced original case has been insanely mentioned as INR 5500. Surprisingly, the price of the screen protector has been mentioned reasonably at INR 350.</p>  <p>In addition to being seriously absurd, this is also infinitely WTF. Apple hasn’t yet launched the iPad officially in India. They still run the ‘<a href="http://www.apple.com/in/ipad/notify-me/" target="_blank">Notify Me</a>’ page for iPad on their site. I am not sure from where these guys got the MRP of iPad in India. Looking at the figures, it looks like they have taken the prices from ebay.</p>  <p>Also, since the iPad has not been officially launched in India, this one will probably ship with a non India style power adapter. However, it is not mentioned anywhere.</p>  <p>As always, we would recommend you not to get fooled by these so called offers and either wait for the official launch in India (god knows when) or have a friend or relative get it for you from abroad where its available at a much cheaper price.</p>
