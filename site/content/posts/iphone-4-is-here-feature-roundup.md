<p>The iPhone 4 and iOS4 got announced on June 7th, 2010. It’s pretty close (read as “same”)    to the <a href="/apple/iphone-4g-leaked" target="_blank">leaked iPhone prototype</a> that Gizmodo “found in a bar”.</p>
<p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/iPhone4Pic1.jpg"><img title="iPhone4-Pic1" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/iPhone4Pic1_thumb.jpg" alt="iPhone4-Pic1" width="516" height="328" /></a></p>
<p>As Steve Jobs said in the keynote, this is the “biggest leap since the original iPhone”. It indeed is, read further to know why</p>
<p><strong>The core features of this iPhone 4 are</strong></p>

<ul>
	<li>Retina Display brings 4times more pixels – Sending the resolution of the iPhone to 960x640.</li>
	<li>IPS Display – Better Wide-Angle viewing (like the iPad)</li>
	<li>Contrast Ratio of 800:1 which is 4 times better than iPhone 3G(S)</li>
	<li>HD Video recording – Records videos at 720p (it’s HD)</li>
	<li>Frontal camera for Video chat – We’ve had this for ages, but without proper network support. Finally we have it. FaceTime any one?</li>
	<li>New Apple A4 processor – Developed by Apple’s own team. Supposedly manages power better.</li>
	<li>Better battery life – More calls, more surfing. Yay!</li>
	<li>Gyro + Accelerometer – 6 Axis motion detection – Awesome games to come up using this.</li>
	<li>5MP Camera with LED Flash and 5x Digital Zoom</li>
	<li><a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEEE_802.11n-2009" target="_blank">802.11n</a><strong> – </strong>Yes WiFi ‘n’ ready.</li>
	<li>Dual Mike for Noise cancellation – Yes, now you don’t have to push your phone into your ear and talk less louder.. will ya.</li>
	<li>New Stainless steel body to make it studier in case of accidental fall.</li>
	<li>24% thinner than iPhone 3GS – Surely gonna fit well in my pocket.</li>
	<li>New glass material used in aircrafts and high speed trains which is 12 times harder than plastic. I am sure there’ll be a group of users who’ll still manage to break it.</li>
</ul>
<p>The iOS4.0 as earlier announced provides</p>

<ul>
	<li>The OS can now support <a href="/apple/apple-announces-iphone-os-4-with-multitasking" target="_blank">Multi tasking</a> applications. So you can now listen to music, surf the web and tweet at the same time.. Aah paradise.</li>
	<li>Has <a href="/iphone/video-chat-on-the-next-iphone-rumors" target="_blank">Video chat</a> as earlier discovered by curious developers for the folks who wish to get “closer”.</li>
	<li>Unified Inbox – Much awaited, helps manage multiple email boxes (I’ve got 5) and threaded conversations to make life easier.</li>
	<li>iBooks – For the book worms in iPhone users</li>
	<li>Folders – so that you can manage/group/categorize your apps together.</li>
	<li>Home screen wallpaper</li>
	<li>Tap to focus in Video</li>
	<li>Spell checker</li>
</ul>
<p>And <a href="/iphone/iphone-os-4-will-bring-orientation-lock-file-sharing-ipod-controls" target="_blank">tens</a> of other features (<a href="http://www.apple.com/iphone/softwareupdate/" target="_blank">100 new features</a>).</p>
<p>The new iPhone is to be released in 5 countries on June 24th (pre orders starting from June 15th 2010) at a price tag of 199$ for the 16GB iPhone and 299$ for 32GB iPhone. You can bid the 2 year old iPhone 3G good bye like how iPhone 2G was killed off with the <a href="/iphone/the-new-iphone-3gs" target="_blank">release of iPhone 3G(S)</a>.</p>
