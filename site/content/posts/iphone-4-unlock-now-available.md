<p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/08/image.png"><img title="image" alt="image" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/08/image_thumb.png" width="164" height="244" /></a> </p>  <p>The dev team has done it again. They have released the latest version of ultrasn0w which is now capable of unlocking the iPhone 4!</p>  <div></div>  <p>Here are the steps you need to do:</p>  <ol>   <li>     <div>Jailbeak your iPhone 4 by visiting <a href="http://jailbreakme.com" target="_blank">jailbreakme.com</a> from the phone’s Safari browser. </div>   </li>    <li>     <div>Once jailbroken, open Cydia and add the repository “repo666.ultrasn0w.com”. </div>   </li>    <li>     <div>Search for and install ultrasn0w. </div>   </li>    <li>     <div>Your phone is now unlocked! </div>   </li> </ol>  <p>The latest version supports unlocking of the following basebands:</p>  <ul>   <li>     <div>iPhone4 baseband 01.59 </div>   </li>    <li>     <div>3G/3GS basebands 04.26.08, 05.11.07, 05.12.01 and 05.13.04 </div>   </li> </ul>  <p>Additionally, you can also go to <a href="http://jailbreakme.com" target="_blank">jailbreakme.com</a> to jailbreak any iPhone, iPod Touch, or iPad. The beauty with this method is that this works inside the Safari browser on your device. No PC or Mac is needed!</p>  <p>[via <a href="http://blog.iphone-dev.org/" target="_blank">Dev-Team Blog</a>]</p>
