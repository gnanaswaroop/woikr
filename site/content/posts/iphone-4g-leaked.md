<p>So, it just happened to be a regular day when an Apple Employee (I hope still) <a href="http://www.flickr.com/photos/graypowell" target="_blank">Gray Powell</a> had too much to drink and left behind a well confiscated iPhone next generation phone (or its near production prototype) in a bar.</p>

<p>It's a real deal for sure. The phone was working for a day before Apple remotely wiped it out. But it was too late, much before one of "the other drunk" guy in the bar opened the Facebook application and logged into as Gray.</p>
<p>The Gizmodo folks acquired the phone and ripped the phone apart to find out the parts neatly fitted and this doesn't look like a prank/spoof phone, which does imply that this could be the model which is due to be released by Apple in June 2010.</p>
<p>The new things noticed were</p>

<ul>
	<li>A frontal camera - Glad we can finally video conference, Girls *Ahem* can use it as a makeup mirror too.</li>
	<li>New phone design - the back of the phone is flat and no longer curvy.</li>
	<li>Separate volume buttons instead of a single button as earlier - some say this could be used for a hard button for clicking pictures on the camera application.</li>
	<li>Better battery life (5.25 WHr at 3.7V) - The one in iPhone 3GS was 4.51 WHr at 3.7V.</li>
	<li>Fits well in your pant pockets and purses &amp; possible doesn't rotate on vibrate</li>
	<li>Really unstable version of iPhone OS 4.0 - The camera app seems to have crashed a couple of times.</li>
	<li>A mini printer - Okay I admit - I made up the last one.</li>
</ul>
<p>There are several arguments that this could be a controlled leak to suppress the "possible" attention that HTC Incredible (Android based) might be getting.</p>
