<p>We are giving away 3 promo codes for <a href="http://itunes.apple.com/us/app/bookbuddy/id395149950#">BookBuddy</a> - a $4.99 book managing app for the iPhone.</p>
<p>BookBuddy is a powerful book management application which gives you  access to your entire book catalog anywhere. Using BookBuddy is fun and  easy and allows you to quickly find any book in your library, share your  favorite books and keep track of the borrowed ones.</p>

<p><strong>Features</strong>:</p>

<ul>
	<li>Quickly add books to your library by searching Google Books for book’s title, author or ISBN.</li>
	<li>Create your own categories to organize your books.</li>
	<li>Share your favorite books with family and friends.</li>
	<li>Personalize your own Wish List.</li>
	<li>Keep track of books borrowed by your family and friends.</li>
	<li>Manage your list of Unread books.</li>
	<li>Quickly find any book in your library by entering book title or author.</li>
	<li>Easily access any book’s web page directly from the application.</li>
</ul>
