<g:plusone size="medium"></g:plusone></li><li></li></ul><p>We’ve long cribbed that movie booking was not easy from mobile devices.    <br><a href="http://in.bookmyshow.com/" target="_blank">BookMyShow</a> answers our cries by releasing an iOS version of the application.</p>
<p><a href="/wp-content/uploads/2010/12/BookMyShowLogo.jpg" rel="lightbox[4969]"><img title="BookMyShow-Logo" alt="BookMyShow-Logo" src="/wp-content/uploads/2010/12/BookMyShowLogo_thumb.jpg" width="172" height="59"></a> </p>
<p>Here’s a quick look at what all the application provides. </p>
<p>The iOS application allows you to select from a list of movies only at the moment, but booking for plays, sport and other events is going to come soon – Well, this is a start.</p>
<p>
</p><div><span id="more-4969"></span></div>
<p>It’s got a couple of interesting features. One can</p>
<ul>
<li>
<div>Select the movie to watch and can book across all cinemas in all the Indian cities. </div>
</li>
<li>
<div>Seat selection – pretty useful if you’d not want to sitting in the front rows. I wonder when the Indian railways will ever implement this for their online reservation. </div>
</li>
<li>
<div>Payment options are flexible Credit/Debit cards &amp; Internet banking. </div>
</li>
</ul>

<p>I am a little worried here though, for the internet banking pages – like ICICI appears BookMyShow folks have built a custom UI around it to match the ‘black theme’ and it’s a little in-secure – We’d want them to show us the original ICICI’s website where we enter our user/password and not on their custom UI and then pass on to ICICI’s website.</p>
<p>Also we’d like to be able to see all the movies playing in a particular cinema (we’re too lazy to travel around to the other part of the city, no?). That’s missing for now. </p>
<p>Let us know if you like/hate it or you can also thank <a href="http://twitter.com/BookMyShow" target="_blank">@BookMyShow</a> team on twitter.</p>
<p>[Download <a href="http://itunes.apple.com/in/app/bookmyshow-com/id405894842?mt=8#" target="_blank">BookMyShow application</a> on iTunes]</p>
