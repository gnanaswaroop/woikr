<p align="justify"><a href="http://www.epicgames.com/" target="_blank">Epic Games</a> (makers of <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gears_of_War_(series)" target="_blank">Gears of War</a>) have released a trailer of their upcoming iPhone game <a href="http://www.epicgames.com/news/epic-games-and-chair-entertainment-announce-unreal-engine-3-powered-infinit/" target="_blank">Infinity Blade</a>. The game uses Unreal Engine 3 and blows away any other high-end iPhone game in terms of graphics quality on the iPhone. This is the same game which was showcased as “Project Sword” at the September 2010 Apple special event and is also featured in the latest ads for <a href="http://www.apple.com/iphone/gallery/ads.html#ad-every" target="_blank">iPhone 4</a> and <a href="http://www.apple.com/ipad/gallery/ads.html#ad-electric" target="_blank">iPad</a>.</p>
