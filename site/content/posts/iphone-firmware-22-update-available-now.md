<p>The latest iPhone firmware update 2.2 is out now. Its a 246 MB download and if you have the latest iTunes installed, it should prompt you to download and install it the next time you tether your iPhone.</p>

<a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/iphone22update.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1778" title="iphone22update" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/iphone22update.jpg" alt="" width="499" height="250" /></a>
<p>Here is a quick list of new features in 2.2:</p>

<ol>
	<li>Google Maps Street View</li>
	<li>Public transit and walking directions in Google Maps</li>
	<li>Over-the-air podcast downloads</li>
	<li>Mail enhancements</li>
	<li>Safari tweaks</li>
	<li>Improved sound quality for visual voicemail</li>
	<li>Fewer dropped calls (again)</li>
</ol>
<p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/iphone222.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-medium wp-image-1779" title="iphone222" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/iphone222-300x225.jpg" alt="" width="300" height="225" /></a></p>
<p>There no MMS or copy and paste though :(.</p>
<p><span>As usual, if you have a jailbroken or unlocked iPhone, DO NOT, I repeat, DO NOT update.</span></p>
