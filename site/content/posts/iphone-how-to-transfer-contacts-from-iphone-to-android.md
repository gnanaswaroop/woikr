<p align="justify">With so many awesome Android phones in the market, there will be lots of iPhone users who might want to jump the ship to Android. One of the challenges is to migrate all your contacts from iPhone to Android. Here we will present you with as few options to accomplish this easily via Google contacts.</p>
<p> <span id="more-6418"></span>  </p>
<p align="justify">Before you start with any of the following options, empty your “My Contacts” area in Gmail. To do this, click on “Contacts” in the left nav in Gmail, select all your contacts and then uncheck the “My Contacts” label under the “Groups” dropdown. Note that Gmail will only select the contacts visible on the page when you do a select all (using the checkbox in the header). So you might have to do this a couple of times to move all your contacts out of My Contacts.</p>
<p align="justify">Once you have done this, follow <strong><u>any</u></strong> of the following two options:</p>
<p align="justify">1. Sync your Mac address book to Google (applicable only for Mac):</p>
<ul>
<li>
<div align="justify">Sync your iPhone’s contacts to your Mac’s address book using iTunes</div>
</li>
<li>
<div align="justify">Open address book in your Mac</div>
</li>
<li>
<div align="justify">Open address book preferences</div>
</li>
<li>
<div align="justify">Under Accounts tab, check “Synchronize with Google” option</div>
</li>
<li>
<div align="justify">Provide your Gmail account details</div>
</li>
</ul>
<p align="justify">2. Sync your iPhone’s contacts to Google (using iTunes on Windows):</p>
<ul>
<li>
<div align="justify">Connect your iPhone to your computer and open iTunes</div>
</li>
<li>
<div align="justify">Click on “Summary” in the left nav</div>
</li>
<li>
<div align="justify">Under the “Info” tab, check Sync contacts</div>
</li>
<li>
<div align="justify">Choose Google contacts from the drop down menu</div>
</li>
<li>
<div align="justify">Provide your Google account details</div>
</li>
<li>
<div align="justify">Click “Apply”</div>
</li>
</ul>
<p align="justify">This will sync all your iPhone contacts to Gmail’s contacts under the “My Contacts” label by default (this is why we cleared that area).</p>
<p align="justify">Now enable contacts sync with Google on your Android device. By default, android also syncs all the contacts under “My Contacts” (you can change this, but we don’t want that for this tutorial). So you will get all the contacts from your iPhone to your shiny new Android over the air!</p>
