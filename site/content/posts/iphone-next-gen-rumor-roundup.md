Here are the current rumors floating around the web around the next gen iPhone:
<ol>
	<li><strong>Apple has increased next gen iPhone production</strong> - This might be true owing to the much hype the iPhone OS 3.0 has created.</li>
	<li><strong>iPhone to have FM receiver &amp; transmitter</strong> - We would love to have this ability in the next gen iPhone. For many people, FM plays a key role in deciding whether to go for the phone or not. The added benefit of the FM transmitter will be truly awesome for listening to music in your car.</li>
	<li><strong>Video recording</strong> - This has to come. People have been asking for this and Apple must pay attention to this.</li>
	<li><strong>Auto focus in camera</strong> - Although not a nccessity, this will be a welcomed feature. This will let amateurs take awesome pictures using the iphone.</li>
	<li><strong>Magnatometer (digital compass)</strong> - I seriously doubt this one.</li>
	<li><strong>Voice control</strong> - Dunno what this is. If its voice commands, then yeah, bring it on.</li>
</ol>
