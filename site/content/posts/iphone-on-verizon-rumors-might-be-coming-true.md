<p>The iPhone CDMA rumors <a href="/iphone/iphone-cdma-is-coming-soon" target="_blank">we talked about</a> seem to be coming true. There were two new developments that seem to make it more clear that iPhone is coming on Verizon soon.</p>
<p>One, Verizon has registered the domain name iphoneonverizon.com according to the whois page as discovered by <a href="http://pocketnow.com/iphone/verizon-buying-up-iphone-related-domains" target="_blank">pocketnow</a>. Two, Offwire a retail website has already got an iPhone 4 Verizon category of accessories. We believe this is pretty good evidence that the rumors are coming true soon.</p>
<p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/verizon_iphone_url.jpg"><img title="verizon_iphone_url" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/verizon_iphone_url-300x140.jpg" alt="" width="300" height="140" /></a></p>
<p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/verizon_iphone_accessories.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-medium wp-image-5643" title="verizon_iphone_accessories" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/verizon_iphone_accessories-247x300.jpg" alt="" width="247" height="300" /></a></p>
<p>While this is great information for US residents as they won't have to get stuck to AT&amp;T's bad network just because they want an iPhone, I'm not sure if it's of any use to us Indian people. While we have 2 big CDMA carriers in Reliance and Tata Indicom, we have never been big fans of their network. But Reliance surely has an ability to launch iPhone 4 in India at a decent price. Do you think a CDMA iPhone will mean Reliance or Tata Indicom will launch it in India down the line? Will you be interested if the price with 2 year contract is around 20k?</p>
