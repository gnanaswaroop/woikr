<p><img src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/iphone-logo.jpg" /> </p>  <p>Apple has released iPhone firmware 3.1.2 yesterday. The update comes with the following fixes &amp; improvements:</p>  <ul>   <li>     <div>Resolves sporadic issue that may cause iPhone to not wake from sleep</div>   </li>    <li>     <div>Resolves intermittent issue that may interrupt cellular network services until restart</div>   </li>    <li>     <div>Fixes bug that could cause occasional crash during video streaming</div>   </li> </ul>  <p><strong><font color="#ff0000">DO NOT</font></strong> upgrade if you are on a jailbroken or unlocked iPhone. The iPhone dev team has given the following update on their <a href="http://blog.iphone-dev.org/" target="_blank">blog</a>:</p>  <ul>   <ul>     <li>       <div>PwnageTool and redsn0w are not yet compatible with 3.1.2</div>     </li>      <li>       <div>There is no estimated release time for compatible tools (please don’t bug us about this).</div>     </li>      <li>       <div>Any information we have regarding this update will be posted here.</div>     </li>      <li>       <div>You can also follow us on twitter - <a href="http://twitter.com/iphone_dev" target="_blank">@iphone_dev</a></div>     </li>   </ul> </ul>  <p>[via <a href="http://blog.iphone-dev.org/post/207705127/3-1-2-and-you" target="_blank">Dev Team Blog</a>]</p>
