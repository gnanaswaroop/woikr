<p><a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Fadell">Tony Fadell</a> - the guy who designed the first iPod, through his company - <a href="http://www.nest.com/">nest labs</a>, has come up with a beautiful looking thermostat that learns user behavior and can help you save energy. Fadell calls it the thermostat for the iPhone generation. Here is the official video:</p>
<p></p>
<p>Amazing. The thermostat can also be controlled using your iPhone or computer from anywhere in the world where you have internet access. So, if its a cold day, you can set your desired temperature when you leave office so that you come home to a warm &amp; comfortable temperature. Check out all the features of the device <a href="http://www.nest.com/living-with-nest/">here</a>. Here is another video of Fadell explaining the product to TechCrunch:</p>
<p>The nest thermostat is priced at $249 which is a little steep, but might prove worth it with all those <a href="http://www.nest.com/living-with-nest/">energy savings</a>. You can pre order one here: <a href="https://store.nest.com/">https://store.nest.com/</a>.</p>
