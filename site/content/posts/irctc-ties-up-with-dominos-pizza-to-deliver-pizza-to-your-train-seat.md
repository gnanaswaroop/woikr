<a href="http://irctc.co.in" target="_blank">Indian Railways Catering and Tourism Corporation (IRCTC)</a> has tied up with Domino’s Pizza in India to deliver pizza to your train seat.

The service is being run as a pilot project in 12 Indian cities - Agra, Alwar, Ambala, Jaipur, Jalandhar, Mathura, Muzzafarnagar, New Delhi, Pathankot, Vapi, Bharuch and Vadodara and is only available to passengers travelling in trains which do not have apntry car.

To avail this service, passengers will need to make a meal booking via IRCTC’s e-Catering service (<a href="http://ecatering.irctc.co.in" target="_blank">ecatering.irctc.co.in</a>, 1800-1034-139 (Toll-free), 0120-4383892-99 (Toll) or SMS MEAL to 139) using their PNR number. Given that these pizzas have been added as standard menu items to the e-Catering menu, all the standard rules of the e-Catering service apply to this service as well.

IRCTC plans to extend the pizza menu to more cities gradually.
