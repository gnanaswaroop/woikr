<p><a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jenga">Jenga</a> is one of my favorite games. I play it with my roommates and at get together with friends. The game is based on an awesome concept. It is the game which the geek gang plays on the famous show "<a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Big_Bang_Theory">The Big Bang Theory</a>". The game is now available in the <a href="http://itunes.apple.com/us/app/jenga/id392915994?mt=8">iOS app store for $0.99</a>.</p><p><img title="jenga-ios.png" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/09/jenga-ios.png" alt="jenga ios" width="320" height="480" /></p>
<p>The game supports multiplayer via game center, but you can also play it using the traditional pass and play method. Although nothing beats the fun and super precision requirement of the real game, this version actually succeeds to maintain the thrill, thanks to the awesome physics engine.</p>
<p>Download it for <a href="http://itunes.apple.com/us/app/jenga/id392915994?mt=8">$0.99 from the App Store</a>.</p>
