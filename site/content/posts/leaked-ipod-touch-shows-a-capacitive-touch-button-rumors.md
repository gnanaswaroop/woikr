<p>Thanks to the underbelly of the world, we have new rumors to chase every day. The latest amongst them is that of a leaked iPod Touch’s &quot;blurry snap&quot; showing a capacitive touch button (like those you see on the new Android phones) in the place of a hard button. </p>  <p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/04/iPod-Touch-5thGen-Leaked-Capacitive-Touch-button.jpg"><img title="iPod-Touch-5thGen-Leaked-Capacitive-Touch-button" alt="iPod-Touch-5thGen-Leaked-Capacitive-Touch-button" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/04/iPod-Touch-5thGen-Leaked-Capacitive-Touch-button_thumb.jpg" width="197" height="240" /></a></p><p>Having a capacitive touch instead of the currently &quot;well-accepted&quot; home button doesn't make any sense, I agree with <a href="http://gizmodo.com/#!5789077/an-ipod-touch-capacitive-home-button-doesnt-make-any-sense" target="_blank">Gizmodo</a> that this would be a huge inconvenience as it could get easily triggered while playing a game or while making a call. I so hope that the next gen iPod/iPhone 5 would offer much better features to this, NFC perhaps?</p>  <p>The leaked pictures also show that the iPod Touch model has a 128GB flash memory and was running iOS4.2.1 (internal build). Also the iPod Touch model seems to have a metal back(aluminium like the original iPhone2G) similar to the rumors of iPhone 5 (thanks for replacing the crack-able glass Apple). </p>  <p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/04/iPod-Touch-5thGen-Leaked-back-side.jpg"><img title="iPod-Touch-5thGen-Leaked-back-side" alt="iPod-Touch-5thGen-Leaked-back-side" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/04/iPod-Touch-5thGen-Leaked-back-side_thumb.jpg" width="224" height="180" /></a>&#160;<a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/04/iPod-Touch-5thGen-Leaked-Firmware-About-Page.jpg"><img title="iPod-Touch-5thGen-Leaked-Firmware-About-Page" alt="iPod-Touch-5thGen-Leaked-Firmware-About-Page" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/04/iPod-Touch-5thGen-Leaked-Firmware-About-Page_thumb.jpg" width="240" height="180" /></a></p>  <p>Also Sony CEO <a href="http://www.9to5mac.com/59019/howard-stringer-says-sony-image-sensors-delayed-for-apples-ipad/" target="_blank">slipped a word</a> and mentioned that they're supplying 8.0 MP camera sensors (like to replace the current 5.0 MP <a href="http://www.reuters.com/article/2011/04/04/idUSL3E7F428120110404" target="_blank">OmniVision sensors</a>) for the iPhone and iPad(s). We wouldn't be surprised if the next generation iPod Touch would get this too.</p>  <p>Whoever takes the horribly blurred pictures of leaked Apple proto-types, Would you please get yourself a better camera, I think a 100$ dumb phone takes better pictures than the Samsung Digital camera you have.
