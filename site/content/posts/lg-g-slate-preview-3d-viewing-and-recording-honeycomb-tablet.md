<p>LG didn't plunge into the Android Tablet race early like it's competitor Samsung. The company earlier announced that Android 2.2 wasn't <a href="http://www.engadget.com/2010/10/04/lg-scraps-plans-for-froyo-tablet-will-wait-for-next-android-ver/" target="_blank">mature enough</a> for tablets and that it would wait for Android 3.0. It's recent products, <a href="/reviews/lg-optimus-one-p500-hands-on-and-review-with-pics" target="_blank">LG Optimus One</a> and <a href="/android/lg-unveils-worlds-first-dual-core-phone" target="_blank">LG Optimus 2x</a> are noteworthy.</p>
<p>LG G-Slate is a LG Optimus series tablet running Android 3.0 Honeycomb operating system also known as the LG Optimus PAD.</p>
<p>Here's the list of promising features that separates this tablet from the rest (in a good way).</p>
<p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/02/LGGSlatePicture.png"><img title="LG-G-Slate-Picture" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/02/LGGSlatePicture_thumb.png" alt="LG-G-Slate-Picture" width="542" height="331" /></a></p>
<p></p>

<h5>Interesting features</h5>
<ul>
	<li>
<div>Runs Android 3.0 Honeycomb which is built specifically for tablets.</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Supports 4G networks for faster browsing (to be released with T-Mobile in the US)</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Is a Google tablet (would come bundled with Android market etc)</div></li>
	<li>
<div>8.9" inch 3D Capacitive multi-touch display (didn't you think a 7’' Samsung Galaxy Tab was lame?)</div></li>
	<li>
<div>2MP front facing camera for those "intimate" video chats *ahem*.</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Two Rear facing <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stereoscopy" target="_blank">Stereoscopic 3D</a> cameras capture 3D videos</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Can record Full HD (1080p) videos also</div></li>
	<li>
<div>1Ghz Dual Core NVIDIA Tegra2 processor.</div></li>
	<li>
<div>32 GB Internal flash memory (going the Apple way?)</div></li>
	<li>
<div>5MP rear camera with LED Flash (Don't hope for a DSLR here, it’s a tablet)</div></li>
	<li>
<div>HDMI output in case you wish to watch the movie on your tablet on your Full HD TV.</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Micro-USB Charging (Will you companies use <a href="/news/european-commission-standardizes-microusb-as-mobile-charging-port" target="_blank">1 standard</a> already?).</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Has connectors for docking station (does it come free?)</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Decent 10 hours playback.</div></li>
	<li>
<div><a href="/iphone/mobile-safari-gyroscope-demo-shows-the-future-of-google-maps" target="_blank">Gyroscope</a> and an accelerometer for those fun games.</div></li>
</ul>
