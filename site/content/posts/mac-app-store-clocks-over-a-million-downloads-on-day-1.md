<p>I liked the Mac App Store when I saw it. I knew it's gonna be as awesome as the iPhone App Store but I didn't know it'll be this huge! Heck, I would've been happy with a figure like 10k downloads. It's awesome. Steve Jobs said in a press release, "We're amazed at the incredible response the Mac App Store is getting. Developers have done a great job bringing apps to the store and users are loving how easy and fun the Mac App Store is."</p>
<p>As TUAW says, the sales will definitely go down after the initial rush. But I believe this might prove to be a really important strategy move for Apple and Macs in the coming future.</p>
