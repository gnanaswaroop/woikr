<g:plusone size="medium"></g:plusone></li><li></li></ul><p><a href="/wp-content/uploads/2010/12/fluid_google_tasks_mac_menu.jpg" rel="lightbox[4861]"></a>If you love the <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_menu" target="_blank">Mac Menubar</a> like me, I’m sure you’re always looking for ways to access pretty much everything you use directly from your Menubar. Here is a way to access all your Google Tasks from you Mac’s Menu, organize your activities and fix your life forever. Potentially.</p>
<ul>
<li>Download and install <a href="http://fluidapp.com/" target="_blank">Fluid</a>.</li>
<li>Launch Fluid and enter URL as <code>"http://mail.google.com/tasks/ig"</code> for a normal Gmail account or&nbsp;<code>"http://mail.google.com/tasks/a/<em>&lt;your-domain-name&gt;</em>/ig"</code> for a custom Google Apps for your domain account.<br>
<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2010/12/fluid_create_google_tasks.jpg" rel="lightbox[4861]"><img title="fluid_create_google_tasks" src="/wp-content/uploads/2010/12/fluid_create_google_tasks-300x174.jpg" alt="" width="300" height="174"></a></li>
<li>Create the app and launch it.</li>
<li>Goto the app menu and select “Convert to MenuExtra SSB…”.</li>
<li>Your Google Tasks app is up and running in the Menu Bar. You can resize it to suit you.<br>
<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2010/12/fluid_google_tasks_mac_menu.jpg" rel="lightbox[4861]"><img title="fluid_google_tasks_mac_menu" src="/wp-content/uploads/2010/12/fluid_google_tasks_mac_menu-275x300.jpg" alt="" width="275" height="300"></a></li>
</ul>
<p>Go ahead,&nbsp;organize&nbsp;your life!</p>
