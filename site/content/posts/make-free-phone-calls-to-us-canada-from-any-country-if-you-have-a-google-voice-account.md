<p><img title="image" height="36" alt="image" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/09/image43.png" width="156" /> </p>  <p>A few weeks back, Google launched a feature in Gmail which enabled their Google voice users to <a href="/google/call-phones-from-gmail" target="_blank">make free calls to phones</a> in the US and Canada from within Gmail. However, this feature is only available to users physically located in the US and Canada. Even if you have a Google voice account, Gmail doesn’t show up the option when you log in from any other country (they use your ip address to detect where you are located).</p>  <p>However, there is a smart workaround using which you can use use this feature from any country.</p>   <p>Here are the steps:</p>  <ol>   <li>     <div>Sign in to Gmail/Google talk and Google Voice simultaneously. Yes, if you have a Google voice account, you can sign in from any country.</div>   </li>    <li>     <div>Click on the Call button in Google Voice.</div>   </li>    <li>     <div>In the dialog box that pops up, select “Google Talk” under “Phone to call with”:        <br />&#160;<img title="image" height="350" alt="image" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/09/image44.png" width="366" /> </div>   </li>    <li>     <div>Click Connect.</div>   </li>    <li>     <div>You will now get an anonymous call in Gmail or Google Talk (wherever you are signed in):        <br /><img title="image" height="169" alt="image" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/09/image45.png" width="231" /> </div>   </li>    <li>     <div>Answer the call. Google will now call the number you specified and connect you to the call.</div>   </li>    <li>     <div>Enjoy!</div>   </li> </ol>  <p>Thanks <a href="http://flavors.me/bharatjilledumudi" target="_blank">Bharat</a> for the tip!</p>
