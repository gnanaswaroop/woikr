<p>After being in news for all the <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Zuckerberg#Legal_controversies" target="_blank">controversies</a>, <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Criticism_of_Facebook" target="_blank">criticism</a>, <a href="http://www.forbes.com/profile/mark-zuckerberg" target="_blank">success</a>, a much-hyped potential-Oscar-winner movie biography and other <a href="http://500millionfriends.com" target="_blank">half a billion</a> reasons, Mark Zuckerberg has been named <a href="http://www.time.com/time/specials/packages/0,28757,2036683,00.html" target="_blank">TIME Magazine's Person of the Year for 2010</a>. The magazine wrote, "For connecting more than half a billion people and mapping the social relations among them; for creating a new system of exchanging information; and for changing how we all live our lives, Mark Elliot Zuckerberg is TIME’s 2010 Person of the Year."</p>
<p><img title="time_zuckerberg" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/12/time_zuckerberg.jpg" alt="" width="307" height="409" /></p>
<p>Zuckerberg joins the elite group of achievers including <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohandas_Karamchand_Gandhi" target="_blank">MK Gandhi</a>, <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adolf_Hitler" target="_blank">Adolf Hitler</a>, <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winston_Churchill" target="_blank">Winston Churchill</a> and <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Gates" target="_blank">Bill Gates</a>.</p>
