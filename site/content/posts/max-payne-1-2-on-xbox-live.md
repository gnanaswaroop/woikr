<p>I have a confession to make. I never played Max Payne. I saw the trailers, read the reviews, I even saw my friends playing it. But, I never played it myself. To add to the shame, I don’t seem to remember any reason for not playing it!</p>  <p>Anyway, when I logged into Xbox Live today, I was pleasantly surprised by 2 new Xbox Originals. Yes, Max Payne 1 &amp; 2 are now available on Xbox 360 for 1200 points!</p>  <p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/04/max-payne.jpg"><img title="Max_payne" height="300" alt="Max_payne" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/04/max-payne-thumb.jpg" width="211" /></a> </p>  <p>It actually is a good strategy by Rockstar and Microsoft. Just before the release of Max Payne 3, tempt people with Max Payne 1 &amp; 2. Get publicity for your game and earn money selling your 8 year old games!</p>  <p>Anyway, I’m not complaining. At least I won’t be the <em>“one who hasn’t played Max Payne”</em> any more.</p>  <p>[<a href="http://majornelson.com/archive/2009/04/27/new-xbox-originals-now-available-max-payne-and-max-payne-2.aspx" target="_blank">Announcement</a>]</p>
