<p>Just in time for the festivities, Microsoft India has launched the <a href="http://www.xbox.com/en-US/Xbox360/Consoles/Systems/Xbox3604gb" target="_blank">4 GB black Xbox 360</a> in India at a price tag of INR 14,990. This Xbox 360 model is similar in features to the <a href="/gaming/microsoft-launches-new-250-gb-xbox-360-console-in-india" target="_blank">previously launched 250 GB model</a> and is also <a href="http://www.xbox.com/en-US/kinect" target="_blank">Kinect</a> ready.</p>
<p>Here are the features of the newly launched console:</p>
<p></p>

<ul>
	<li>
<div>4 GB internal flash memory</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Touch sensitive controls</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Five USB ports</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Dedicated Kinect port</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Optical out port</div></li>
	<li>
<div>In built WiFi</div></li>
</ul>
<p>The older Arcade model is now available in India at a price tag of INR 11,990. As always, we would recommend checking the price in the grey market as these are MRPs and you can actually get them at lower prices in the grey markets without loosing on the warranty.</p>
