<p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/08/image57.png"><img title="image" alt="image" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/08/image_thumb33.png" width="150" height="240" /></a> </p>  <p>Finally, Microsoft has launched the black 250 GB Xbox 360 console in India at a price tag of INR 21,990. The company, in a <a href="http://www.microsoft.com/india/msindia/msindia_new250GB_xbox360.aspx" target="_blank">press release</a> on Thursday, August 26th 2010 announced that the new console will be available at all leading organized retail stores across India.</p>   <p>The new console has glossy black finish and is much better than its predecessors. Here are some of its key features:</p>  <ul>   <li>     <div>Built in Wi-Fi</div>      <ul>       <li>         <div>The new Xbox 360 comes with 802.11n Wi-Fi built in for a faster and easier connection to Xbox LIVE. Download games from Xbox LIVE Marketplace in 1080p and 5.1 surround sound from anywhere in the house. Compatible with b/g/n networks.</div>       </li>     </ul>   </li>    <li>     <div>250 GB HDD</div>      <ul>       <li>         <div>The internal 250GB hard drive allows you to save your games, music, pictures, trailers, levels, demos, and other con­tent available from Xbox LIVE Marketplace.</div>       </li>     </ul>   </li>    <li>     <div>Kinect ready with dedicated Kinect port</div>      <ul>       <li>         <div>Coming this holiday, Kinect (previously called <a href="/gaming/project-natal-from-microsoft" target="_blank">Project Natal</a>) will make YOU the controller. With a dedicated, specialized port, the Xbox 360 250GB is built to connect seamlessly with the Kinect sensor.</div>       </li>     </ul>   </li>    <li>     <div>5 USB ports, HDMI port &amp; Optical out port</div>   </li>    <li>     <div>Smaller form factor with&#160; quieter operation</div>   </li>    <li>     <div>Better cooling</div>   </li> </ul>  <p><img title="image" alt="image" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/08/image58.png" width="240" height="240" /> </p>  <p>The package contains the following items:</p>  <ul>   <li>     <div>Xbox 360 250 GB S console </div>   </li>    <li>     <div>Xbox 360 250 GB S removable hard drive </div>   </li>    <li>     <div>Xbox 360 black wireless controller </div>   </li>    <li>     <div>Xbox 360 black headset </div>   </li>    <li>     <div>Xbox 360 composite A/V cable </div>   </li>    <li>     <div>1-month Xbox LIVE Gold membership </div>   </li>    <li>     <div>1-year limited console warranty </div>   </li> </ul>  <p>At a price tag of INR 22,000, we would have also expected at least 6 months subscription to Xbox Live Gold and a HDMI cable (seriously, what’s the fucking point of bundling a composite cable and advertising the HD capabilities of a device?). A decent HDMI cable will cost you around INR 1000 in India. Considering that the same console is <a href="http://www.amazon.com/Xbox-360-250GB-Console/dp/B003O6JJKY/ref=sr_tr_3?ie=UTF8&amp;s=videogames&amp;qid=1282900377&amp;sr=8-3" target="_blank">available at $300</a> (approx. INR 14,000) in the US, we would say that Microsoft is ripping off Indian consumers. They can blab about import duties all they want, but a price difference of INR 8,000 is totally absurd. We would recommend you to wait for a few days for the console to be available in “unorganized” retail stores in India and save a few thousand bucks. Also, keep checking forums like <a title="http://www.consoul.in" href="http://www.consoul.in">http://www.consoul.in</a> as they usually run attractive deals.</p>  <p>However, if you cannot wait, you can buy it online at the <a href="http://www.microsoftstore.co.in/microsoft/822/product/Xbox-360-250-GB-Console.aspx" target="_blank">Microsoft Store</a>.</p>  <p></p>  <p>[via <a href="http://twitter.com/Xbox360India/statuses/22249695144" target="_blank">Xbox 360 India</a>]</p>
