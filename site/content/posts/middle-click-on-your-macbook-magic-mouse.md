If you are a browser junkie like me and have been missing out the Middle Click ever since you moved to a new Macbook (or got the <span>touchy</span> Magic Mouse), you have a reason to rejoice. I recently found a simple but smart project called <a href="http://clement.beffa.org/labs/projects/middleclick/" target="_blank">3-tap middle click</a>. It does exactly what it says. It maps the 3-finger tap gesture to  middle click on your Mac. It works perfectly with all applications.

So now you can use a 3-tap for opening a link in a new tab, to close tabs or whatever. All thanks to <a href="http://clement.beffa.org/" target="_blank">Clément Beffa</a>. Donate if you wish :)
