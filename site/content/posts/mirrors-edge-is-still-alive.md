Turns out what we heard <a href="/gaming/ea-kills-mirrors-edge" target="_blank">before</a> wasn't entirely true. Mirror's Edge is still alive!

EA's Frank Gibeau said in an interview with <a href="http://www.computerandvideogames.com/291633/news/ea-mirrors-edge-2-must-grow-franchise-audience/" target="_blank">CVG</a>:
<blockquote>"Not at all. We're just trying to figure out how to bring Mirror's Edge back and in what way. That's part of the creative development process. I know there are some stories about how EA killed Mirror's Edge. Well, I'm the guy; I greenlight the games. I did not kill it - as God is my witness.

What we said internally is: 'What do we need to do to make [a sequel] a hit?' We need to go from version one to version two so that it sells two to three times as many, it finds a much bigger audience. The first game from a quality standpoint was good, but now it needs to be great.

We're actively working on ideas in the Mirror's Edge universe, we just haven't locked yet on a way to bring it back and a way that fans will be excited, but at the same time we can get it to a bigger audience."</blockquote>
Phew. I'm so glad. Mirror's Edge is "Still Alive"!
