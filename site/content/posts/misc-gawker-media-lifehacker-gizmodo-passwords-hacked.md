<g:plusone size="medium"></g:plusone></li><li></li></ul><p>Gawker Media has&nbsp;<a href="http://lifehacker.com/5712785/faq-compromised-commenting-accounts-on-gawker-media" target="_blank">reported</a> that their servers have been compromised and the commenting user account’s passwords have possibly leaked. If you ever commented on any of the Gawker Media websites (<a href="http://lifehacker.com" target="_blank">Lifehacker</a>, <a href="http://gizmodo.com" target="_blank">Gizmodo</a>, <a href="http://gawker.com" target="_blank">Gawker</a>, <a href="http://jezebel.com" target="_blank">Jezebel</a>, <a href="http://io9.com" target="_blank">io9</a>, <a href="http://jalopnik.com" target="_blank">Jalopnik</a>, <a href="http://kotaku.com" target="_blank">Kotaku</a>, <a href="http://deadspin.com" target="_blank">Deadspin</a> and <a href="http://fleshbot.com" target="_blank">Fleshbot</a>)&nbsp;you should be worried.</p>
<p>Here is what you need to do:</p>
<ol>
<li>Go to &nbsp;<a href="http://www.slate.com/id/2277768/" target="_blank">http://www.slate.com/id/2277768/</a>, enter your email address in the provided widget and it will tell you whether your account has been compromised.</li>
<li>Irrespective of the result from Step 1, go to <a href="http://www.lifehacker.com" target="_blank">Lifehacker</a>, press Login at the top-right corner and reset your password, or login and change the password.<br>
<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2010/12/lifehacker_login.jpg" rel="lightbox[4855]"><img title="lifehacker_login" src="/wp-content/uploads/2010/12/lifehacker_login-300x137.jpg" alt="" width="300" height="137"></a></li>
<li>If you use the same email/password combination for multiple accounts, please go ahead and change the passwords on all your important accounts.</li>
</ol>
<p>An advice from us, please try to keep passwords different for different accounts. At least have sets of passwords for un-important and important accounts.</p>
