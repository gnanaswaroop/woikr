<p><a href="http://www.helpingdoc.com/">HelpingDoc.com</a> is a new service operating in Delhi/NCR enabling users to find and book appointments with doctors in their area. The site has tie ups with most doctors in Delhi/NCR and offers a comprehensive list of doctors available in an area with their schedule, consultation fee and detailed profile. A quick search on the site will tell you there are a lot more doctors in your area than you ever thought!</p>
<p>The appointment process is detailed as follows:</p>
<ol>
<li>Find a suitable doctor near you.</li>
<li>Look for the time slots available.</li>
<li>Book a time slot as per your convenience.</li>
<li>HelpingDoc will give you a call to confirm the appointment. They also call the doctor to confirm the appointment.</li>
<li>In case there are any changes in the doctor’s schedule, HelpingDoc will inform you over the phone to reschedule the appointment or choose another doctor.</li>
<li>In case you cancel/reschedule an appointment, they will also call you to take feedback and find out the reasons for the same.</li>
</ol>

<p>The service looks promising and covers most prominent doctors. The site is easy to use and gives you enough information to make a decision. It is extremely helpful in cases where you might want to get a second opinion.</p>
<p>We hope that all our readers stay healthy. But in case you do need to see a doctor sometime, <a href="http://www.helpingdoc.com/">helpingdoc.com</a> is worth a shot.</p>
