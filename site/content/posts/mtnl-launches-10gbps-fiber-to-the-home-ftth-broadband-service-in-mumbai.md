<p>State owned <a href="http://www.mtnl.net.in/">Mahanagar Telecom Nigam Limited</a> has announced the launch of high speed <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiber_to_the_x">FTTH</a> (Fiber To The Home) broadband service in the Mumbai circle with the Delhi circle launch to follow on 1st May 2011.</p>
<p>The service will enable consumers to enjoy high speed broadband (up to 10Gbps) over a self healing network of fiber optics. The network will have ample bandwidth to offer advanced services like <a href="/?s=IPTV&amp;x=0&amp;y=0">IPTV</a>, <a href="/?s=hdtv&amp;x=0&amp;y=0">HDTV</a>, 3DTV, video on demand , Bandwidth on Demand, Video conferencing etc.</p>
<p>Plans will start from INR 649 for limited usage. Unlimited plans are available for INR 999 for 1 Mbps, INR 3499 for 4 Mbps &amp; INR 5999 for 10 Mbps.</p>
