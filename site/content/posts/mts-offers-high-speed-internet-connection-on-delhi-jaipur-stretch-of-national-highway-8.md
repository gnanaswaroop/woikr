<p><a href="http://www.mtsindia.in/">MTS</a> is offering high speed mobile broadband connectivity on the Delhi - Jaipur stretch of National Highway 8. Users on this stretch will be able to enjoy high speed data based services like live TV, video on demand on their laptops and smart phones.</p>
<p>MTS says that they plan to extend their network to other National Highways in future.</p>
