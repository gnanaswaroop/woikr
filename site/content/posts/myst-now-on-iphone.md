Remember the good old days when point-and-click adventure games used to be awesome. If you do, you sure remember Myst. At that time, I was awestruck by it’s gorgeous artwork and amazing soundtrack. Now, you can experience all that on your iPhone.

It does come at a cost though. It’s a huge 727 MB download! Obviously, it’ll take up all that space on your iPhone as well. And yes it costs $5.99. I’m actually surprised since I was expecting the price to be around $10.

I personally don’t even mind removing all my songs and videos from the iPhone if the game is worth it :)
