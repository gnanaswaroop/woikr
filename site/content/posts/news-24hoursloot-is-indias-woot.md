<p align="justify"><a href="http://www.woot.com" target="_blank">woot</a> is a pretty famous website in the US which offers super awesome deals every day on one product. They sell refurbished &amp; new products at really cheap prices.</p>
<p align="justify">An Indian version of woot is here – <a href="http://www.24hoursloot.com/" target="_blank">24hoursloot.com</a> goes by the same concept. The idea is simple:</p>
<ul>
<li>
<div align="justify">They start selling a new product at midnight every day at amazingly cheap prices. </div>
</li>
<li>
<div align="justify">The item is available to order for the next 24 hours or till stocks last. </div>
</li>
<li>
<div align="justify">The item is delivered at your home. They do charge for shipping.</div>
</li>
<li>
<div align="justify">You do not have to sign up on the website to buy the product</div>
</li>
</ul>
<div align="justify"><span id="more-3725"></span></div>
<p align="justify">If you are fast enough, you can get amazing deals. Till now they have sold cool T shirts, stickers, posters and electronic items. And as the customer base grows up, we can expect to see much more variety of products on the site.</p>
<p align="justify">Although the deal of the day is unveiled at midnight, you can <a href="http://www.twitter.com/24hoursloot" target="_blank">follow them on twitter</a> or <a href="http://www.facebook.com/pages/24hoursLoot/469072140645" target="_blank">become a fan on facebook</a> to get a sneak peak at around 11 pm daily.</p>
<p align="justify">What’s more? They also have some contests running where you can win free t-shirts! Go to <a title="http://www.24hoursloot.com/win-t-shirts" href="http://www.24hoursloot.com/win-t-shirts">http://www.24hoursloot.com/win-t-shirts</a> to check them out.</p>
<p align="justify">
