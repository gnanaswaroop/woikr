<p style="text-align: justify;">Apparently, the folks in Beijing are too happy with the free wifi test runs during the mammoth Olympics of 2008. They are now gonna roll out the free wi-fi service all over Beijing and will cover it completely by 2011.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">According to the  <em>Beijing Business Today</em>, the plans for free Wi-Fi are being extended:</p>
<blockquote>
<p style="text-align: justify;">by 2011 Beijing will become the “largest wireless city in China”…</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">…At present there are pilot schemes for in the districts of Xuanwu, Chongwen, Chaoyang, Shijingshan, Daxing and Pinggu, in places associated with agriculture, local government offices, and scientific technology.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Furthermore, nearly half of Beijing’s top universities have wireless Internet: Tsinghua university&nbsp; alone has 500 hotspots.</p>
</blockquote>
