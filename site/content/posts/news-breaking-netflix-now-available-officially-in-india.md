<div>CES 2016: Netflix announced in a <a href="https://media.netflix.com/en/press-releases/netflix-is-now-available-around-the-world">press event at CES 2016</a> in Las Vegas that it is going global. The company said that starting immediately, it is available in almost all the countries of the world except a few (Crimea, North Korea, Syria &amp;&nbsp;China).</div>
<div></div>
<div><img fetchpriority="high" decoding="async" title="netflix-plans-pricing-India.png" src="/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/netflix-plans-pricing-India.png" alt="netflix-plans-pricing-India" width="571" height="248"></div>
<div></div>
<div>The plans for India start at INR 500 per month and go up to INR 800 per month. The pricing is similar to US pricing and pretty close to what you would get after converting the USD plan rates to INR. However, do not expect all the US content to be available in the Indian version. Netflix says it is still working to get distribution rights for many titles (including some of the original content the company has produced).</div>
