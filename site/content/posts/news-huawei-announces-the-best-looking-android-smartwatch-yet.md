<g:plusone size="medium"></g:plusone></li><li></li></ul><p>Huawei has announced its Android smartwatch at the Mobile World Congress and it is the best looking android a&nbsp;yet. Huawei claims that they surveyed various potential customers and based on their feedbck of the current smartwatch options in the market, they have come up with one that looks like a luxury watch and caters to what people are looking for in a smartwatch.</p>
<p>The watch has a round display and comes with some beautiful watchfaces which does give it the&nbsp;sophisticated look of a luxury watch. It works with Google Now to let you issue voice commands.</p>
