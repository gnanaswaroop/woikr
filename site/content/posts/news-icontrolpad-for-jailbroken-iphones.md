<g:plusone size="medium"></g:plusone></li><li></li></ul><p>Tired of using touch screen controls for games? Missing the buttons to control the character in yoru favourite iPhone game? Check out&nbsp;<a href="http://www.icontrolpad.com/" target="_blank">iControlPad</a>.</p>
<p><a href="/wp-content/uploads/2008/12/icontrolpad1.jpg" rel="lightbox[1895]"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1897" title="icontrolpad1" src="/wp-content/uploads/2008/12/icontrolpad1.jpg" alt="icontrolpad1" width="320" height="241" srcset="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/12/icontrolpad1.jpg 320w, /wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/12/icontrolpad1-300x225.jpg 300w" sizes="(max-width: 320px) 100vw, 320px"></a></p>
<p>This cool device fits around your jailbroken iPhone and adds control buttons which you can use to play games. Here is what the creators have to say:</p>
<blockquote>
<p>Emulators and games just not really working well enough with the touch screen?<br>
We didn’t think so either – so we built this little add on.<br>
Yeah. Realise the full potential of the iPhone by adding real controls.</p>
<p>Your iPhone just slides in and locks in to place via the serial port,<br>
there are no modifications needed and you can remove it at any time.</p>
<p>The iControlPad is already supported by some of the biggest iPhone devs (including ZodTT<br>
PSX, GBA emu etc.) and we are sending out units to several others.</p></blockquote>
<p><a href="/wp-content/uploads/2008/12/icontrolpad2.jpg" rel="lightbox[1895]"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1896" title="icontrolpad2" src="/wp-content/uploads/2008/12/icontrolpad2.jpg" alt="icontrolpad2" width="320" height="240" srcset="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/12/icontrolpad2.jpg 320w, /wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/12/icontrolpad2-300x225.jpg 300w" sizes="(max-width: 320px) 100vw, 320px"></a></p>
<p>The device is not ready to be shipped yet. Keep checking for updates.</p>
