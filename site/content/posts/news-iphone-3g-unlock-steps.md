<p>The <a href="http://blog.iphone-dev.org/" target="_blank">iPhone dev team</a> released the iPhone 3G unlock aka <a href="http://www.yellowsnow.com" target="_blank">YellowSnOw</a> on the new year. The initial version had a few hiccups for some users. However, they have released a new version which seems to be working fine for most users. The steps are broadly classified as:</p>
<ol>
<li>Jailbreak your iPhone</li>
<li>Install YellowSnOw from Cydia or Installer</li>
<li>Run it</li>
<li>Turn off your iPhone 3G and install your 3rd party sim</li>
<li>Switch on your iPhone</li>
</ol>
<p>The above steps are clearly illustrated by crunchgear in the article here:&nbsp;<a href="http://www.crunchgear.com/2009/01/01/how-to-unlock-your-3g-iphone/" target="_blank">http://www.crunchgear.com/2009/01/01/how-to-unlock-your-3g-iphone/</a></p>
<p><a href="/wp-content/uploads/2009/01/winpwn_success.png" rel="lightbox[1963]"><img class="alignnone size-medium wp-image-1964" title="winpwn_3G_success" src="/wp-content/uploads/2009/01/winpwn_success-300x264.jpg" alt="winpwn_3G_success" width="300" height="264" srcset="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/01/winpwn_success-300x264.jpg 300w, /wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/01/winpwn_success.png 700w" sizes="(max-width: 300px) 100vw, 300px"></a></p>
<p>Note that if you have an iPhone 3G under contract, unlocking it will void your warranty and the contract.</p>
