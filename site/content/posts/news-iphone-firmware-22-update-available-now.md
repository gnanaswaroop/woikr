<p style="text-align: justify;">The latest iPhone firmware update 2.2 is out now. Its a 246 MB download and if you have the latest iTunes installed, it should prompt you to download and install it the next time you tether your iPhone.</p>
<p><a href="/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/iphone22update.jpg" rel="lightbox[1777]"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1778" title="iphone22update" src="/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/iphone22update.jpg" alt="" width="499" height="250" srcset="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/iphone22update.jpg 807w, /wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/iphone22update-300x150.jpg 300w" sizes="(max-width: 499px) 100vw, 499px"></a></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Here is a quick list of new features in 2.2:</p>
<ol style="text-align: justify;">
<li>Google Maps Street View</li>
<li>Public transit and walking directions in Google Maps</li>
<li>Over-the-air podcast downloads</li>
<li>Mail enhancements</li>
<li>Safari tweaks</li>
<li>Improved sound quality for visual voicemail</li>
<li style="text-align: justify;">Fewer dropped calls (again)</li>
</ol>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><a href="/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/iphone222.jpg" rel="lightbox[1777]"><img class="alignnone size-medium wp-image-1779" title="iphone222" src="/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/iphone222-300x225.jpg" alt="" width="300" height="225" srcset="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/iphone222-300x225.jpg 300w, /wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/iphone222.jpg 537w" sizes="(max-width: 300px) 100vw, 300px"></a></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">There no MMS or copy and paste though :(.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><span style="text-decoration: line-through;">As usual, if you have a jailbroken or unlocked iPhone, DO NOT, I repeat, DO NOT update.</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">[Update 1st Dec 08] – We know this is old news now, but feel free to <a href="/apple/how-to-upgrade-and-unlock-your-iphone-2g" target="_blank">update to 2.2</a>. We at woikr are able to <a href="/apple/iphone-21-firmware-for-iphone-2g-working-with-airtel-without-unlock" target="_blank">use it without further unlocking</a>.</p>
