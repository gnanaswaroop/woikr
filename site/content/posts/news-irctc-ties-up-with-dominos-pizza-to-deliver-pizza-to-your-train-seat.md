<p><a href="http://irctc.co.in" target="_blank">Indian Railways Catering and Tourism Corporation (IRCTC)</a> has tied up with Domino’s Pizza in India to deliver pizza to your train seat.</p>
<p>The service is being run as a pilot project in 12 Indian cities – Agra, Alwar, Ambala, Jaipur, Jalandhar, Mathura, Muzzafarnagar, New Delhi,&nbsp;Pathankot, Vapi, Bharuch and Vadodara and is only available to passengers travelling in trains which do not have apntry car.</p>
<p>To avail this service, passengers will need to make a meal booking via IRCTC’s e-Catering service (<a href="http://ecatering.irctc.co.in" target="_blank">ecatering.irctc.co.in</a>,&nbsp;1800-1034-139 (Toll-free), 0120-4383892-99 (Toll) or&nbsp;SMS MEAL&nbsp;to 139) using their PNR number. Given that these pizzas have been added as standard menu items to the e-Catering menu, all the standard rules of the e-Catering service apply to this service as well.</p>
<p>IRCTC plans to extend the pizza menu to more cities gradually.</p>
