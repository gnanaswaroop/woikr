<p>Radio cab service provider Meru has launched its iOS and Android apps to enable users to book their cabs anytime and anywhere. The apps offer features like live map view, ICE alert and push notifications to give users more control on their cab bookings.</p>
<p>The overall process of booking the cabs is same as that on web. You select a time, pick up location and drop location to submit your request. In a matter of seconds, the app will let you know if your booking has been accepted or not.</p>
<p><img title="meru-app.png" src="/wp-content/uploads/2013/09/meru-app.png" alt="meru-app" width="227" height="362"></p>
<p>The best part about the apps is the ability to view the trip live on map. This will help you tell the driver the way to your home as you can see where the cab is in real time. You can also see the cab moving live in real time in the map view while sitting in the cab! Another great feature is the ability to specify emergency contact who can be automatically notified about the cab details as soon are you board it and start the trip. This can be used in case of emergencies.</p>
<p>Down the apps from here: <a href="https://itunes.apple.com/in/app/meru-cabs/id673009740?mt=8">iOS version</a>. <a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.winit.merucab">Android version</a>. Windows version is coming soon.</p>
