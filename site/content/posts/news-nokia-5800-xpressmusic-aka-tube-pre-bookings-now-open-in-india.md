<p>The much anticipated touchscreen phone from Nokia – the 5800 XpressMusic aka “<em>Nokia Tube</em>” is about to be launched in India soon. The phone is being touted as the <em>iPhone killer </em>with the following key features:</p>
<ol>
<li>3.2” Touch Screen Display</li>
<li>Surround Sound</li>
<li>8 GB Memory</li>
<li>High Resolution 3.2 MP Camera with flash</li>
<li>200 preloaded songs</li>
</ol>
<p>The features do sound promising, however, the success of the phone will primarily depend on the price. The rumored price is around 20k which seems appropriate for a phone with the above set of features. Anything more will ruin everything just as it did for the <a href="/news/iphone-3g-best-selling-handset-in-the-us-worst-sales-in-india/" target="_blank">iPhone 3G in India</a>.</p>
<p><a href="/wp-content/uploads/2008/12/nokia_5800_prebook_india.png" rel="lightbox[1912]"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1914" title="nokia_5800_prebook_india" src="/wp-content/uploads/2008/12/nokia_5800_prebook_india.png" alt="nokia_5800_prebook_india" width="264" height="357" srcset="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/12/nokia_5800_prebook_india.png 264w, /wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/12/nokia_5800_prebook_india-221x300.png 221w" sizes="(max-width: 264px) 100vw, 264px"></a></p>
<p>Nokia has started pre bookings for this phone in India. In case you are interested in buying one, head on to <a href="http://www.nokiaprebook.com/" target="_blank">http://www.nokiaprebook.com/</a> to claim yours as soon as its launched in here.</p>
<p>To explore the features of the phone, head on to <a href="http://www.xpressmusic.in/" target="_blank">http://www.xpressmusic.in/</a></p>
