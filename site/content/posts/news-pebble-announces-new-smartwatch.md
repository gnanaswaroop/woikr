<p><a href="http://www.getpebble.com">Pebble</a> – the company which built the first usable smartwatch has announced its next flagship product. Pebble Time – a next generation pebble with a colored screen and a redesigned operating system is now available via kickstarter.</p>
<p>Pebble started its journey with a successfully funded kickstarted campaign and has never looked back. They surely did not need another kickstarted campaign to get this project funded, but I guess they did this in nostalgia. The project surpassed its funding goal within&nbsp;17 minutes&nbsp;of going live on kickstarter.</p>
<p><a href="/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/dc375b9dc9d2d35f12bf995e561f713f_original.png" rel="lightbox[9663]"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-9668" src="/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/dc375b9dc9d2d35f12bf995e561f713f_original-600x224.png" alt="pebble time - design" width="600" height="224" srcset="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/dc375b9dc9d2d35f12bf995e561f713f_original-600x224.png 600w, /wp/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/dc375b9dc9d2d35f12bf995e561f713f_original-300x112.png 300w, /wp/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/dc375b9dc9d2d35f12bf995e561f713f_original.png 680w" sizes="(max-width: 600px) 100vw, 600px"></a></p>
<p>The new watch is thiner than its predecessors and has a curved design to better fit on your wrist. It also fits on most 22mm watch bands – so you are not limited to what the company offers.</p>
<p>The new interface follows a timeline approach to display information. You scroll forward or backward in time to get the info you need – whether its that score of the just ended game you want to check or the weather in the city you are traveling tomorrow.</p>
<p><a href="/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/a361fd85680fccb17de65c13aa3e73bd_original.png" rel="lightbox[9663]"><img class="alignnone size-large wp-image-9667" src="/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/a361fd85680fccb17de65c13aa3e73bd_original-600x271.png" alt="pebble time - colors" width="600" height="271" srcset="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/a361fd85680fccb17de65c13aa3e73bd_original-600x271.png 600w, /wp/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/a361fd85680fccb17de65c13aa3e73bd_original-300x135.png 300w, /wp/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/a361fd85680fccb17de65c13aa3e73bd_original.png 680w" sizes="(max-width: 600px) 100vw, 600px"></a></p>
<p>The watch will ship in May 2015 at a retail price of $199. Kickstarter backers can get it at a reduced price of $159 (gone as of this writing) and $179 (going fast). There are distributer level tiers also available for anyone who wants to buy them in bulk.</p>
<p>Here is the link to the <a href="https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/597507018/pebble-time-awesome-smartwatch-no-compromises?utm_campaign=ks2&amp;amp;utm_content=hero&amp;amp;utm_medium=web&amp;amp;utm_source=getpebble.com">kickstarter campaign</a>.</p>
