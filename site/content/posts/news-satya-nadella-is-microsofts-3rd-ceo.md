<p>Putting an end to the speculations, Microsoft has announced that Satya Nadella will be the 3rd CEO of the company succeeding Steve Ballmer.</p>
<p><img title="satya-nadella.png" src="/wp-content/uploads/2014/02/satya-nadella.png" alt="satya nadella" width="490" height="316"></p>
<p>Satya, born in Hyderabad, India is 46 years old and feels honored, humbled and excited with his new role. He has spent 22 years at Microsoft and feels that Microsoft has talent, resources and perseverance – everything that is required for a company to change the world.</p>
