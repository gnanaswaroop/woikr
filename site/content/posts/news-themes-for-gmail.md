<p>Gmail is getting better and better. This one is gonna blow you away – Themes. There were a few unofficial scripts available which let you do the same, but this is totally official and super cool. Plus the initial collection is awesome. Check it out:</p>
<p><a href="/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/gmail_skins_grid.png" rel="lightbox[1756]"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1757" title="gmail_skins_grid" src="/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/gmail_skins_grid.png" alt="" width="500" height="777"></a></p>
<p>As usual, they are slowly rolling out to all the users. Once its activated in your account, you will find a new “<a href="http://mail.google.com/mail/?shva=1#settings/themes" target="_blank">Themes</a>” tab under Settings. It will look like this:</p>
<p><a href="/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/gmailthemes.jpg" rel="lightbox[1756]"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1759" title="gmailthemes" src="/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/gmailthemes.jpg" alt="" width="500" height="276" srcset="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/gmailthemes.jpg 630w, /wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/gmailthemes-300x165.jpg 300w" sizes="(max-width: 500px) 100vw, 500px"></a></p>
<p>This is just in time for the holiday season. I hope they spring up some holiday based themes there. I personally like this one (Terminal – click for a larger view):</p>
<p><a href="/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/gmail_terminal.png" rel="lightbox[1756]"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1758" title="gmail_terminal" src="/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/gmail_terminal.png" alt="" width="500" height="272" srcset="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/gmail_terminal.png 1080w, /wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/gmail_terminal-300x163.png 300w" sizes="(max-width: 500px) 100vw, 500px"></a></p>
<p>Yeah yeah, I know. Its a bit unreadable, but hey, its the good old terminal :).</p>
