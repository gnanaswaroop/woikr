<p style="text-align: justify;">The popular torrents search engine <a href="http://www.torrentz.com" target="_blank">www.torrentz.com</a> domain has been taken over by some warez guys. If you try to open <a href="http://www.torrentz.com" target="_blank">torrentz.com</a> now, you will be taken to the following hacked page (from warez2share.com):</p>
<p><a href="/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/torrentz_screenshot.png" rel="lightbox[1720]"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1721" title="torrentz_screenshot" src="/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/torrentz_screenshot.png" alt="" width="500" height="243" srcset="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/torrentz_screenshot.png 537w, /wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/torrentz_screenshot-300x145.png 300w" sizes="(max-width: 500px) 100vw, 500px"></a></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Torrentz.com guys knew that someone was after their domain. They have been displaying the following message on the site since the last few days:</p>
<blockquote style="text-align: justify;"><p>Someone was trying to takeover our domain name with faked documents and a creative story. If you have trouble reaching our site please use <a href="http://www.torrentz.eu/" target="_top">www.torrentz.eu</a> as a backup.</p></blockquote>
<p style="text-align: justify;">If you are a regular user of torrentz.com, it is suggested that you use <a href="http://www.torrentz.eu" target="_blank">torrentz.eu</a> for sometime until they get their domain back.</p>
