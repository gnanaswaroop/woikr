<p>The latest addition to the iPhone and iPod suite of App store apps is the <a href="http://www.google.co.in/url?sa=t&amp;source=web&amp;ct=res&amp;cd=3&amp;url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.webex.com%2Fiphone%2F&amp;ei=xOVlSfiTH5yA6gOylPC9Bw&amp;usg=AFQjCNEBzOeFdj8gb1k_OIDQbvF_1mhkpQ&amp;sig2=B4mVD0wETqpufPNAQjXFrQ" target="_blank">Webex app</a> [<a href="http://itunes.apple.com/WebObjects/MZStore.woa/wa/viewSoftware?id=298844386&amp;mt=8" target="_blank">iTunes App Store Link</a>] from <a href="http://www.google.co.in/url?sa=t&amp;source=web&amp;ct=res&amp;cd=3&amp;url=http%3A%2F%2Fen.wikipedia.org%2Fwiki%2FCisco_Systems&amp;ei=TeVlScTEEpyi6gOC1siqBw&amp;usg=AFQjCNGQE6_aCBeTYJubthhYOPVtJehWOA&amp;sig2=OL0WM9pRa9E3j5Htt-pnQQ" target="_blank">Cisco</a>. The free app lets you attend any <a href="http://www.google.co.in/url?sa=t&amp;source=web&amp;ct=res&amp;cd=3&amp;url=http%3A%2F%2Fen.wikipedia.org%2Fwiki%2FWebEx&amp;ei=buVlSd2bC5DG6gP5ooWqBw&amp;usg=AFQjCNGNq_4sWZRIAchvseW4vpVHeXWd0Q&amp;sig2=Hki7iRWakI4Ryr-dFkEDOw" target="_blank">webex</a> meeting over 3G or wi-fi on your iPhone. The following features are available in a meeting:</p>
<ol>
<li>Simultaneous conference calls (not available on iPod touch)</li>
<li>Chat</li>
<li>Document sharing</li>
</ol>
