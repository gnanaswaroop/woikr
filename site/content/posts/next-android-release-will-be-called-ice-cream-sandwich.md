<p>We heard a <a href="/rumors/you-can-taste-android-ice-cream-this-summer-rumor" target="_blank">rumor</a> that the next Android release called Ice Cream will be coming this summer. But the truth is that it will be called Ice Cream Sandwich. At least that's what <a href="http://www.crunchbase.com/person/jason-kincaid" target="_blank">Jason Kincaid</a> of <a href="http://techcrunch.tv/omg-jk/" target="_blank">OMG/JK</a> was told by <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andy_Rubin" target="_blank">Andy Rubin</a>.</p>
<p>If you asked why, well they say that Froyo and Ice Cream aren't much different and they wanted to keep the both easily distinguishable. I think it's just that Andy Rubin loves Ice Cream Sandwich more. Or maybe the next release will be Jelly Sandwich. Or whatever. I'm over the whole dessert naming scheme.</p>
