<p>Almost 1.5 months back <a href="/android/nexus-one-to-get-gingerbread-soon" target="_blank">we heard</a> that Nexus One will be getting Gingerbread soon. Now finally, the official <a href="http://twitter.com/googlenexus/" target="_blank">Nexus One twitter account</a> finally <a href="http://twitter.com/googlenexus/status/16974464310845440" target="_blank">announced</a> that Nexus One will be getting Gingerbread update in a few weeks. Since the <a href="http://news.samdroid.net/2010/12/gingerbread-ported-to-nexus-one/" target="_blank">unofficial alternative</a> is still not stable, it's best to wait some more.</p>

<blockquote>The Gingerbread OTA for Nexus One will happen in the coming weeks. Just hang tight!</blockquote>
