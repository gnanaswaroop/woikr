<p>After being available for <a href="/mobile-phones/google-nexus-s-now-available-for-pre-order-in-india-for-27999">pre order</a> for some time on flipkart, Google's flagship branded <a href="http://www.google.com/nexus/#">Nexus S</a> mobile phone is now available in stores across India at a price tag of INR 29,590. In case you are the kind who does not believe in buying stuff online (even though its cheap), you can head over to your nearest brick and mortar mobile store to get your hands on this baby.</p>
<p>As always, the same thing is <a href="http://www.flipkart.com/samsung-google-nexus-s-mobile-mobcu43efesazke2">available online at flipkart.com for INR 26990</a>.</p>
<p>And in case you forgot, here is a quick run down of the phone's features:</p><div>
<ul>
<li>4-inch Super Clear LCD screen sporting a 480 x 800 pixel resolution</li>
<li>3G, EDGE/GPRS, Wi-Fi with DLNA capabilities</li>
<li>GPS with A-GPS support</li>
<li>Bluetooth 2.1 + EDR with A2DP, USB 2.0</li>
<li>5 megapixel camera with autofocus and LED flash</li>
<li>VGA front facing camera</li>
<li>16GB inbuilt storage</li>
</ul>
