Good news for all the owners of Nikon D40/D40x/D60 series of cameras! Nikon has launched a 35mm f1.8 prime lens with focus motor built-in. This means it'll auto-focus with your crippled cameras. The lens will be priced $199.95 tentatively.

<a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/02/nikkor35mm.jpg"><img class="size-medium wp-image-2137" title="Nikkor AF-S 35mm f1.8 prime " src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/02/nikkor35mm-300x255.jpg" alt="Nikkor AF-S 35mm f1.8 prime " width="300" height="255" /></a>

For simplicity, I'll just list out the reasons why this is such a big news. Here you go:
<ul>
	<li>Prime lenses are known to be very sharp and fast (read great for low light situations). Every photographer worth his/her salt must have one. (Don't ask me if I have one :P)</li>
	<li>At $200, it is the cheapest prime lens with motor available in the market. It is less than half the price of Sigma 30mm f1.4.</li>
	<li>At 200g, it is half the weight of Sigma 30mm f1.4.</li>
	<li>Earlier for this focal range the only option in prime lenses was Sigma f1.4, or maybe some Macro lenses. It gives you a choice!</li>
</ul>
That's more than enough to sign me up. Time to save money!
