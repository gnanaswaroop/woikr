<p>We talked about Nokia N9 before in context of the Nokia and Microsoft partnership. That probably isn't going to happen. And we are not sure when Nokia is planning to launch N9 too. Till then, you can always get the Chinese replicas!</p>
<p>They probably don't do one-tenth of what the Nokia phone will do, but they only cost $105. And they look pretty darn good from the pictures. They have a 3" touchscreen displays, dual-SIM support, front &amp; rear cameras, WiFi, Bluetooth and an FM radio. They also run a Symbian lookalike called MTK OS.</p>
<p>If you love showing off new fancy phones to your friends, find one and buy it.</p>
