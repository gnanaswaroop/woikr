<p>The much anticipated touchscreen phone from Nokia - <a href="http://WWW.xpressmusic.in/" target="_blank">XpressMusic 5800</a> aka '<em>Tube</em>' has been officially launched in India today (9-Jan-2009). The phone was launched in Mumbai by none other than the brand ambassador of Nokia - Priyanka Chopra. Nokia was <a href="/news/nokia-5800-xpressmusic-aka-tube-pre-bookings-now-open-in-india/" target="_blank">accepting pre bookings for the phone</a> since the last few days in India at  <a href="http://www.nokiaprebook.com/">http://www.nokiaprebook.com/</a>.</p>
<p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/01/nokia_5800_priyanka_chopra.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-medium wp-image-1994" title="nokia_5800_priyanka_chopra" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/01/nokia_5800_priyanka_chopra-300x225.jpg" alt="nokia_5800_priyanka_chopra" width="300" height="225" /></a></p>
<p>The phone boasts of the following features:</p>

<ul>
	<li>3.2 inch TFT Touchscreen - 360 x 640, 16m colors</li>
	<li>Accelerometer and Proximity sensor</li>
	<li>81MB internal memory and 8GB microSD included with package</li>
	<li>GPRS/EDGE, 3G with HSDPA, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth with A2DP and micro USB 2.0</li>
	<li>3MP AF camera with Carl Zeiss Optics</li>
	<li>GPS with A-GPS support</li>
	<li><span><span class="kLink"><span>Media </span></span><span class="kLink"><span>player</span></span></span><span> for</span> audio and video</li>
	<li>3.5mm earphones/TV out</li>
</ul>
<a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/01/nokia_5800_in_hand.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-medium wp-image-1995" title="nokia_5800_in_hand" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/01/nokia_5800_in_hand-294x300.jpg" alt="nokia_5800_in_hand" width="294" height="300" /></a>
<p>The official price of the phone is INR 19,990. The price seems to be perfect for a phone with the above set of features. At this price, this phone will give a tough competition to other touchscreen phones in India, especially the iPhone 3G which, even after the <a href="/news/iphone-3g-best-selling-handset-in-the-us-worst-sales-in-india/" target="_blank">embarrasing sales figures in India</a>, is selling at an <a href="/apple/iphone-in-india-official-price-announced/" target="_blank">insane price of 31k</a>.</p>
<p>Nokia is also running a promotion offer for this phone under which you can get <a href="http://www.sennheiser.com/sennheiser/home_en.nsf" target="_blank">Sennheiser</a> earphones worth INR 3000 by paying only INR 600.</p>
