<p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/08/image33.png"><img title="image" alt="image" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/08/image_thumb10.png" width="244" height="132" /></a> </p>  <p>Adam, the tablet by Indian startup <a href="http://www.notionink.in" target="_blank">Notion Ink</a> (started by IIT alumnus), has been priced at $498.</p>  <p>The $498 model will have WiFi, 3G, GPS, <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pixel_Qi" target="_blank">Pixel Qi</a> display and a Tegra 2 processor. </p>  <div></div>  <p>There will also be 3 cheaper models:</p>  <p><strong>$449</strong>: will not have 3G</p>  <p><strong>$449</strong>: will not have Pixel Qi display</p>  <p><strong>$399</strong>: will not have both the above</p>  <p>The tablet will be submitted for FCC approval in November and might launch in December this year or (highly likely) at the CES 2011.</p>  <p><em>Thanks <a href="http://flavors.me/bharatjilledumudi" target="_blank">Bharat</a> for the tip.</em></p>  <p>[via <a href="http://www.engadget.com/2010/08/12/notion-ink-adam-priced-at-498-lives-up-to-promise-of-being-bel" target="_blank">Engadget</a>]</p>
