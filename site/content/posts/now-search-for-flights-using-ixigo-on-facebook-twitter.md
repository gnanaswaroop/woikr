<p>Gurgaon based <a href="http://www.ixigo.com">iXiGO</a> travel search engine has launched an innovative way to search for flights on Facebook &amp; Twitter. All you have to do is visit their Facebook page at <a href="https://www.facebook.com/iXiGOsearch">https://www.facebook.com/iXiGOsearch</a> and post your query on the wall. For example- "Delhi to Bangalore on 14th Feb". Within seconds, iXiGo will reply to your wall post with the cheapest flight options for your query.</p>
<p>Twitter search works the same way where you have to send a public message addressed to <a href="http://twitter.com/#!/iXiGOsearch">@iXiGOsearch</a> followed by your query and you will get a public reply within seconds from the same account.</p>
<p>Socially searching for flights is probably the first of its kind with this approach and iXiGO seems to have implemented it pretty well with their natural language processor understanding different kinds of queries accurately. Its a lot of fun and a must try.</p>
