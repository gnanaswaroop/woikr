<p>If you are in Mumbai over this weekend (Feb 19 and 20), this is something for you. Orkut has started a new community called <a href="http://www.orkut.com/Community?cmm=111143149" target="_blank">Orkut Box Office</a> which, as per them, is the <em>"one stop shop for movie reviews, latest updates and free tickets".</em> While we are not asking you to believe them on that (neither do we), we actually don't mind any freebies as long as they are, well, free!</p>
<p>Here are the steps:</p>

<ol>
	<li>Visit <a href="http://www.orkut.com/boxoffice">www.orkut.com/boxoffice</a> (I know you killed your Orkut account, you must go and create one. Didn't you hear it properly - <em>free movie tickets</em>!)</li>
	<li>Post a comment/review/funny story there. Anything will do. Maybe even fraandship requests.</li>
	<li>They will send an email/scrap to you telling the day, date and time of the movie and instructions on how you can pick up your free tickets!</li>
	<li>Pick up your tickets from the movie cinema (PVR Juhu or Malad) at least 30 minutes before the show.</li>
	<li>Close your Orkut account and don't tell anyone about the above.</li>
</ol>
<p>PS: They say they will give tickets to early birds. That's obviously a vague statement but what's the harm in trying?</p>
