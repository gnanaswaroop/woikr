<p>The iPhone (and iPod Touch ofcourse) truly have become a major computing platform. Everyone these days seems to be launching the iPhone version of their popular dekstop/mobile apps.</p>
<p>The latest one to join the bandwagon is Adobe. They've launched a limited sorta edition (obviously) of Photoshop for the iPhone. It's called Photoshop.com for mobile (what's with the webapp-ish name?). According to Adobe it allows you to:</p>

<ul>
	<li>Crop, rotate, change color with just a touch.</li>
	<li>Give an extra glow with Soft Focus.</li>
	<li>Get artsy with Sketch.</li>
	<li>Apply one-touch effects like Warm Vintage and Vignette.</li>
</ul>
<p>All this with undo! (these marketing guys are becoming really cheap these days)</p>
