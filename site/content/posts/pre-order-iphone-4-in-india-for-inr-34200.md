<p>Looks like <a href="http://www.homeshop18.com/iphone/gsm-handsets/categoryid:3027/search:iPhone/">HomeShop18</a> has started taking pre orders for Apple iPhone 4 in India. They have put a price tag of INR 34200 for the 16 GB &amp; INR 40200 for the 32 GB model. Both the models are available in black &amp; white color. They are also charging INR 40 as shipping charges. The phone will be shipped to your address in 20 days. Thats probably in time with the planned Airtel &amp; Aircel launch.</p>
<p>Although the price is comparable to international prices, it might be worth waiting a few days to see what price Airtel &amp; Aircel launch the phone at.</p>
