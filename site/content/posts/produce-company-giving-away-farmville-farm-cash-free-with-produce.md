Green Giant Fresh, a produce company in the US is giving away free Farmville farm cash with select boxes of their produce. Farmville, the ultra popular social game on Facebook has been responsible for many such unprecedented crazy ideas.



The vegetable boxes will carry Farmville stickers as shown in the image above and can be redeemed for 5 free farm cash, with a limit of 3 redemptions per user.

Participating super stores include Target Stores (with Fresh Produce), United Supermarkets, Hen House, Price Chopper, Wal-Mart, Winn-Dixie, Sweetbay and Aldi.
