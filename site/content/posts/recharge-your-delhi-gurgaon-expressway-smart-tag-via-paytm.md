<p>Online recharge website <a href="https://www.paytm.com">paytm</a> is now offering online recharging facility for Delhi - Gurgaon expressway smart tags. All you have to go is enter your smart tag number and select a recharge amount in line with your plan and you can instantly recharge your smart tag online.</p>
<p>This will be super convenient for people commuting on the expressway on a  daily basis. In case you were looking for such a solution, hop on to <a href="https://www.paytm.com">paytm.com</a> to recharge.</p>
<p>No news on other expressway recharges yet, however, the UI looks as though paytm has plans to expand to other locations as well.</p>
