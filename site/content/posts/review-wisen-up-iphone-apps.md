<p><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-3270" title="wisen_up_1" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/wisen_up_11.png" alt="wisen_up_1" width="113" height="167" /><br>
When Apple says, "There's an app for just about anything", they really mean it. Consider <a href="http://www.globaldelight.com/iPhone/wisenup/index.html" target="_blank">Wisen Up</a>, an app best described as a collection of facts, trivia, myths and quizzes about medical science and human body. Now what use could this be to anyone? Well, it's a pretty cheap way to pass time when you can't beat that level and have no Internet to update your Twitter status.
<p>Wisen Up comprises of multiple sections that each feature flash cards containing a piece of trivia, fact or a question. Instead of trying to explain them, I'll try to give an example so you can decide on your own whether you want this.</p>

The sections are as follows:<br />
<ol>
	<li><span>Myths<br>
</span>Do you think shaved hair grow faster?<span><br>
</span></li>
	<li><span>Weird, Incurable diseases<br>
</span>Did you know that Face Blindness patients can't identify anyone by their facial features.<span><br>
</span></li>
	<li><span>Amazing Body Facts<br>
</span>If you yell for around 8.5 years, you can produce enough energy to heat a coffee mug.</li>
	<li><span>Types of Doctors</span><br>
Well this one is pretty self-explanatory.</li>
	<li><span>Name your Fear<br>
</span>Peladophobia is the fear of being around bald people!<span><br>
</span></li>
	<li><span>First Aid mistakes<br>
</span>Do NOT lean backwards when your nose bleeds. (Weird right? They say it can be dangerous.<span><span>)</span><br>
</span></li>
	<li><span>Personality Quizzes<br>
</span>Find out whether you are Inspector Clouseau from The Pink Panther.</li>
	<li><span>Body Language<br>
</span>Understand those gestures<span>.</span></li>
	<li><span>Ancient Cures<br>
</span>Apparently, cow dung can cure baldness.</li>
	<li><span>Be a Doctor<br>
</span>No, seriously!</li>
	<li><span>Funny Medical snippets<br>
</span>First the doctor told me the good news: I was going to have a disease named after me.</li>
	<li><span>Tell Me Why<br>
</span>Why do <span>we</span> you cry? The answer is in there. Really!</li>
</ol>
<p><span>Verdict</span><br>
As surprising as it is, I actually liked the app. Anything that provides me over an hour of light reading (and more if you want to take the quizzes) for less than a dollar is sure worth buying.
