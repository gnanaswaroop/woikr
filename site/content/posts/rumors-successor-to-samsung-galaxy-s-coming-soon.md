<p>It has been <a href="http://translate.googleusercontent.com/translate_c?hl=en&amp;sl=auto&amp;tl=en&amp;u=http://japanese.yonhapnews.co.kr/headline/2011/01/10/0200000000AJP20110110002900882.HTML&amp;rurl=translate.google.com&amp;twu=1&amp;usg=ALkJrhjWH51xCpZMw4qswSDTIwZkZR7F_w" target="_blank">reported</a> by an official Korean News agency that Samsung is about to launch the successor to their <a href="/android/samsung-sells-10-million-galaxy-s-phones-half-still-on-2-1" target="_blank">successful line</a> of Android based Galaxy S phones at Mobile World Congress in Barcelona next month. This probably explains the <a href="http://www.cellpassion.com/2010/12/28/samsung-discontinues-galaxy-s/" target="_blank">sudden disappearance</a> of Samsung Galaxy S from the Indian market. This is pretty much what happened last year, when Samsung discontinued <a href="/reviews/samsung-galaxy-spica-i5700-android-sans-frills-part-1-hardware" target="_blank">Galaxy Spica</a> just before launching the new series of Galaxy phones.</p>
<p>The successor will be called Galaxy S2 (GT-i9100). It is <a href="http://twitter.com/eldarmurtazin/status/3774614010335232" target="_blank">rumored</a> to be based on a dual-core processor, features a Super AMOLED Plus screen and runs Android 2.3 Gingerbread.</p>
<p>[via <a href="http://pocketnow.com/android/samsung-galaxy-s-successor-again-said-to-debut-at-mwc" target="_blank">Pocketnow.com</a>]</p>
