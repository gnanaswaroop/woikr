<p>Popular online music streaming service <a href="http://saavn.com/">Saavn</a> has added English music to its catalogue. This will enable Indian listeners to stream popular English tracks on their devices or browsers using the saavn app or website. This is the 6th language Saavn has forayed into after Gujarat, Hindi, Marathi, Tamil &amp; Telugu.</p>
<p>It is great news for Saavn users in India. This move is also expected to increase Saavn's subscriber base in India as the only other way to stream English music in India was to somehow get services like Pandiora, Sotify &amp; Rdio to work from Indian IP addresses.</p>
