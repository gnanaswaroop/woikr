<p>Online music streaming service <a href="http://www.saavn.com">Saavn.com</a> is about to launch its Pro service at the price of $3.99 a month. Pro users will be able to cache songs they want in their mobile apps for offline playback later. Saavn will also offer high quality 320kbps songs in the future to pro users.</p>
<h3>Features</h3>
<ul>
<li>Offline Listening: Available on up to 5 devices per account.</li>
<li>Ad-Free Listening</li>
<li>Sync multiple devices over cellular or wi-fi data connection</li>
<li>Pro Audio at 320 kbps (will be added later).</li>
</ul>
<div>The Pro services are primarily aimed at mobile customers and will be available from March 1st onwards. You can register in advance by going to pro.saavn.com.</div>
