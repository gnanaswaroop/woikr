<p>I've been waiting for this for so long now. Android is popular. <em>Very</em> popular. It was about time someone puts it in a Media Player and tries to compete with the iPod Touch. Samsung is doing exactly the same.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/12/galaxy-player.jpg"><img class="size-medium wp-image-5422 alignnone" title="galaxy-player" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/12/galaxy-player-300x300.jpg" alt="Samsung Galaxy Player" width="300" height="300" /></a></p>
<p>The newly announced media player (YP-GB1) carries the Samsung Galaxy brandname and looks exactly like the Galaxy S smartphone. Here are the specs:</p>

<blockquote>
	<li>4-inch WVGA (800x480) Super Clear LCD screen (Not Super AMOLED)</li>
	<li>1GHz CPU (Hummingbird I guess)</li>
	<li>8, 16 and 32 GB variants</li>
	<li>Runs Android 2.2 Froyo</li>
	<li>3.2MP rear camera and a front facing video-calling camera (iPod Touch has a low-res rear camera)</li>
	<li>9.9 mm thick (iPod Touch is 7.1 mm)</li>
	<li>T-DMB and SoundAlive audio enhancing technology</li>
	<li> Wi-Fi/Bluetooth 3.0</li>
	<li>GPS (Unlike iPod Touch)</li>
	<li>microSD card slot (Unlike iPod Touch)</li>
	<li>Removable Battery (Unlike iPod Touch)</li>
</blockquote>
<p>I have serious doubts about an Android based Music Player though. For one they don't have a Music Store yet. But one thing this will be a great device for testing Android apps for cheap.</p>
