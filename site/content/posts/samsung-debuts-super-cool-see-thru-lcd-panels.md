<p>Samsung electronics has done what no electronics company has done before. Mass produce transparent LCD panels. Just have a look at this:</p>
<p><img title="NewImage.png" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/03/NewImage39.png" alt="NewImage" width="515" height="385" /></p><p>Yes, this is a see thru LCD panel. Something that looks straight out of star trek (or Avatar). We will get to see them soon on the store shelves.</p>
<p>Apparently, Samsung has also developed some tech to trash the backlight and use the ambient light to view stuff on these displays. As most power consumption in a LCD is for backlighting, these panels consume 90% less (whoa!) power than (traditional ?) LCDs.</p>
