<p>After <a href="/rumors/samsung-galaxy-s2-image-leaked-rumor" target="_blank">all the rumors</a> all these days, Samsung has finally officially released the Samsung Galaxy S2. This one looks like the bigger brother of Galaxy S and doesn't match with the image we saw earlier but the specs (see ahead) look pretty much the same.</p>

<p><strong>Official Specifications</strong></p>

<ul>
	<li>4.3-inch 800x480 Super AMOLED Plus display</li>
	<li>1GHz dual-core CPU (Still not sure whether it's Samsung's own Exynos or the Tegra 2)</li>
	<li>Android 2.3 (Gingerbread) OS</li>
	<li>8 Megapixel Rear Camera and a 2 Megapixel front camera for video chat</li>
	<li>Gyroscope</li>
	<li>16 GB and 32GB variants</li>
	<li>NFC Capabilities</li>
	<li>8.49mm thick and only 116g!</li>
</ul>
<p>We also heard the rumors of NFC chip but that doesn't seem to be the case yet. While the specs are excellent, I personally think 4.3" is way too big for a cellphone. Even in my big hands the iPhone feels much more handy and usable than my 4" Nexus S. Your mileage may vary of course.</p>
