<p>While it's popular as the best Android Tablet launched yet, Samsung Galaxy Tab never impressed me much. I have always looked at it as Galaxy S phone with a <em>much</em> larger screen. But if these leaked specs turn out to be true it will be an excellent option for someone looking for a 7" tablet. Here are the leaked specs with my thoughts.</p>

<ul>
	<li>7 inch 2048×1200 Super-AMOLED display<br>
That resolution looks way too high! Imagine a Full HD 1080p video will have black bars on right on native resolution. Or, you will have to zoom and interpolate it. Also I can't imagine this kinda display costing the same amount as the old Galaxy Tab. It'll be awesome if Samsung actually pulls it off though.</li>

	<li>3D Display after upgrade to Honeycomb. (OMG! But frankly it's not something I'm a big fan of)</li>
	<li>Android OS 2.3 Gingerbread with a future upgrade to 3.0 Honeycomb (As expected)</li>
	<li>NVIDIA Tegra 2 processor (I thought Samsung might go for their custom CPU)</li>
	<li>Gorrila Glass screen (Nice!)</li>
	<li>8 Megapixel Rear camera with 1080p Video recording and Dual LED flash.</li>
	<li>3 Megapixel Front camera (Full HD Video chat!)</li>
	<li>USB Port (Excellent addition)</li>
	<li>64 GB Flash Memory, 1024 MB RAM and MicroSD Storage</li>
	<li>Accelerometer, Gyroscope, Compass and Barometer (Yeah Barometer! I hear it helps in more accurate weather prediction)</li>
	<li>Standard connectivity options including Wifi, 3G and Bluetooth</li>
</ul>
