Samsung Galaxy Tab was the only worthy competitor to the iPad in the last gen tablet wars. Yeah 6 months back is already last generation, welcome to the Android world. I have been expecting a lot from the next Galaxy Tab. While I would never buy it (I'm 100% pro-iPad when it comes to tablets), I am pretty sure that it will be a decent alternative for Apple-haters. Here are the specifications according to Samsung.

<strong>Specifications</strong>
<ul>
	<li>10.1-inch 1280x800 Super AMOLED display (No Gorilla glass)</li>
	<li>1 GHz Dual-core Tegra 2 processor</li>
	<li>Android 3.0 Honeycomb OS</li>
	<li>8Megapixel Rear camera with LED Flash and Full HD recording;  2 Megapixel front camera for video chat (I don't understand the concept of having High-res rear cameras on Tablets. I'd prefer if they just reduce the price)</li>
	<li>Dual surround sound speakers</li>
	<li>Awesome 6860mAh battery (at least on paper)</li>
	<li>246.2 x 170.4 x 10.9mm and 599 grams</li>
</ul>
No details on the <a href="/rumors/samsung-galaxy-tab-2-leaked-specs-are-delicious-tablets" target="_blank">previously rumored</a> features like barometer and USB port. If they launch it for $400, I may recommend it to people who hate Apple or love Flash or are crazy about Google/Android. Otherwise, it looks pretty average.
