<p>It's CES time and we expect a gazillion gadgets to be launched. We have decided to cover only the ones that we find interesting. Here is one that fits the boat - Samsung Infuse 4G.</p>
<p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/samsunginfuseside.jpg"><img title="samsunginfuseside" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/samsunginfuseside-300x295.jpg" alt="Samsung Infuse 4G" width="300" height="295" /></a></p>
<p>Samsung Infuse 4G, announced by AT&amp;T, is being touted as the thinnest Android Phone ever. But what I found rather interesting is the mega 4.5" Super AMOLED display. Let me make that clearer, MEGA 4.5" SUPER AMOLED DISPLAY! The phone also features a 1.2 GHz Hummingbird (Only Single Core ch-ch) display and an 8 Megapixel camera. The rest of the specs are pretty normal and not worth mentioning before it actually launches. The launch date or price haven't been disclosed yet.</p>
<p>[via <a href="http://androidandme.com/2011/01/phones/att-announces-the-thinnest-android-device-the-samsung-infuse-4g" target="_blank">AndroidAndMe</a>]</p>
<p><strong>UPDATE: </strong>We found out how thin is this thing actually. As per <a href="http://www.samsunghub.com/2011/01/06/samsung-infuse-4g-is-slimmer-and-superior-than-galaxy-s" target="_blank">SamsungHub</a> it is 8.99mm, almost a mm thinner from it's older brother i.e. the <a href="/android/samsung-sells-10-million-galaxy-s-phones-half-still-on-2-1" target="_blank">Galaxy S</a>. It's also over 0.2mm thinner than the <a href="/mobile-phones/lg-optimus-black-the-worlds-thinnest-android-with-nova" target="_blank">LG Optimus Black</a> which is also claiming to be the thinnest phone ever. Just to compare it's also 0.3 mm thinner than the iPhone 4.</p>
<p>I guess we can officially give it to Samsung :)</p>
