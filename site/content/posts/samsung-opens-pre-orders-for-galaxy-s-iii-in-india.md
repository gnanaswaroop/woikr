<p>Samsung has opened pre booking for its latest Galaxy phone, the S III in India via its online store <a href="https://samsungindiaestore.com/Products/ProductDetails?X00O000V1764UtGkkXXX10O14Z872395030agsft5f6dhdj557jkslf3627fhd3g3gh34dsrie378037hU7hu9pppTY/ct/BAy9vkm2FMvd7DOHvBxJIIIIX987fs62h0yr9df4g380ty0tt32r45en674Oty00ui0">samsungindiaestore.com</a>. One has to pay INR 2000 to book a Galaxy S III. Samsung will send a coupon of the same amount to the customer which can be redeemed against the full payment of the phone when its launched for sale (the rumored date is 31-May-2012). Furthermore, users buying the phone within 3 days of the launch will also get a bluetooth headset worth INR 2900 free with the phone.</p>
