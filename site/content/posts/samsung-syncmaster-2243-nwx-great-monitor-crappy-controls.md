<p>The Samsung SyncMaster 2243 NWX is one of the popular 22'' monitors in the market. It is well placed above its very worthy competitors from LG &amp; Viewsonic. Its a great monitor for all home based purposes like watching movies, playing games and surfing the Internet.</p>

<h3>Specifications</h3>
	<li>22” wide viewable area. (55 cm)</li>
	<li>1680 x 1050 max resolution.</li>
	<li>8000:1 contrast ratio</li>
	<li>5 ms response time.</li>
	<li>Magic Color Chip</li>
	<li>Magic Brightness</li>
	<li>Standard RGB connectivity (No DVI)</li>
The package comes with a RGB cable and a table top stand. The monitor can also be mounted on the wall to save desk space.
<div><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/09/samsung_2243_nwx.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-medium wp-image-1047" title="samsung_2243_nwx" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/09/samsung_2243_nwx-265x300.jpg" alt="" width="265" height="300" /></a></div>
<p>The monitor looks great thanks to the pitch black screen. The 5 ms response time is good enough for watching movies or playing games. However, a response time of 2 ms would have added more value to the package. The max resolution is near Full HD. Watching movies off DVDs is great on this one. To watch HD videos, you need to couple this up with a powerful graphics card which can handle 30 fps at such high resolutions without tearing the frame.</p>

<div><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/09/samsung_2243_nwx_side_view.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-medium wp-image-1046" title="samsung_2243_nwx_side_view" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/09/samsung_2243_nwx_side_view-147x300.jpg" alt="" width="147" height="300" /></a></div>
<p>The monitor can be rotated almost 360 degrees without moving or rotating the base. This is helpful if your desk is in a corner - usually the case with home PCs. However, the monitor has a large viewing angle so you won't have to keep rotating it.</p>

<div><img class="alignnone size-medium wp-image-1044" title="samsung_2243_nwx_wallpaper_2" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/09/samsung_2243_nwx_wallpaper_2-300x300.jpg" alt="" width="300" height="300" /></div>
<p>Everything looks great on this monitor. Its a different experience all together to work on a (relatively) large screen. You have so much more space where you can keep much more windows. Apps like Gmail and Google Reader will look awesome at these resolutions and the wide aspect ratio (16:10) serves as cherry on the cake.</p>
<p>The monitor scores well in looks section, Its the most symmetrical monitor out there. It will give your desk a trendy as well as an elegant look.</p>

<div><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/09/samsung_2243_nwx_controls.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-medium wp-image-1049" title="samsung_2243_nwx_controls" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/09/samsung_2243_nwx_controls-270x300.jpg" alt="" width="270" height="300" /></a></div>
<p>Sadly, to improve the looks, Samsung probably completely ignored usability. The controls are at the bottom right corner of the screen on the front. But they are not visible to naked eyes. Only when you put your head dead close to the screen, you will be able to spot the "touch" controls. It is almost impossible to control this thing. To make matters worse, the "Power on/off" control is right next to the "Auto Adjust" control. So every time you try to reach out to switch it off, you end up auto adjusting it. To understand what I mean, click on the above image to enlarge it and look hard at the bottom right end of the screen. You will be able to spot the almost invisible controls.</p>

<h3>Conclusion</h3>
<p>All in all, even with crappy controls, this is a great monitor. At the end of the day what matters is the display quality and the looks - and it scores well in both the categories. The monitor is available for <strong>approx 12500 INR</strong> at Nehru Place. At this price, it is definitely a value for money option which also delivers great performance. Additionally, Samsung monitors are known for their maintenance free operation. And even if you need it any time, Samsung's on site service kind of rocks.</p>
