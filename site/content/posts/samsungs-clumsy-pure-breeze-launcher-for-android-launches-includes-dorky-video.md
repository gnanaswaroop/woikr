<p>If you are one of those 3 people who like Samsung's Android customizations but have an HTC phone, here is something for you. Samsung has launched their <em>next gen</em> launcher called "Clumsy Pure Breeze Launcher" in the Android Market. Ok, I added clumsy.</p>
<p>The only thing worse than the name is the dorky promotion video.</p>
<p>httpv://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nRwaR4GyLmU</p>
<p>Some ideas like the Kite sound interesting but I'm not sure if it will actually improve your productivity. If you are still interested, the <a href="https://market.android.com/details?id=com.samsung.smcl.spblauncher" target="_blank">launcher costs $2</a> on Android Market. The <a href="https://market.android.com/details?id=com.samsung.smcl.spblauncherlite" target="_blank">Lite version</a> limits the number of things you can organize.</p>
<p>Personally, I love <a href="http://www.launcherpro.com/" target="_blank">LauncherPro</a> for it's simple, clean UI and brilliant performance. <a href="http://jbthemes.com/anderweb/" target="_blank">ADW</a> and <a href="http://zeam.org/" target="_blank">Zeam</a> are other good alternatives.</p>
