These 3D glasses will be showcased in the upcoming CES 2011. Can’t wait to try it on!</p>  <p>[Via <a href="http://www.samsunghub.com/2011/01/03/samsung-creates-worlds-lightest-designer-3d-glasses/" target="_blank">SamsungHub</a>]</p>
