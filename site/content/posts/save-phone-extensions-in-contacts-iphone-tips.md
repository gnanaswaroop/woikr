<p>Do you have to call a lot of phone numbers where you have to dial an extension? Do you have many friends with desk extensions? Then this tip is for you. There is a way you can save a contact's extension in your iPhone and it will dial it for you when you call that contact. Here is how:</p><ul>
<li>Edit the contact you want to add the extension for</li>
<li>Tap the number you want to modify</li>
<li>Tap the "+*#" button on the dial pad. This will change the dial pad to show some symbols</li>
<li>Tap the pause button as shown above. This will add a comma after the number</li>
<li>Add the extension after the comma at the end of the number and hit save</li>
</ul>
<p>Done! Next time you call this contact from your iPhone, it will dial the main number, give a small pause after the line is connected and then dial the extension, connecting you directly to the person you want to talk to.</p>
<p>You can insert multiple pauses, so you can also use this trick to store the sequence of digits that will take you directly to a customer care executive of your bank or any other service provider.</p>
