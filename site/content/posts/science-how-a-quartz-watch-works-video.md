<g:plusone size="medium"></g:plusone></li><li></li></ul><p style="text-align: justify;">Ever wondered how a simple Quartz watch works? It requires extremely accurate engineering to create a tiny tuning fork that creates vibrations that are measured to show time. And how does the Tuning fork vibrate? Reverse Piezo-electric effect – generating pressure using electricity. Curious?&nbsp;This is arguably the coolest video you’ll see all day.</p>
