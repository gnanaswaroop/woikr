<p>While browsing through the website of <a href="http://tenonedesign.com">Ten One Design</a> - makers of <a href="/gaming/a-tactile-joystick-for-your-ipad">fling tactile joystick for iPad</a>, I came across this neat piece of iOS app - <a href="http://itunes.apple.com/us/app/autograph/id339423436?mt=8#">Autograph</a>.</p>
<p>Autograph is an iOS app (<a href="http://itunes.apple.com/us/app/autograph/id339423436?mt=8#">currently free</a>) which lets you sign on your iOS device and then send the signature image to any of your computers on the same network running the free <a href="http://tenonedesign.com/autographmobile.php">Autograph Helper application</a>. You can then use the signature image in any document (MS word, Pages etc.).</p><p>Autograph is also available as a <a href="http://tenonedesign.com/autograph.php">Mac app for $6.95</a> which lets you capture signatures on your Mac using the multi touch trackpad. However, the iOS app offers the same functionality via your iOS device and is currently <strong>free for a limited time</strong>. And something about signing on my iPhone/iPad and sending it wirelessly to my mac makes me feel good :). Also, using the iOS app, you can send signatures to your windows computers on the same network.</p>
<p>The app is a universal binary for iPhone and iPad. So it runs natively on the iPad providing a much larger area for your signature.</p>
