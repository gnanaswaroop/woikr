<p>Now this is cool. Sony has issued a press release claiming that it has successfully tested a prototype system to transmit power without any wires. The prototype setup like this:</p>

<img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-3292" title="image_thumb12_400x442.shkl" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/image_thumb12_400x442.shkl.jpg" alt="image_thumb12_400x442.shkl" width="400" height="442" />
<p>The system can push 60 watts of electrical energy over 20 inches and can be extended using passive extender units. Pretty cool.</p>
<p>Judging by the image on the TV, it looks like it will take some time for this prototype to materialize in an actual product and reach the markets.</p>
