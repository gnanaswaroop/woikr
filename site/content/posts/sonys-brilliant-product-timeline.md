Talk about Sony and different people start thinking about different things. Your parents will think about top notch TVs, your bass freak cousin will think about music systems (remember those Sony <em>decks</em>), your <em>foreign-return-uncle</em> will think about camcorders, your MBA brother will think about Vaio laptops, your little sister will think about mobile phones and I (in the presence state of mind) will think about PSP and PS3.

If you think of it Sony has created some of the most amazing gadgets in the last few decades. And while doing so it has converted so many normal human beings into gadget freaks. So hats off to Sony, if it wasn’t for you, gadget freaks like me would probably never exist.

BTW, if you’re confused why I’m all touchy-feely about Sony today, it’s because of this brilliant <a href="http://gizmodo.com/5245132/sony-product-timeline-is-a-glorious-gadget-history-lesson" target="_blank">Sony product timeline</a> that I just saw at <a href="http://gizmodo.com/" target="_blank">Gizmodo</a>. Thanks guys, for reminding me how much I used to love Sony once!
