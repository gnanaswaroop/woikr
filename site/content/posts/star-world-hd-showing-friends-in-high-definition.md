<p>This is super awesome news for fans of the incredibly popular TV series <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friends">Friends</a>. Star Wold HD is airing the True High Definition version of the show in India starting right from Season 1. Since the show started well before there were HD cameras, it looks like the studio went back to the original raw video tapes and edited them back in HD. Pretty awesome effort I must say.
