<p>The <a href="/tag/gmail/">Gmail</a> team has launched what should have been a standard Gmail feature since its inception. An option to import your mail and contacts from accounts like Yahoo!, Hotmail, AOL, and other <a href="http://mail.google.com/support/bin/answer.py?answer=117173" target="_blank">buttload of webmail services</a>.</p>  <p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/05/gmailimport1.jpg"><img title="gmail-import1" height="110" alt="gmail-import1" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/05/gmailimport1-thumb.jpg" width="534" /></a>&#160; </p>  <p>There is also an option to keep fetching this data for 30 days. So that you can test drive Gmail and make up your mind.</p>  <p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/05/gmailimport2.jpg"><img title="gmail-import2" height="124" alt="gmail-import2" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/05/gmailimport2-thumb.jpg" width="530" /></a></p>  <p></p>  <p></p>  <p>This should make it easy to convince friends and family members to switch to Gmail, especially those who, for some unknown reason, are madly in love with Yahoo! mail.</p>  <p>[via <a href="http://gmailblog.blogspot.com/2009/05/import-your-mail-and-contacts-from.html" target="_blank">Official Gmail Blog</a>]</p>
