<p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/03/image11.png"><img title="image" alt="image" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/03/image_thumb10.png" width="240" height="124" /></a> </p>  <p>The popular Mexican fast food giant <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taco_Bell" target="_blank">Taco Bell</a> is now in India and has opened its first outlet in Bangalore. They plan to open more outlets in other locations in India in the coming months.</p>  <p>The company will put a touch screen self ordering kiosk in its outlets for all the tech savvy customers.</p>   <p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/03/image12.png"><img title="image" alt="image" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/03/image_thumb11.png" width="244" height="202" /></a> </p>  <p>They have an amazing veg &amp; non-veg <a href="http://tacobell.co.in/tacomenu.aspx" target="_blank">menu</a> which they claim is “Craveable – the kind of food you just cant say ‘no’ to”. They also have free refills for all Pepsi products!</p>  <p>The first outlet is located in Bangalore at the following address:</p>  <blockquote>   <p>Taco Bell</p>    <p>UG 49, Ground Floor</p>    <p>Malleswaram, Sampige Road</p>    <p>Mantri Square, Bangalore</p> </blockquote>  <p>[via <a href="http://tacobell.co.in" target="_blank">tacobell.co.in</a>]</p>
