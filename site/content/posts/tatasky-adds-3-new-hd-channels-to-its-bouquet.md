<p>TataSky has added 3 new HD channels to its HD gold pack. The new channels are Star Movies HD, Star World HD and Star Gold HD. <a href="/home-entertainment/dish-tv-launches-30-new-hd-channels-in-india">Unlike Dish TV</a>, these are real HD channels &amp; not upscaled ones. They have 1080i, 16:9 video &amp; Dolby Digital sound. Moreever, their programming is also different from their SD counterparts.</p>
<p><img title="star-gold-hd-logo.png" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/05/star-gold-hd-logo.png" alt="star gold hd logo" width="95" height="50" /><img title="star-movies-hd-logo.png" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/05/star-movies-hd-logo.png" alt="star movies hd logo" width="95" height="50" /><img title="star-world-hd-logo.png" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/05/star-world-hd-logo.png" alt="star world hd logo" width="95" height="50" /></p>
<p>This is awesome news for all TataSky HD subscribers as they now have 6 true HD channels to watch. The others are National Geographic HD, Discovery HD World &amp; a cricket HD channel (depending on the series going on).</p>
<p>If you have a <a href="/home-entertainment/tatasky-launches-indias-cheapest-hd-set-top-box">TataSky HD</a> or <a href="/home-entertainment/tatasky-hd-launched-in-india-at-a-price-of-inr-3990">HD+</a> box, hop over to channel 302 for Star Gold HD, 342 for Star Movies HD &amp; 202 for Star World HD now.</p>
<p><strong><span><img title="star-plus-hd-logo.png" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/05/star-plus-hd-logo.png" alt="star plus hd logo" width="95" height="50" /></span></strong></p>
<p><strong>UPDATE</strong>: Good news. It looks like Star Plus HD has also been upgraded to true HD transmission (1080i, 16:9) as opposed to upscaled one as it was for the last few months. Now we all can watch saas bahu serials in HD!</p>
