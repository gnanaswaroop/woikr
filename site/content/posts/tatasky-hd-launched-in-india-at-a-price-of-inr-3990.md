<p><a href="/home-entertainment/tatasky-about-to-launch-hd-pvr-tatasky-hd-in-india">We told you a few days back</a> that TataSky was all set to launch their HD PVR set top box in India. Today, TataSky has launched their advanced HD PVR set top box at a competitive price of INR 3999 in India. The price is <a href="http://tatasky.hungamatech.com/2010/plushd/cost.html">inclusive of installation charges</a>. Here is how it looks like:</p>
<p><img title="NewImage.png" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/02/NewImage2.png" alt="NewImage" width="580" height="435" /></p><p>TataSky claims that TataSky+ HD is the most advanced HD PVR set top box in India. Here are some of its features:</p>
<ul>
<li>HD 1080i Picture Quality</li>
<li>Dolby Digital Plus Sound with 7.1 channel output</li>
<li>Record HD shows from Mobile and Internet</li>
<li>500 GB hard disk (capable of recording 625 hours of content)</li>
<li>Pause + Record + Rewind Live TV</li>
<li>Series Link</li>
<li>Dual Access Remote Recording</li>
<li>TV on Demand</li>
<li>Auto Standby</li>
</ul>
<p>Your monthly charges will increase by INR 30 per month as you will have to subscribe to the HD Gold pack to watch (and record) HD content.</p>
<p>Oddly enough, there is still no mention of the ports and cables in the set top box on the official website of TataSky+ HD with an even odd url of <a href="http://tatasky.hungamatech.com/2010/plushd/index.html">http://tatasky.hungamatech.com/2010/plushd/index.html</a>. I have no clue why this website is hosted on hungamatech.com and not tataskyplushd.com or something more logical.</p>
<p>However, if you are among the loyal subscribers of TataSky+ waiting for this, you can pre order one here: <a href="http://tatasky.hungamatech.com/2010/plushd/book-tata-sky-now.html">http://tatasky.hungamatech.com/2010/plushd/book-tata-sky-now.html</a>.</p>

You will get a callback within 15 minutes and they will arrange someone to give you a call to place the order within 24 hours using a credit card.</p>
