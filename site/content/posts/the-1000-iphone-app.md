We finally have a proper iPhone app that costs more than a frickin MacBook! Yes, we remember the <a href="http://gizmodo.com/5033621/99999-iphone-app-i-am-rich-probably-only-applies-to-developer" target="_blank">"I am Rich" app</a> but let's face it, it was only meant for show-off and lasted only a few days before Apple pulled it out.

<a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/01/500x_barmax.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-3642" title="500x_barmax" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/01/500x_barmax.jpg" alt="" width="500" height="237" /></a>

BarMax was designed by Harvard lawyers to help law students pass the Bar exam. It consists of a full 2 month course including audio lectures, practice tests and more. As of now, it's meant only for the California Bar exam but <a href="http://www.barbri.com/wps/portal/barbri/home" target="_blank">BarBri</a> plans to launch the app for other popular states by the end of this year. In case you want to try before spending that hard earned money (duh!) there is a <a href="http://itunes.apple.com/in/app/barbri/id326863682?mt=8" target="_blank">free trial version</a> of the app.

And just FYI, the second most expensive app isn't too far off. It's a video surveillance app called <a href="http://lextechlabs.com/ira_pro" target="_blank">iRa Pro</a> and will cost you $899.99!
