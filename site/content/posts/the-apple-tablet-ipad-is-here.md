But here you go - It features....<br>


The Hardware
<ul>
	<li>Has a 10’ inch High resolution LED screen with a 1024x768 resolution.</li>
	<li>1 GHz Apple A4 series processor – designed by Apple to give the best performance for multi touch features and the awesome games.</li>
	<li>Multi-touch – Not like those stupid Windows tablets we’ve been seeing, the real deal – with as good sensitivity as the iPhone.</li>
	<li>A great battery life with 10 hours of use and 1 Month of standby.</li>
	<li>Built in speakers and the “30 pin” Apple exclusive pin for connecting 3rd party docs/camera kits</li>
</ul>
The Software
<ul>
	<li>Runs 140,000 Applications the <a href="http://www.apple.com/ipad/app-store/" target="_blank">iPhone Applications</a> from the App store in Full screen, Of course there is an <a href="http://www.apple.com/ipad/sdk/" target="_blank">iPad SDK</a> for developing specifically for the iPad.</li>
	<li>Comes with iBookStore where one can go download e-books. Suck on this the <a href="http://Amazon.com/Kindledx" target="_blank">e-book readers</a>.</li>
	<li>Does iWork – the office suite – specially created for the iPad’s with multi gesture easy use. It heavy on your pocket with 9.99$ each app (it sucks here, we agree, but hey there’s Google Docs).</li>
	<li>Has got the usual Youtube, Safari, Maps, iPod applications for your entertainment.</li>
</ul>
Comes in 2 models – The normal one is with Wi-Fi (802.11 a/b/g/n) and the extended model comes with 3G.
