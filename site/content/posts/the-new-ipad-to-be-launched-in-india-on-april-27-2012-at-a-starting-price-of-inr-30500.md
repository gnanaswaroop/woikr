<p><a href="http://www.google.com/url?sa=t&amp;rct=j&amp;q=new%20ipad%20site%3Awoikr.com&amp;source=web&amp;cd=1&amp;ved=0CD0QFjAA&amp;url=http%3A%2F%2Fwoikr.com%2Fipad%2Fthe-new-ipad-with-retina-display-is-here%2F&amp;ei=vU2MT7XbCO-SiAeHicDdCQ&amp;usg=AFQjCNFLyql5EK9J7bTMjkqg1vkcsbMWyg&amp;sig2=kyd1lhXtE3EoalgvAjcgEQ">The new iPad</a> with the awesome retina display and quad core graphics will be available in India on April 27, 2012 at a starting price of INR 30,500 says <a href="http://www.apple.com/in/ipad/">Apple India's website</a>. It will be available at all apple authorized resellers. As per the <a href="http://www.apple.com/pr/library/2012/04/16New-iPad-Arrives-in-South-Korea-11-Additional-Countries-This-Week.html">press release</a>, the prices of all the models will be as follows:</p>
<p><strong>WiFi only:</strong></p>
<p>16GB: INR 30,500</p>
<p>32GB: INR 36,500</p>
<p>64GB: INR 42,500</p>
<p><strong>WiFi + 4G:</strong></p>
<p>16GB: INR 38,900</p>
<p>32GB: INR 44,900</p>
<p>64GB: INR 50,900</p>
<p>The iPad 2 will be available at a lower price of INR 24,500 for 16GB WiFi only model and INR 32,900 for 16GB WiFi + 3G model. Following the trend worldwide, the 32GB and 64GB models of the iPad 2 will be discontinued.</p>
