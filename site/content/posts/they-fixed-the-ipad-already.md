<a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/03/hero_20100307.png"></a><a href="http://www.pcworld.com/article/191563/apple_quietly_fills_in_some_ipad_blanks.html" target="_blank">PCWorld</a> has some details about the soon-to-be-launched iPad. With a device like iPad, maybe fixed is a bit too optimistic, but Apple surely has addressed some issues.
<ul>
	<li>iPad will support MS Exchange server.</li>
	<li>iWork app will support exporting to MS compatible office file formats.</li>
	<li>iPad will have a volume control and a screen orientation lock button.</li>
	<li>iPad will support the Motion JPEG AVI format. So those movies you take from your digital cameras will work right out of the box.</li>
</ul>
If any of these resolves any of your doubts wait till April 3 or just pre-order!

But mind you, still no flash, still no video conference support. Like you even care :)
