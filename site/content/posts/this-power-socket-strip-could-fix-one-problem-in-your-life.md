<div>If anyone tells me they haven't had issues with the Power Socket strips, I'd refuse to believe them. None of the chargers/plugs look alike. Some bulge to left, some to right and some are wide enough to cover up the left and right both.</div>
<div><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/12/power_strip_design_1.jpg"><img title="power_strip_design_1" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/12/power_strip_design_1.jpg" alt="" width="600" height="317" /></a></div>
<div>Cheng-Hsiu Du and Chyun-Chau Lin have clearly found a perfect solution to the frustrating problem - "Rotating 360". This power strip allows each of the socket to be rotated around the strip's axis to take care of the protruding plugs. It's arguably one of the smartest design ideas I've seen. Look ahead for description and concept pictures.</div>
