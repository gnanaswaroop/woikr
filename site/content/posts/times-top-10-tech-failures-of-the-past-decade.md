<p><a href="http://www.time.com" target="_blank">Time magazine</a> has <a href="http://www.time.com/time/specials/packages/completelist/0,29569,1898610,00.html" target="_blank">published a list</a> of top 10 tech failures of the past decade. The list was completed by <a href="http://www.google.co.in/">24/7 Wall St</a>.</p>
<p>Here’s the list:</p>

1. <a href="http://www.microsoft.com/windows/windows-vista/default.aspx">Microsoft Vista</a> (the annoying and under performing operating system)<br>
2. <a href="http://www.gateway.com/">Gateway</a> (a computer manufacturer which couldn’t sell much)<br>
3. <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HD_DVD">HD DVD</a> (The <a href="/tag/hd/" target="_blank">high definition</a> format disc that lost to <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_ray" target="_blank">Blu-ray</a>)<br>
4. <a href="http://www.vonage.com/index.php?ic=1">Vonage</a> (a <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voice_over_IP" target="_blank">VoIP</a> company)<br>
5. <a href="http://www.youtube.com/">YouTube</a> (Dunno why its in the list)<br>
6. <a href="http://www.sirius.com/">Sirius XM</a> (the <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satellite_radio" target="_blank">satellite radio</a>)<br>
7. <a href="http://www.zune.net/en-US/">Microsoft Zune</a> (a well deserved entry)<br>
8. <a href="http://www.palm.com/us/">Palm</a> (the Smartphone and PDA company)<br>
9. <a href="http://www.iridium.com/">Iridium</a> (a <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satellite_phone" target="_blank">satellite phone</a> company)<br>
10. <a href="http://www.segway.com/">Segway</a> (2 wheel electric vehicle company)
