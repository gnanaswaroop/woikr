<p><a href="http://www.tomtom.com">TomTom</a> - the Amsterdam based navigator company has launched <a href="http://www.tomtom.com/en_in/">GPS navigators in India</a>. The devices will compete directly with rival company MapMyIndia's navigation products and will offer touch &amp; voice controls, emergency assist and bluetooth compatibility in addition to standard GPS features like turn by turn navigation &amp; lane assistance.</p>
<p>The three models Via 100, Via 125 and Via 120 will be available in 4.3 inch and 5 inch displays with a starting price tag of ? 15,000.</p>
