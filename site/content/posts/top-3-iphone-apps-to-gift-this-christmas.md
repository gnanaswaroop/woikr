<strong><a href="http://itunes.apple.com/us/app/cut-the-rope/id380293530?mt=8&amp;uo=4" target="_blank">Cut the Rope</a><br>
</strong>An absolute fun game with a great mix of physics and puzzles. In this excellent game, you would need to feed "Om Nom" the cutie frog with Candy. There're interesting puzzles that you would need to solve on the way to collect stars and avoid the Eecky spiders.
