<p>The popular torrents search engine <a href="http://www.torrentz.com" target="_blank">www.torrentz.com</a> domain has been taken over by some warez guys. If you try to open <a href="http://www.torrentz.com" target="_blank">torrentz.com</a> now, you will be taken to the following hacked page (from warez2share.com):</p>

<a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/torrentz_screenshot.png"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1721" title="torrentz_screenshot" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/torrentz_screenshot.png" alt="" width="500" height="243" /></a>
<p>Torrentz.com guys knew that someone was after their domain. They have been displaying the following message on the site since the last few days:</p>

<blockquote>Someone was trying to takeover our domain name with faked documents and a creative story. If you have trouble reaching our site please use <a href="http://www.torrentz.eu/" target="_top">www.torrentz.eu</a> as a backup.</blockquote>
<p>If you are a regular user of torrentz.com, it is suggested that you use <a href="http://www.torrentz.eu" target="_blank">torrentz.eu</a> for sometime until they get their domain back.</p>
