<p>Toyota showcased its new and arguably amazing in car entertainment system called <a href="http://www.toyota.com/entune/">Entune</a>. The system pairs with your mobile phone over bluetooth and provides a plethora of amazing options to you in your car leveraging your mobile phone's data connection. You need to have a mobile app on your phone for the system to work though.</p>
