<p>Whenever I travel by train I often rely on my phone alarm to wake me up at the scheduled arrival time. If you know one thing about the <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Railways" target="_blank">Indian Railways</a>, it's never on time. There are a probably gazillion reasons for a train to be late despite having no real &quot;traffic jams&quot;, but hey – it still happens. Ever woke up early even though the train was a couple of hours late? And all we do is curse the railways and wait.</p>  <p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/07/image.png"><img title="image" alt="image" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/07/image_thumb.png" width="188" height="111" /></a></p>  <div></div>  <p>So this new service called PYKA solves your train travel woes by calling you (repeatedly)<strike>alerting you through an SMS (a call would be better I’d say)</strike> a few minutes before the train reaches the destination. This is way better than a preset alarm. </p>  <p>To use this you need to send an SMS to register your mobile number, train number and the destination station code in this format</p>  <blockquote>   <p><strong>PYKA</strong> &lt;<strong><u>TRAIN-NUMBER</u></strong>&gt; &lt;<strong><u>STATION-CODE</u></strong>&gt; and send it to 92200 92200 </p> </blockquote>  <p>&#160;<a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/07/image1.png"><img title="image" alt="image" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/07/image_thumb1.png" width="351" height="199" /></a> </p>  <p>Here's an introduction video which surely cracked us up. </p>  <p>&#160;</p>  <p>I think it’s high time Indian Railways had their own such service.   <br />If you try out the service, let us know in the comments.</p>  <p></p>  <p></p>  <p></p>  <p>[Via <a href="http://www.ideophone.in/products/pyka/" target="_blank">IdeoPhone</a>]</p>
