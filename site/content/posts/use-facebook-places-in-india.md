<p>If you hate Facebook for not launching "<a href="http://facebook.com/places" target="_blank">Places</a>" in India and other countries (except US, UK, Japan),  then join the club.</p>
<p>Although this article primarily explains its usage on iPhone(s), the same approach should work when Facebook releases Places for other mobile platforms such as Android, Blackberry - Your VPN Configuration would change.</p>
<p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/10/FacebookPlaces.png"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-4638" title="FacebookPlaces" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/10/FacebookPlaces.png" alt="" width="513" height="109" /></a></p>
<p>So, You know where I am going?<br>
Yes, if we find a way to get  a US IP address – we’ve got our way to access Facebook Places.<br>
Although it won’t  be as fun as there will be very few people who’ll be using it.</p>
<p></p>
<p><strong>Step 1</strong>: Configure <a href="http://hotspotshield.com/clientless/iphone/get_started.php" target="_blank">HotSpot Shield for iPhone</a><br>
Follow these steps</p>

<ol>
	<li>
<div>Go to Settings –&gt; Network –&gt; VPN –&gt;  Add New VPN  Configuration</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Fill out the settings as written below.<br>
VPN  Connection type: IPSec<br>
Description: HotspotShield<br>
Server:  68.68.107.101<br>
Account: z0um4v<br>
Password: z0um4v<br>
Use Certificate: OFF<br>
Group Name: hss<br>
Secret: hss</div></li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Step 2</strong>: Turn the VPN 'On' from Settings<br>
If  you get authentication errors, keep retrying – It’s a free service and probably  suffers a lot of load.</p>
<p>Wait for the iPhone VPN to connect (you would get a VPN icon  beside the Wifi on the top taskbar) –<br>
Check the screenshots uploaded in 'Step 3'  below.</p>
<p><strong>Step 3</strong>: Get started with using Facebook Places<br>
If the VPN isn’t connected, you will receive the usual error that Facebook  places isn’t available in<br>
your location or that some other error has occurred.</p>
