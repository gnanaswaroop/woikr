<p>People living in places where its freezing outside have a genuine problem when it comes to capacitive touch screens - They cannot use them while wearing gloves. They have to take their gloves off to use their smart phones, unless they use one of <a href="http://www.topiphoneresource.info/best-iphone-gloves/">these</a>. But not everyone would want to switch to a new pair of gloves. For such people, there is <a href="http://nosedial.com">NoseDial</a> - an iPhone app that lets you dial a number using your nose!</p><p>The app (<a href="http://itunes.apple.com/us/app/nosedial/id408719052?mt=8#">available for $0.99 here</a>) lets you navigate through the contacts on your iPhone by tilting it. The app shows pictures of your contacts and you can call them by touching the screen with your nose.</p>
