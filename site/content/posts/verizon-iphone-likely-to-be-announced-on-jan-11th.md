<p>Well, this is a good news for Verizon Apple fans who've waited years for a CDMA iPhone.  It's here and it's going to be announced on the 11th of January 2011.</p>
<p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/Verizon-iPhone.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-6046" title="Verizon iPhone" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/Verizon-iPhone.jpg" alt="" width="500" height="317" /></a></p>
<p>As Gizmodo reports the Verizon  invite has been sent to only <a href="http://techcrunch.com/2011/01/07/what-is-this-verizon-event-and-why-was-i-invited-could-it-be-dare-i-say-iphone/" target="_blank">Apple</a> <a href="http://digitaldaily.allthingsd.com/20110107/the-verizon-iphone-cometh-verizon-announces-jan-11-event/">fans</a> (who've not pissed off Apple earlier) and Apple CEO Steve Jobs is likely to appear at the event. We can't wait to see how Apple <a href="http://www.engadget.com/2011/01/06/tweaked-iphone-4-parts-video-pulled-from-youtube-due-to-a-copyr/" target="_blank">modified the iPhone design</a> to get rid of the "Antenna-Gate" issues. This is the invite that was sent to MG Siegler (<a href="http://twitter.com/parislemon" target="_blank">@parislemon</a>) of TechCrunch and AllThingsDigital team.</p>

That's a great reason for people to move away from AT&amp;T.</p>
