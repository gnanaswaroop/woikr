<p><img src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/05/vodafone_logo.gif" /> </p>  <p>In order to tap in rural customers with little or no power supply, Vodafone has launched a solar power mobile handset VF 247 in India at a price of INR 1500.</p>   <p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/07/image27.png"><img title="image" height="400" alt="image" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/07/image_thumb23.png" width="381" /></a> </p>  <p>The phone is made keeping the rural customers in mind and boasts of features like Torch, FM radio and a prepaid balance indicator. Sun Boost, an inbuilt hardware and software ensures that the phone charges under normal daylight also.</p>  <p>[via <a href="http://www.pluggd.in/vodafone-solar-mobile-vf247-297/">PI</a>]</p>
