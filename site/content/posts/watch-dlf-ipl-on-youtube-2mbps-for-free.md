<p><a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Premier_League" target="_blank">DLF IPL</a> will be available live on YouTube from 12-March-2010 to 25-April 2010. This means you can watch all IPL matches from your desk in office. No more running to the cafeteria to catch the score.</p>
<p><img src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/12/logo_airtel.gif" alt="" /></p>
<p>To add the cherry on the cake, Airtel broadband customers will be able to watch IPL matches on <a href="http://www.youtube.com/ipl" target="_blank">youtube.com/ipl</a> @ 2 Mbps. This speed boost is only for the <a href="http://www.youtube.com/ipl" target="_blank">ipl channel</a> on YouTube and will not be available to any other YouTube channel or any other website.</p>
<p></p>
<p>Here are the details:</p>

<ul>
	<li>
<div>The 2 Mbps speed is an automatic upgrade , only applicable for <a href="http://www.youtube.com/ipl">www.youtube.com/ipl</a></div></li>
	<li>
<div>For all others site customers will get their current plan speeds.</div></li>
	<li>
<div>While there is no charge for 2Mbps upgrade , Streaming on YouTube will be considered as part of data transfer limits of your current broadband tariff plan.</div></li>
	<li>
<div>The 2 Mbps bandwidth is not guaranteed all the time and is dependent of traffic.</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Upgraded speed of 2Mbps applicable only for content streaming on youtube.com/ipl page and not for other YouTube content.</div></li>
</ul>
<p>So what are you waiting for? Head over to <a title="http://www.youtube.com/ipl" href="http://www.youtube.com/ipl">http://www.youtube.com/ipl</a> to catch all the action!</p>
