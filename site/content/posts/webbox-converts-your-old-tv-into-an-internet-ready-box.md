In developing countries like South Africa, *ahem* India – mobile phones have seen an explosive growth. Vodafone’s Pan-African subsidiary – Vodacom has released a new keyboard like gadget called <a href="http://www.vodacom.co.za/vodacom/services/Other+Services/Vodafone+WebBox?ciFormat=default&amp;ht=t&amp;selected=6_a6&amp;vgnextrefresh=1" target="_blank">WebBox</a> which runs Android 2.1 OS that can be connected to your TV to surf the internet at 2.5G speed (EDGE) through your mobile networks.

It runs Android, but don’t expect a whole lot of software to come pre-installed with it. It's primarily for Internet browsing/email sending in other words, a very creative <a href="/search/netbook" target="_blank">netbook</a> without an actual monitor, instead uses your TV.

<div></div>
I think the potential of such a device is huge. It's primary features are
<ul>
	<li>
<div>Opera Mini – preinstalled, which gives a great browsing experience by compressing web pages by almost 90%. You can of course use <a href="http://www.opera.com/mobile/download/" target="_blank">Opera Mini</a> on your mobile phone.</div></li>
	<li>
<div>QWERTY keyboard (which is the gadget) for easier typing – compact size (almost the size of your laptop computer – just a hell lot lighter).</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Easy connection to the TV through a RCA cable (same as your old VCD/DVD player or your son's video game console). It's a simple Plug-And-Play.</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Many pre-installed applications which can help you send SMS and emails easier.<br>
<a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/02/webboxmenu.jpg"><img title="webbox-menu" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/02/webboxmenu_thumb.jpg" alt="webbox-menu" width="509" height="262" /></a></div></li>
	<li>
<div>Games, Notes applications pre-installed (you can quickly take down notes) while surfing. But just don't buy it to play games. Stick to your video game consoles for that.</div></li>
	<li>
<div>SIM Card provision (just like your mobile phone) as this works only on mobile networks Wish they had a WiFi support too. Nevertheless, <a href="/android/motorola-atrix-4g-a-laptop-and-phone-hybrid-seriously" target="_blank">Motorola ATRIX</a> – you just got a competitor which is many times cheaper.</div></li>
	<li>
<div>2GB SD Card to store your media – You can also stream photos and videos from your online albums.</div></li>
	<li>
<div>FM Radio too – in case you’re still into that thing. In India that’s the favorite commute entertainment and is doing pretty good.</div></li>
	<li>
<div>No info on the processor yet, but I am guessing it'll be the usual 600Mhz processor that is a standard on most budget phones of 2010.</div></li>
	<li>
<div>The screen resolution is going to suck, but if you’re using an old TV (with lower resolutions, not talking about <a href="/search/hdtv" target="_blank">Full HD TVs</a> here).</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Affordable hardware pricing (102$) in South Africa (soon be released in other markets)</div></li>
</ul>
If this gets released in India, I am confident it’ll get very popular as Indian household(s) have great TV and mobile penetration. This coupled with <a href="/telecom/airtels-low-rental-gprs-plan" target="_blank">cheap EDGE plan</a> would create a powerful internet ready TV in your household.
