<p>The week saw 2 major app launches in the iPhone App Store:</p>

<ol>
	<li><a href="http://www.skype.com" target="_blank">Skype </a> [<a href="http://itunes.apple.com/WebObjects/MZStore.woa/wa/viewSoftware?id=304878510&amp;mt=8" target="_blank">Appstore Link</a>] - The much awaited app has been finally launched for the iPhone. And it is doing <a href="http://share.skype.com/sites/en/2009/04/skype_for_iphone_zooms_past_on.html" target="_blank">pretty well</a>! The free app lets you make <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VoIP" target="_blank">VoIP</a> calls from your iPhone over the WiFi network. Yeah, no 3G support. But the app is <a href="http://www.skype.com/intl/en/download/skype/iphone/" target="_blank">pretty neat</a>. With support for iPod touch devices (with external microphone) as well, the app is definitly set to make a difference in the arena. Check out the slick interface:
