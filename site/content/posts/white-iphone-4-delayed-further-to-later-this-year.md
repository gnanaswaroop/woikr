<p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/07/image15.png"><img title="image" height="156" alt="image" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/07/image_thumb11.png" width="244" /></a> </p>  <p>Bad news for people who are waiting for the white iPhone 4. Apparently, Apple is facing some manufacturing challenges in producing the white model. In a <a href="http://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/statement-by-apple-on-white-iphone-4-99096524.html">press statement</a> released today, Apple has told that the white iPhone 4 will not be available until later this year.</p>  <p>Here is the full statement:</p>  <blockquote>   <p>White models of Apple’s new iPhone® 4 have continued to be more challenging to manufacture than we originally expected, and as a result they will not be available until later this year. The availability of the more popular iPhone 4 black models is not affected.</p> </blockquote>  <p>[via <a href="http://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/statement-by-apple-on-white-iphone-4-99096524.html">PR News</a>]</p>
