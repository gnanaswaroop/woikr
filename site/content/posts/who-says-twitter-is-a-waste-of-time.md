<p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/03/image14.png"><img title="image" alt="image" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/03/image_thumb13.png" width="240" height="216" /></a> </p>  <p>Whosoever says (or believes) that tweeting is like any other useless social networking waste of time needs to look at the following tweets.</p>  <p>Apparently, a couple in Hyderabad is trying to get a passport for their 4 months old baby but the officers at the Passport office in the city are not accepting their application without a bribe.</p>  <p>One of their friend <a href="http://twitter.com/justkalpana/status/11227342941" target="_blank">tweeted</a> their grief addressing it to <a href="http://www.tharoor.in" target="_blank">Mr. Shashi Tharoor</a> (Minister of External Affairs, and <a href="http://indiatoday.intoday.in/site/Story/63473/Nation/I+will+not+stop+tweeting:+Shashi+Tharoor.html" target="_blank">infamous Indian tweeter</a>) and two NDTV senior editors. Passport division, incidentally, comes under the wings of the Ministry of External Affairs. The minister <a href="http://twitter.com/ShashiTharoor/status/11229829249" target="_blank">has responded</a> saying that he will look into the issue.</p>   <p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/03/image15.png"><img title="image" alt="image" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/03/image_thumb14.png" width="540" height="872" /></a></p>  <p></p>  <p>[via <a href="http://www.labnol.org/india/indian-minister-helps-on-twitter/13306/" target="_blank">Digital Inspiration</a>]</p>
