<h3>Steps to redeem the promo codes:</h3>
<ol>
	<li>Open iTunes and login to your US iTunes Store account</li>
	<li>On the store home page, click on "Redeem Code" in the top right section</li>
	<li>You might be asked to accept the updated terms and conditions. You will have to accept them in order to redeem the code.</li>
	<li>Enter the code in the box and click submit.</li>
	<li>Your app will start downloading in iTunes. Once its downloaded, sync it to your iPhone.</li>
	<li>In case it is not synced to your iPhone, make sure it is selected in the Applications tab in iTunes for your iPhone.</li>
	<li>Enjoy! Do not forget to write a review of the apps in the AppStore :).</li>
</ol>
