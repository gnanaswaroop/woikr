<p>A chain mail has been doing some rounds in every one's mailbox here in India. There are a lot of allegations on the new iPhone 3G available now through Vodafone and Bharti Airtel. Some true some false; woikr clarifies these allegations for you.</p>
<p><span><span><strong>1. </strong><strong>No A2DP on Bluetooth, can't share any (mp3, images) files via bluetooth</strong></span></span></p>
<p>True. However, Bluetooth hardware is there which is used for Bluetooth headset but no one has yet implemented the above stuff as it has full fledged email.</p>
<p><strong>2.</strong><strong> <span><span>Can't use as a wireless modem, (you pay ridiculous data charges to activate internet on your </span></span><span><span>phone</span></span><span><span><span>,</span> but cannot connect it to your PC and browse when you are at home) </span></span></strong></p>
<p>False. There's an app called "NetShare" to do this. This, like on other phones, will discharges the battery very fast.  Anyhow, Mobile Safari works great by itself. We recommend you to limit EDGE/3G  browsing to Mobile Safari.</p>
<p><strong>3. </strong><strong> <span><span>No copy and paste (cannot copy a part of a.... say.... email &amp; send it.... have to type it out yourself)</span></span></strong></p>
<p>True. But there's nothing limiting this phone from allowing this - it's just its not top priority for Apple right now. And we think that's stupid on Apple's part. Anyhow, there's a new framework called as <a href="http://www.openclip.org/" target="_blank">OpenClip</a> for this.</p>
<p><strong>4. </strong><strong><span><span>No 3.2 or 5 megapi<span>xel </span></span></span><span><span><span>camera (the 2M</span>P camera it carries is worst camera I've ever seen..... photos look like they have come out of a VGA camera) </span></span></strong></p>

<div>The 2 MP camera is not that bad. It isn't just perfect as it lacks flash and zoom. The images aren't at all bad if taken in proper lightning. Check out some images we took in a cricket match at night.</div>

<p>Meanwhile, did you know, its the <a href="http://www.flickr.com/cameras/" target="_blank">most popular cameraphone on flickr</a>!</p>
<p><strong>5</strong><strong>. <span><span>No video recording with camera</span></span></strong></p>
<p>True. There is an app available in Cydia called Video Recorder, it's paid, but records limited length videos in the trial version too. Also, there are apps like Qik, Flixwagon -  which provide live video streaming. These apps are not yet available on App Store because of Apple's stun silence on video applications for now.</p>
<p><strong>6. <span><span>No front camera</span></span></strong></p>
<p>This is something we agree, but how many of us video conference on our mobile phones ?</p>
<p><strong>7. </strong><span><span><strong>No JAVA support</strong></span></span></p>
<p>False. There are <a href="http://blogs.sun.com/java4iphone/entry/cydia_iphone_java_and_jocstrap" target="_blank">apps in Cydia</a> - Java/iPhone, JamVM and Jikes that work as Java interpreters. Also, we are hoping that Sun and Apple work together to get us a proper Java version. After all, iPhone's hardware is more than suitable to run Java.</p>
<p><strong>8. <span><span>No physical keyboard (BELIEVE me! this alone is a deal breaker... touch keyboard as good as it looks... once you use it then only you come to know about the importance of having a hard keypad)</span></span></strong></p>
<p>Believe us! This is the best way to do it. What if a country wants its own version of keyboard. Say a keyboard in Hindi? Will they remove the hard keyboard? With a soft keyboard, all you need to do is display different characters on the keys. Check this example for Japanese keyboard.</p>


<a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/08/japanese_keyboard.png"><img title="Japanese Keyboard" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/08/japanese_keyboard.png" alt="Japanese Keyboard" width="320" height="480" /></a>
<p>Did you know Apple supports handwriting recognition for Chinese? Check it out, it's pretty nice!</p>


<a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/08/chinese_keyboard.png"><img title="Chinese Handwriting recognition" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2008/08/chinese_keyboard.png" alt="Chinese Handwriting recognition" width="320" height="480" /></a>
<p><strong>9. <span><span>No 3G network yet in India .. so why pay for expensive 3G phone if you cant use its 3G services .. so, for India , iPhone 2.0 is no better than its first version</span></span></strong></p>

<p>True. However, do not expect blazingly fast speed with 3G in India. We all know that EDGE is here for namesake only. Woikr recommends Wifi it is the best way to browse net on the phone. The same goes for other Wifi phones.<br>
Plus, iPhone 3G might drive the operators and TRAI to speed up the process for 3G launch in India as this is the first phone with the word "3G" in its name. Till now, many people who bought Nokia N95s  and other 3G phones did not know that they are 3G capable.
<p><strong>10. <span><span>No removable battery. (You cannot change battery. Have to send it to apple outlet.. Come on now!!!!...This is not an iPod. </span></span><span><span>Its a bloody phone. You cannot live without phone for 2-3 days)</span></span></strong></p>

<p>True. The only plus point is that like iPod's battery replacement, there are chances that you get a a new phone when you submit it for battery replacement ;-). Now who doesn't want a new phone?<br>
Additionally , the iPhone 3G has visible screws, this should not be a problem to replace. Apple service is good at such things.<br>
Although, we still agree Apple has no reason to not allow us from changing batteries.
<p><strong>11. <span><span>Cannot send MMS</span></span></strong></p>
<p>False. There are MMS applications available on Cydia and Installer.</p>
<p><strong>12. <span><span>Can't use as external storage device (16 GB of utter waste.. next time you go to your friend's house &amp; find some interesting songs, movies<span> </span></span></span><span><span>etc... sorry you cannot connect your '16 GB' phone and share it!!!! </span></span><span><span><span>Apple</span></span></span><span><span> apparently has a solution.. " BUY MUSIC &amp; MOVIES FROM </span></span><span><span><span>iTUNES</span></span></span><span><span><span> STORE </span>"..... Give me a break will you APPLE!!!)</span></span></strong></p>

<p>True and False. There's an app called as diskAid to do this. However, the songs and videos you copy on your iPhone using this tool will not be available in the iPod app on the phone. You will will need to transfer hem through iTunes.<br>
There have been enough debates about drag and drop media functionality on Apple products. They just do not listen. Probably they don't want us to do it that way.
<p><strong>13. <span><span>No FM radio</span></span></strong></p>
<p>True. However, there are Internet Radio apps. <a href="http://www.google.co.in/url?sa=t&amp;ct=res&amp;cd=1&amp;url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.flytunes.fm%2F&amp;ei=XIeuSOKIMJmI6gO6lvjzDw&amp;usg=AFQjCNH5JUJKYlgZ7eAnWauZToTMBO4sEw&amp;sig2=q-PF4htsZYQI7-3YPD-6IQ" target="_blank">Flytunes</a>. They have Indian channels as well. Works pretty well over EDGE. Will surely work fine over 3G. Awesome if you have wifi.</p>
<p><strong>14. <span><span>Can't use your mp3 files as ringtones (This is heights man!!!) </span></span></strong></p>
<p>False. You can use the apps like "Ringtones" to do this on the iPhone or u can use iTunes to create ringtones. There are numerous desktop apps to convert mp3s to ringtones. And the process is pretty simple too!</p>
<p><strong>15. <span><span>Cannot insert any other operator's SIM card</span></span></strong></p>
<p>True. And at INR 31000, we do expect this option.</p>

<strong><span><span>16. Completely tied to iTunes... Connect to your friend's iTunes &amp; you risk erasing everything on your phone.</span></span></strong>
<p>False. You just need to <a href="http://www.iphoneatlas.com/2008/01/15/itunes-76-lets-you-manually-manage-iphone-musicvideos/" target="_blank">configure it properly</a>. Also, you can now authorize your phone at 5 locations using the App Store id - that way u wont erase every thing.</p>

Most of our phones already have these features. &amp; we cannot do with out them.... i mean can you imagine paying so much for a phone with which you cannot transfer files via bluetooth. what age is this ...STONE AGE??? </span></span></strong></p>
<p>False. Ever heard of email? Go see how clumsy email is on other so called smart phones.</p>

And want to add third party software??.. YOU HAVE TO BUY THEM FROM iTUNES STORE ONLY!!! . (Shell out more money). Apple is a bloody control freak. Dont surrender your freedom to it.</span></span></strong>
<p>False. App store is one of the best things that happened to the mobile market. Plus, there are tons of free (useful and entertaining apps and games). Stop whining, Grow up.</p>

When they want to buy a phone, they have to go to network operators and buy only those phones that are available on that network &amp; stick to that company till their contract expires. You want to change carriers? Not before paying damages, &amp; buying another phone on the other network again. </span></span></strong></p>

<p>False. There's no contract, there would be some nominal charges to be paid to get you iPhone unlocked officially. These details are not released yet.<br>
And please, stone age! You are talking about cellular plans here, not technology :-)

Apple is trying to IMPOSE THEIR MODEL ON US riding on the hype iPhone has created in the US (yes it was a flo<span>p in Europe</span></span></span><span><span><span>. That's why Apple n</span>ever talks about its sales in Europe ).</span></span></strong>
