<p>This is possibly the craziest iPhone related news I've ever read. A woman in HongKong attempted suicide and the primary reason appears to be the fact that she lost her iPhone 4.</p>
<p><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-6538" title="iPhone_suicide" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/iPhone_suicide.jpg" alt="iPhone Suicide Attempt" width="500" height="254" /></p>
<p>Mrs. Wong is a housewife and was gifted an iPhone 4 by her husband who is a bus driver. She lost the iPhone just after using it for 2 days. She asked the husband to buy her a new one but he couldn't afford it and hence refused. Mrs. Wong, apparently, was so much in love with the iPhone 4 that she decided to end her life by jumping from 14th floor. Thankfully, Mr. Wong prevented her from committing this utterly stupid crime. Maybe he should buy her a <a href="/iphone/sophone-a-fake-iphone-that-blew-our-mind-knockoffs" target="_blank">SoPhone</a> for now.</p>
