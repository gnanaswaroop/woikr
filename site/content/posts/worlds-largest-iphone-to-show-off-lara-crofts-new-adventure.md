<p>EA has created world's largest iPhone display to advertise the <a href="http://itunes.apple.com/app/lara-croft-guardian-light/id394106986?mt=8" target="_blank">$6.99 iOS app</a> for Lara Croft's new adventure - <a href="http://www.laracroftandtheguardianoflight.com/" target="_blank">Lara Croft and The Guardian of Light</a>. The iPhone display was made by stacking together 56 iPads and was unveiled at London’s St. Pancras International Train Station. I am not sure if it does more to advertise the game or iPhone/iPad. Anyway, it's Lara Croft on a mega sized, uber-cool iPhone-like display. Who's complaining?</p>
