<p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/image18.png"><img title="image" height="149" alt="image" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/image_thumb15.png" width="242" /></a> </p>  <p><a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finland" target="_blank">Finland</a> has become the first country in the world to make broadband a legal right for each of its citizen.</p>  <p>According to the <a href="http://www.lvm.fi/web/en/news/view/920307" target="_blank">Ministry of Transport and Communications</a>, starting July 2010, every person in Finland will have the right to a one megabit (1 Mbps) broadband connection. And by the end of 2015 it will be increased to a whooping 100 Mbps!</p>  <p>Lets just say that this is the one of the most wonderful world news that we have heard lately.</p>  <p>[via <a href="http://www.techcrunch.com/2009/10/14/applause-for-finland-first-country-to-make-broadband-access-a-legal-right/" target="_blank">TechCrunch</a>]</p>
