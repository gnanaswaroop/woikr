<p>Apple has announced the dates for the <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Worldwide_Developers_Conference" target="_blank">World Wide Developer Conference</a> (WWDC) 2009. Its gonna happen on June 8-12 in San Francisco. Tickets are selling now for $1,295, which includes a $300 early bird discount when purchased before April 24.</p>
<p><a href="http://developer.apple.com/wwdc/experience/" target="_blank">Click here</a> to learn more about the event.</p>

<a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/03/iphone_unlocked.jpg"><img class="alignnone size-medium wp-image-2366" title="iphone_unlocked" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2009/03/iphone_unlocked-244x300.jpg" alt="iphone_unlocked" width="244" height="300" /></a>
<p>What is interesting to note is that Apple stores have now started selling contract free iPhone 3Gs. And there is no restriction on the number of iPhones one can buy. This might be an indication that Apple is planning to release the next version of iPhone in the WWDC and wants to get rid of the existing iPhone 3G stock in the US.</p>
<p>The <a href="/lead-story/iphone-firmware-3-whats-new/" target="_blank">next version of the iPhone OS</a> was announced a couple of days back and is bringing a lot of goodies for customers and developers. The next iteration of the iPhone might have a kick ass processor which will do justice to this new firmware.</p>
