<p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/07/image6.png"><img title="image" alt="image" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/07/image_thumb2.png" width="200" height="150" /></a> </p>  <p>The price of Xbox 360 Arcade console in India has been dropped form INR 16,990 to INR 14,676. This is good news for all people who were planning to buy the console.</p>  <div></div>  <p>The Xbox 360 Arcade console comes with Xbox 360 wireless controller, AV cable, arcade games disc and 256 MB memory unit (to save your game progress). This console is compatible with the upcoming <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinect" target="_blank">Kinect</a> (previously called <a href="/gaming/project-natal-from-microsoft" target="_blank">Project Natal</a>) for Xbox 360.</p>  <p>With this price drop, you can expect the console to be available at even cheaper price on ebay and in grey markets like Palika Bazaar.</p>  <p>In addition to playing games, you can use <a href="http://www.afterdawn.com/guides/archive/stream_video_xbox_360_tversity.cfm" target="_blank">Xbox 360 to stream content</a> from your PC to your HDTV and Home Theater system.</p>  <p>[via <a href="http://www.techtree.com/India/News/Microsoft_Xbox_360_Arcade_Prices_Slashed/551-112189-585.html" target="_blank">Techtree</a>]</p>
