Microsoft UK has announced that during Xtival'09 from May 1-4 all Xbox users will have access to free Xbox Live Gold. So you can play with your friends on Live for free!

Here's what the <a href="http://www.xbox.com/en-gb/xtival09" target="_blank">official website</a> says:
<blockquote>We don’t think anyone should miss out on the treats we’ve got lined-up this year, which is why we’re giving EVERYONE Xbox LIVE Gold membership FREE for the entire weekend.</blockquote>
Let's hope this is applicable in India too. And while we're hoping, let's also hope that Microsoft learns something from Sony and makes Xbox Live free for all ;-)
