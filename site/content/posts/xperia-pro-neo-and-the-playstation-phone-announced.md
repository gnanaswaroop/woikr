If you're wondering why Sony's advertising those year old XPeria 10 in India, Here's why.<br>
As usual whenever a new product is announced in the US, older products are dumped to <span>India</span>(3rd world countries).  This time it's Sony which announced the latest in the XPeria series – <a href="/gaming/sony-xperia-play-the-playstation-phone-leaked-and-previewed-in-china">XPeria Play</a>, XPeria Pro and XPeria Neo at the <a href="http://www.mobileworldcongress.com/event/event_overview.htm">Mobile World Congress 2011</a>.

I hate the fact that that, despite having an open operating system like Android  the hardware companies have gone back to slimy way of categorizing each of their phones as "gaming only", business ready and entertainment/music phones. Didn't they learn enough already that one phone – which "does-it-all" can <a href="/iphone/iphone-now-has-17-25-market-share-rimnokia-wake-up-now">take over the a major chunk of the market</a>? (in case you've not figured it out, I am talking about the <a href="/iPhone">Apple iPhone</a> here).

<a href="http://www.sonyericsson.com/mwcnews/xperia_play/"><strong>XPeria Play</strong></a> is also well known to every one as the Play Station phone and the first Android phone to come with a dedicated gaming keypad. It features
<ul>
	<li>4" inch screen FWVGA screen resolution</li>
	<li>1Ghz Qualcomm's Snapdragon processor</li>
	<li>Dedicated Adreno GPU for those amazing games at 60fps</li>
	<li>Gaming buttons - you'll not miss your gaming controller now.</li>
	<li>Will run Android 3.0.</li>
	<li>5.1 Megapixel camera.</li>
	<li>Can run/download PlayStation games through the PlayStation suite (being developed right now)</li>
	<li>Don't keep your hopes high, the phone will only be available by March 2011.</li>
</ul>
<a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/02/SonyXPeriaPlayingSoccer.jpg"><img title="Sony-XPeria-Playing-Soccer" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/02/SonyXPeriaPlayingSoccer_thumb.jpg" alt="Sony-XPeria-Playing-Soccer" width="504" height="284" /></a>

<strong>XPeria Pro</strong> (advertised as a business phone)
<ul>
	<li>
<div>Slideout QWERTY keyboard</div></li>
	<li>
<div>3.7" screen size</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Runs on Android 3.0 Gingerbread Operating system.</div></li>
	<li>
<div>The Usual 1Ghz Qualcomm Snapdragon processor</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Built in 8GB MicroSD card.</div></li>
	<li>
<div>HDMI Output</div></li>
	<li>
<div>854  x 480pixels (FWVGA) screen resolution</div></li>
	<li>
<div>EXmor 8.1 megapixel camera</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Available in colors - Blue Gradient, Red &amp; Silver.</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Will be available by the end of Q2 2011.</div></li>
</ul>
<a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/02/SonyXPeriaProSnap.jpg"><img title="Sony-XPeria-Pro-Snap" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2011/02/SonyXPeriaProSnap_thumb.jpg" alt="Sony-XPeria-Pro-Snap" width="480" height="282" /></a>

<strong>XPeria Neo</strong> (an entertainment phone, coz it doesn’t have a physical keyboard)

This phone is a stripped down version of the XPeria Pro, the features are very similar except the following.
<ul>
	<li>
<div>No Smart keyboard – Predicts what you're typing. This isn’t the first and won’t be the last to do so.</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Document Viewer only, no editor – So editing documents isn't your piece of cake.</div></li>
	<li>
<div>No physical keyboard – That's for people who like blind landscape typing, the rest of the world still loves soft keyboards.</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Weighs less than XPeria Pro – As it doesn’t have a physical keyboard.</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Available in colors – Black, Silver &amp; Red.</div></li>
	<li>
<div>Will be available by the end of Q1, 2011.</div></li>
</ul>
