<p><a href="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/08/iPadInboxFull.png"><img title="iPad-Inbox-Full" alt="iPad-Inbox-Full" src="/wp/wp-content/uploads/2010/08/iPadInboxFull_thumb.png" width="320" height="251" /></a> </p>  <p>Yahoo Mail is still the largest web mail service in the world. Gmail is gaining popularity, however, it is <a href="http://news.cnet.com/8301-30684_3-10311150-265.html" target="_blank">still behind Yahoo</a> in terms on number of users.</p>  <p>Soon after the iPad was released, Gmail launched an iPad version. Yahoo is catching up and has recently launched a HTML5 version of its popular web mail service for iPad users. This version leverages advanced HTML5 abilities like caching and panes with individual scrolling to give the user a rich Inbox view. You can compose and send email messages even if you are offline as it caches your mails and then sends them whenever an internet connection is available. </p>   <p>The new version also lets you preview photo attachments right in the Inbox view. In addition, users can now find and organize messages using Full Search, personal folders and Smart Folders with messages from your most important contacts, and view photos in optimized resolution for the iPad.</p>  <p>iPad users can access the new version at: <a href="http://mail.yahoo.com" target="_blank">mail.yahoo.com</a>.</p>  <p>[via <a href="http://www.ymailblog.com/blog/2010/08/ipad-therefore-i-am/" target="_blank">Yahoo Mail Blog</a>]</p>
