<p>We have <a href="/android/lg-unveils-worlds-first-dual-core-phone" target="_blank">talked</a> <a href="/wtf/lg-releases-a-weird-video-to-show-off-optimus-2x" target="_blank">a</a> <a href="/iphone/lg-optimus-2x-beats-iphone-4-in-browser-speed-test-android" target="_blank">lot</a> about the LG Optimus 2x. It's now time to take out your Credit Card and buy the world's first dual-core Android phone. The Optimus 2X is the first phone that runs <a href="http://www.nvidia.com/object/tegra-2.html" target="_blank">nVidia’s Tegra 2</a>. It features an 8 MP camera that can also record 1080p videos. The other specs like a 4? display, HDMI out and 8 GB memory are average for high-end phones these days. The phone runs Froyo but is scheduled to upgrade to Gingerbread in the future.</p>
<p>The phone is available for pre-order on eXpansys for £499.99. It'll be shipped on February 21. The price comes out to a little above 35k INR. I guess they deserve to charge a premium for this monster of a phone.</p>
