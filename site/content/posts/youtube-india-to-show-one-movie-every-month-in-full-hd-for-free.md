<p>YouTube India has launched a <a href="http://www.youtube.com/boxoffice">boxoffice channel</a> which will showcase one popular <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bollywood">bollywood</a> movie every month in Full HD. The movie will be free to watch but will contain ads after every 10 minutes. Anyone with a good broadband connection and a browser which supports flash will be able to watch the movie for free. Obviously, for watching the HD versions (720p and 1080p), you will need a super fast connection (4 Mbps or above). However, there are low res versions (480p, 360p &amp; 240p) also available for you to enjoy in case you do not have that kind of connection.</p>
<p>Head over to <a href="http://www.youtube.com/boxoffice">youtube.com/boxoffice</a> to start watching.</p>
<p>Currently, the channel is showing <a href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt1610452/">Band Baaja Baaraat</a>. The channel also has many other blockbusters available under "More Videos" tab.</p>
