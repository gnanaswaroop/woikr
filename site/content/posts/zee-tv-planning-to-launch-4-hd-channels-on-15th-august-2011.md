<p>After Star's foray into the High Definition space with their <a href="/home-entertainment/tatasky-adds-3-new-hd-channels-to-its-bouquet/">AsliHD channels</a>, Zee TV is going to enter the HD game by launchig 4 of their most famous channels - Zee TV, Zee Cinema, Zee Studio, Ten Sports in HD in India on 15th August 2011.</p>
<p>The recent logo rebranding was probably part of this initiative as well. Zee hasn't announced which platforms will carry these channels, but logic dictates that it will surely be available on Dish TV's HD DTH service. Other likely candidates might include Reliance &amp; Videocon. Unfortunately, TataSky will not be able to carry them as it has <a href="/home-entertainment/tatasky-is-maxed-out-on-its-capacity-to-beam-hd-channels/">maxed out on its capacity oh HD channels</a>.</p>
<p>Pretty good news for cunsumers in India. I think we can safely say that the HD revolution in India has been kicked off.</p>
